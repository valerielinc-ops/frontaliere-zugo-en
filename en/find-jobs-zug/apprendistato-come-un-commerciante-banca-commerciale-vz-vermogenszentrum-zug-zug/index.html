Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. We are very successful and growing strongly.

  • You complete a total of six stages in the different areas of the bank.
  • You work about half in Zurich and half in Zug.
  • From the third year of apprenticeship, you accompany the consultants for customer discussions. This gives you a comprehensive insight into all topics relating to banking.
  • You visit the vocational school on one or two days a week and learn everything important about the industry in intercompany courses at the Banking Advisory Centre (bbz) together with the learners of other banks. The bbz offers a very individual, personal care.
  • You will continuously put your theoretical knowledge into practice and learn to move successfully in a networked and digitalized working world.
  • You will be accompanied by experienced trainers throughout your apprenticeship.
