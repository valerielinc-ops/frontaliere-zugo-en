Full description

Are you a morning muffle? Perfect, then read on! With you, our evening team gains a leader who thinks, takes action and keeps an overview even when a lot is going on. Apply now and design a strong evening logistics with us.

You can do that.

  • You will lead our logistics team with up to 25 employees professionally and personnel and ensure efficient and economical personnel deployment.
  • As a strong management duo, you plan short- and long-term assignments, conduct employee meetings and ensure reliable leadership in day-to-day business.
  • You promote cooperation and development in the team, lead meetings and ensure an open flow of information.
  • In addition, you will coordinate the processing, sorting and delivery of Migros online orders and evening shipments as well as the handling of goods in the afternoon and evening, using your organizational talent.
  • You work in a 5-day week and basically take over your assignments in the time window between 11.00 and 21.30.

You bring that with you

  • You have experience in logistics or as a dispatcher. Advantageously, you have knowledge of postal operations as well as operational processes in delivery.
  • In addition, you have completed a basic training EFZ in the field of logistics, already have initial leadership experience and are ready to train in the field of leadership.
  • You work in an organized manner, communicate with the addressee and keep an overview even in demanding situations.
  • In addition, you have the driver's license category B; a driver's license category BE and a forklift card are advantageous.
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge of German as well as a secure handling of MS Office.

With you, flexibility becomes everyday life. We promote progressive teamwork and value a balanced working life. This position can also be taken over by two persons in "co-management". You can apply directly as a duo or we support you in the search for a suitable person.
