Full description

Their tasks shall include

  • Overall responsibility for the personnel, professional, organizational and economic management of the care sector with around 150 employees
  • Ensuring a high quality of care and efficient use of resources
  • Co-shaping the strategic orientation and further development of the institution
  • Close cooperation with the Chief Clinic in the dual management system and promotion of an interprofessional leadership culture
  • Further development and positioning of care within the organization
  • Professional responsibility for care in ambulatories
  • Designing transformation and change processes as a member of the management team and strengthening managers in their implementation

They bring it with them.

  • Well-founded subject-specific education and training as well as SRC recognition and professional licence
  • Management training and several years of management experience in a comparable function
  • Networked and entrepreneurial thinking as well as an independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Ability to bring different interests together, set clear goals and also provide orientation in change processes
  • Convincing leader with high quality standards and creative will
  • Strong capacity for reflection, communication and conflict
  • Experience in the ambulatory environment is beneficial

We offer them

  • A responsible leadership function with room for manoeuvre and a direct impact on the further development of the organization
  • A management that moves forward in an agile manner, works together in a transparent, solution-oriented and appreciative manner and is aware of shared responsibility
  • A dynamic environment with exciting challenges
  • Very good social benefits and an attractive overall package
