Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With VZ Depotbank AG, we offer our customers the opportunity to process transactions efficiently and cheaply. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our headquarters in a central location in Zug.

  • You work in the foundation accounting team of VZ Depotbank, which is responsible for a total of seven pension foundations.
  • Cooperation in the preparation of the annual accounts according to Swiss GAAP FER and the annual reports of the pension foundations.
  • Support in the processing of data from the systems of the custodian bank and foundation administration as well as in the booking in the Abacus.
  • You will gradually learn about the processes and connections in foundation accounting and support you in the preparations for the revision as well as their support.
  • You are committed to the further development of the dynamic organization and take responsibility for smaller projects.
  • Completed commercial or comparable training and a great interest in social insurance and occupational pensions
  • Good communication, thirst for knowledge, enjoyment of contact with different stakeholders and experience in cooperation with several departments
  • Good MS-Office knowledge (especially Excel)
  • Structured operation
  • Reliability and accuracy
  • You will deepen your expertise in the accounting of the various pension foundations (investment foundations, free movement foundations, a 3a foundation and two collective foundations).
  • With its open culture, the rapidly growing VZ offers you the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and further develop your specialist area.
  • You work in a motivated team that supports each other and engages and successfully implements new projects.
  • Your training will be individually supported, including support for external training within the framework of the job profile.
  • Surveys regularly confirm an above-average positive working environment in which employees can develop – become part of it.
