Full description

The Zug Canton Hospital serves the health of the population of the Canton of Zug and the surrounding regions. Around 1,200 employees, 180 of them in education and training, work in a wide range of fields. Every year we treat and care for more than 11,500 inpatient and over 70,000 outpatients. In the interdisciplinary emergency center, more than 27,500 people are treated annually. In our clinic for orthopaedics and traumatology, about 2,500 procedures are performed per year and about 2,000 inpatients and 9,000 outpatients are treated. The clinic is divided into subspecialized teams: shoulder and elbow, hand and wrist, hip, knee, foot and ankle, and traumatology. Spinal surgery is offered in cooperation with a center hospital. The Clinic of Orthopaedics and Traumatology has a qualification for the specialist title Orthopaedic Surgery and Traumatology of the musculoskeletal system in category B1.
