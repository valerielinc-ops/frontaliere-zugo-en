Full description

Do you have in-depth audit experience and would you like to consciously move to an environment in which you can take on responsibility more quickly, work closer to customers and have real ownership? We are looking for experienced individuals who do not want to start from scratch, but would like to pick up where they were already strong: take on responsibility, manage a portfolio and position themselves as a mandate manager. We are a specialized assurance team with a focus on limited audits and SME audits. Our mandates are customer-oriented and diverse. Short decision-making processes, a high level of personal responsibility and a pragmatic way of working characterize our collaboration.

  • Taking over an existing mandate portfolio or specifically building your own portfolio - depending on the initial situation with a clear development path
  • Acting as a sparring partner on an equal footing for customers - both professionally and entrepreneurially
  • Leading and carrying out audits in the area of limited revisions / SME audits with a high level of personal responsibility and quality
  • Further developing employees and actively shaping a high-performance, supportive team culture
  • Contributing your own experience and benefiting from it PwC platform to specifically increase impact and reach Why this role is exciting Because here you don't just "come back" or "take the next step", but:
  • Can consistently translate your experience into real ownership
  • Take on responsibility more quickly than in classic audit setups
  • Grow in a customer- and entrepreneurial environment
  • Find a model that credibly combines performance and flexibility
  • Recognized Swiss auditor qualification or comparable training (e.g. ACCA, ACA, CPA) or on a corresponding training path
  • Several years of experience in auditing, ideally in the area of limited audits / SMEs
  • Clear ambition to take responsibility and manage mandates
  • Entrepreneurial mindset, high level of personal responsibility and strong quality standards
  • Very good knowledge of German and English Who we specifically address This role is deliberately not a standard profile. The target group is experienced individuals with clear aspirations:
  • Re-joiners and returnees who already know PwC and want to come back with more responsibility, clarity and ownership
  • Experienced audit professionals who consciously work closer to SME mandates and want to take on more entrepreneurial responsibility
  • Ambitious candidates on the next career step for whom managing a mandate is the logical next step
