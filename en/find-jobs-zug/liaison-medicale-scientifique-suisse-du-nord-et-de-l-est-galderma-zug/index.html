Medical Scientific Liaison North and Eastern Switzerland

Location: North and Eastern Switzerland (field-based)

As Medical Scientific Liaison (MSL) for German-speaking Switzerland, you are part of the Medical Affairs team and are responsible for the scientific exchange with Key Opinion Leaders and healthcare professionals. You act as a competent contact person for medical-scientific questions and contribute to transferring current scientific findings into clinical practice.

In this position, you work closely with internal and external stakeholders and support the medical strategy by building sustainable scientific partnerships and providing evidence-based information.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for the medical-scientific exchange of products in the fields of therapeutic dermatology and injectable aesthetics
  • Building and maintaining long-term scientific relations with healthcare professionals and key-check leaders in German-speaking Switzerland
  • Presentation and communication of scientific and medical content during individual interviews, and events
  • Development and implementation of medical-scientific activities in accordance with the Medical Affairs Strategy
  • Provide sound medical information to internal and external stakeholders
  • Cooperation with Medical Affairs, Sales, Marketing and other cross-functional teams
  • Monitoring relevant scientific developments and the local health market and derived medical knowledge

Your profile

  • Completed studies in medicine, pharmacy, health sciences or a comparable degree programme (MSc or DD)
  • Professional experience in the field of medical action within the pharmaceutical industry
  • Experience with biologics or systemic therapies, ideally in the fields of dermatology, immunology, allergy or related indications
  • Experience in the development and presentation of scientific content for medical professionals
  • Extensive communication, presentation and relationship management skills and experience in building professional networks
  • Very good German and English knowledge in word and writing
  • High willingness to travel within Switzerland (approx. 80 %)
