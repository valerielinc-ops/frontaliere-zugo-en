Full description

As an automation technician, you take care of independent maintenance and minor repairs. The company offers you a comprehensive induction programme and promotes your professional development. We are looking for a job for our client

  • Independent planning and execution of maintenance work according to a specified cycle
  • Troubleshooting
  • Execution of minor repairs
  • Interesting on-call assignments
  • Customer communication
  • Requirements
  • Completed vocational training in the mechanical, electrical or hydraulic field (e.g. polymechanic, carmechanic, plant and equipment engineer or electrical installer)
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally with customer contact
  • Impeccable manners and a high willingness to learn
  • Good knowledge of the local language, other languages an advantage
  • Valid driver's license – you can't do it without an offer
  • 2-month induction at full pay in our lift camp for your optimal preparation for your new job.
  • Own company vehicle, which you can also use privately.
  • Compensation for your lunch, fair on-call compensation for on-call work and the cleaning of your work clothes, which are of course provided by us free of charge.
  • Excellent development opportunities: We live and breathe personnel development. We provide our employees with various national and international talent programs and support them in terms of time and financially in training and further education.
  • Work-life balance: 40h week, 25 to 30 vacation days, flexible working hours, part-time workload and unpaid vacation.
  • Healthy working environment: High health & safety standards and in-house health management including social counselling and health promotion.
  • Employee benefits: generous participation in the public transport and fitness plan, fleet discounts, discounted receipt of Reka money, discounted insurance premiums and various benefits.
