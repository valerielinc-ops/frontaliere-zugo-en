You enjoy customer contact, are very service-oriented, like to organize and feel comfortable in a dynamic and varied hub function? Your tasks

  • Contact person for our customers in German-speaking Switzerland by telephone and email

Your requirement profile

  • Enjoyment of customer contact and pronounced service orientation

  • Very good knowledge of German, basic knowledge of English and French is advantageous

You support our customers holistically throughout the entire lifecycle of their logistics systems and thus make an important contribution to their operational safety. The task offers a lot of personal responsibility, direct customer contact and a varied working environment.
