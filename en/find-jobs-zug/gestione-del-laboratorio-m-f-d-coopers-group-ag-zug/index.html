Full description

You are an experienced professional who thrives at the intersection of laboratory operations, regulatory compliance, and technical equipment management within a pharmaceutical or diagnostics environment. You combine deep expertise in GxP, GDP, and SHE standards with a sharp analytical mindset for risk assessments, equipment qualification (IQ/OQ/PQ), and process optimization.

Your responsibilities

  • You authorize lab access, optimize space with planning/SHE/facility teams, manage inventory and digital storage platforms (with weekly/monthly reconciliations), act as the primary facility/utility event contact, and supervise routine housekeeping and hazardous waste disposal.
  • You ensure all activities meet GxP, company, and local SHE regulations; enforce Good Documentation Practices (GDP) for logbooks and records; and represent the lab during internal/external audits through regular self-inspections and Gemba walks.
  • You lead and moderate risk assessments, including Equipment Change requests (ECRs) and Equipment Criticality Reviews, while planning and supervising all equipment qualifications (IQ/OQ/PQ), calibrations, maintenance, repairs, and decommissioning
  • You manage and maintain accurate SAP master data for all laboratory equipment across multiple rooms, review, and update essential technical documents (including URS, QP, and Periodic Reviews). Safety Enforcement & Stakeholder Relations: Partner with the SHE department and Biosafety Officer to enforce safety rules, exercise the authority to deny lab access for non-compliance, coordinate and monitor external vendor performance, and run regular meetings to resolve operational or scheduling conflicts.
  • You coach and mentor lab scientists on safety and compliance standards, and drive continuous improvement projects to make lab workflows and equipment qualification processes faster, safer, and more efficient. Team Support: Perform additional duties as assigned to support the broader R&D Services team and manage structured business continuity processes, such as formal handovers.

Your background

  • You have a Bachelor’s degree in the life sciences with 5+ years of experience, or a Master’s degree in the life sciences field with 3+ years of experience, specifically within a pharmaceutical or diagnostics laboratory.
  • You have a solid understanding of GxP quality frameworks, Good Documentation Practices (GDP), and Safety, Health, and Environmental (SHE) standards, with highly desirable experience in managing Non-Conformance (NCs) and participating in internal/external regulatory audits (e.g., ISO or health authority inspections).
  • You have a strong track record in project coordination— specifically regarding setting up lab workflows—with sharp attention to detail in process mapping, risk analysis, and technical problem-solving.
  • You hold a high degree of professional reliability with excellent stakeholder management skills, and a proactive approach to flagging operational risks and organizing vendor collaborations.
  • Fluent in German (written and spoken); Professional English language (written and spoken).
