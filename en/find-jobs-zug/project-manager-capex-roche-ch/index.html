The Position With a view to the future, we are actively shaping the further development of the Roche site in Rotkreuz. The Engineering & Projects department makes a decisive contribution to this: it is responsible for the strategic development of the site as well as for all investments in buildings and facilities (CAPEX). This includes complex construction projects as well as the realization of infrastructure and modern process plants in the areas of production and research. In all our activities, we attach particular importance to sustainability, safety (SHE), quality and cost-effectiveness.

The possibility

As Project Manager CAPEX you are responsible for

  • Project implementation: You are responsible for ensuring that all goals (extent, costs, schedule, quality, SHE, sustainability) are met. You ensure compliance with internal project methods, company guidelines and local building regulations.
  • Team & supplier management: Establishment, training and management of internal, cross-functional teams as well as the management of external partners (planners, entrepreneurs). This includes active participation in procurement, tendering and contract negotiations.
  • Project management & financial degree: Implementation and compliance with project management (progress, risk and change management) as well as monthly reporting. You report regularly to the Steering Committee and advance the commercial project completion
  • Stakeholder alignment & handover: Ensuring continuous coordination with business end users throughout the project lifecycle to guarantee successful commissioning (FAT/SAT etc.) and smooth, fully documented handover to operations.

Who you are

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree/university degree in engineering, construction or in a related field.
  • At least 5–10 years of professional experience as a project manager in the management of investment projects (CAPEX).
  • Practical experience with Diagnostics (DIA) projects is highly preferred.
  • Fluent German skills in word and writing are required.
  • Leadership: Proven ability to build, motivate and lead teams within a matrix organization.
  • Stakeholder management: Excellent communication and collaboration skills at all project levels, combined with a deep understanding of customer and business needs.
  • Project planning: Strong expertise in project management and planning to implement complex initiatives consistently and successfully.
  • Analytical skills & risk management: Very good analytical skills to identify risks early on and solve complex challenges.
