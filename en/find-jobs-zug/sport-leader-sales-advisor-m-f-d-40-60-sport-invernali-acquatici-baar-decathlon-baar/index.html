Full description

Mission Your Future Field: Decathlon Baar You will become part of our well-established team at the Decathlon Store in Baar. Right on our doorstep are the Zugersee, the Zugerberg and the Ägerisee – exactly where our customers experience their outdoor adventures on the water or in the snow. Your main focus is on the winter & water sports departments as well as customer service & cashier. Here you will meet a highly motivated team of passionate athletes who equip locals and tourists with the perfect equipment every day and provide a first-class shopping experience. Become part of a Swiss success story Decathlon Switzerland is growing rapidly: Since the start of 2017 in Neuchâtel, we have expanded our network to around 53 sales outlets. And we continue to expand – especially in German-speaking Switzerland. For you, this means a crisis-proof workplace with real, nationwide career opportunities. Why this job is perfect for you You are not looking for a classic 08/15 job, but want to share your passion for sports from morning to evening? Do you love the action on the sales area as much as the direct customer contact at the checkout and in the service area? Practical information:

  • Job: By immediate or by agreement
  • Place of work: Baar (ZG)
  • Pensum: 60% Your 3 biggest advantages with us:
  • In the middle of the action: Your everyday life takes place 100% on the sales area in Baar. You are the face of Decathlon and advise running enthusiasts and fitness fans with full passion. Rethinking retail: You not only sell, but also drive sustainable circular models – from material rental (e.g. treadmills, dumbbell sets or sports watches) to Second Life and support for digital click & collect orders. Your own empire: You are responsible for your fitness & running stock, provide an inspiring presentation of goods and showcase sporting and seasonal highlights. Your daily action: The tasks
  • Enthusiastic advice: You listen, analyze the needs and find the perfect equipment for stand-up paddling, swimming, skiing or snowboarding.
  • Store presentation: You ensure a seamless presentation of goods in the sports sector and at the cash registers and make your department dynamically and seasonally attractive.
  • Efficient goods management: From incoming goods to placing them on the shelf to preparing online orders, you are actively involved. This job is NOTHING for you if...
  • You prefer to sit quietly behind a desk all day.
  • It is difficult for you to approach people openly, patiently and service-oriented.
  • You think cashing, unpacking pallets, and racking up shelves are “not your job.”
  • You would rather have free Saturdays.
  • You are looking for rigid, unchanging working hours (we plan your bets fairly 6 weeks in advance, but work in the flexible rhythm of retail). Profile that you bring with you
  • Athlete DNA: You are active yourself and burn for winter and water sports.
  • Service orientation: Whether consulting on the surface or cashing under activity – you stay friendly, solution-oriented and calm.
  • Maker mentality: You are flexible and want to learn new things every day.
  • Language competence: You speak perfect German (understanding local dialect is a big plus). Good English or French skills are a great bonus. This is what we offer you (at 100% Pensum)
  • Attractive wage & bonus: A fixed gross salary between CHF 4'100 and CHF 5'000 (13 monthly wages) plus a monthly team bonus (up to 10% of the salary) and an annual profit share.
  • Your sports budget: CHF 225 per year for your sports or fitness subscription and 25% off all Decathlon products (and an additional 25% off sustainable mobility for your commute).
  • Recovery: 5 weeks of paid vacation per year.
  • Success Sharing: The opportunity to invest in Decathlon employee stock once a year.
  • Real career: internal development is lived with us – many of our managers have started on the sales floor! The application process: Fast & uncomplicated
  • Application: No long letter of motivation necessary! Just send us your CV and answer a few short questions.
  • Personality Check: You will receive a link to a 30-minute meeting questionnaire (AssessFirst). We read each application personally – you get your result immediately as feedback for yourself.
  • Conversation in Baar: We meet directly at the store in Baar to talk about your future playing field.
