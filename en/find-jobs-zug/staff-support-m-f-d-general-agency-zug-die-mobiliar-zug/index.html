This is what awaits you

In order to strengthen the support team for the care of our insurance and pension consultants, we are looking for a committed, independent, customer- and service-oriented personality.

You contribute directly to the satisfaction of our customers, actively support our insurance and pension consultants in all sales processes and make a valuable contribution to achieving our goals.

This is what you do with us

The daily customer contact on the phone / reception and via e-mail is close to your heart. You coordinate and support our insurance and pension consultants in all sales processes.

You take on an active role in this service- and sales-oriented cooperation and together with the team ensure optimal support and advice for our customers.

It gives you great pleasure to inspire our customers. With organizational skills, a lot of passion and a high awareness of service quality, you independently prepare offers and process applications and are part of the administrative hub of the General Agency Zug.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed apprenticeship as a businesswoman / Kauffmann EFZ private insurance
  • Several years of professional experience in insurance
  • Interest in obtaining a professional card is advantageous
  • Customer-oriented thinking and acting
  • Enjoying direct customer contact and close cooperation in the team
  • Independent and pragmatic working methods
  • You are communicative, reliable and proactive

We offer you a challenging, varied and independent job in a very motivated team, at a state-of-the-art workplace in a central location and attractive working conditions. We are also happy to support you in your personal development. Your personal development is important to us.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zug?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zug's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zug's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
