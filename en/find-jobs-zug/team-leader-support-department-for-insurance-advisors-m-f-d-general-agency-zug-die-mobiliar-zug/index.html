Full description

This is what awaits you

In this department you take over the leadership of the support team for our insurance consultants:innen. As a team leader, you take on a very important function with us at the general agency. You are passionate and enthusiastic at work – in customer contact and in the support of our insurance and pension consultants. You are also responsible for a team of around 6 employees.

This is what you do with us

The daily customer contact on the phone / reception and via e-mail is close to your heart. You coordinate and support our insurance and pension consultants in all sales processes. You take on an active role in this service- and sales-oriented cooperation and together with the team ensure optimal support and advice for our customers.

It gives you great pleasure to inspire our customers. With organizational skills, a lot of passion and a high awareness of service quality, you independently prepare offers and process applications and are part of the administrative hub of the General Agency Zug.

You are a motivated leader and ensure the professional and personnel leadership of your employees. At the same time, you build up the administrative support for the area of prevention in support. Thanks to your pronounced analytical thinking ability, you drive processes forward and actively develop the future of the general agency.

This is what you bring with you

  • Several years of professional experience in the insurance industry
  • Enjoyment of management tasks and operational responsibility
  • Communicative and assertive personality with negotiating skills
  • High social skills and teamwork

Working with us

We offer you a challenging, varied and independent job in a very motivated team, a state-of-the-art workplace in a central location and attractive working conditions. We are also happy to support you in your personal development. Your personal development is important to us.

Your place of work is train and your workload is 100%. The appointment shall be made by agreement.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zug?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zug's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zug's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
