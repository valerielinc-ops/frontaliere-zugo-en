Andreasklinik Cham Zug is located in the heart of Cham – nestled in a quiet and beautiful environment. Characteristic architecture and stylish interiors meet modern medical infrastructure in a hotel-like environment. The clinic specializes in the musculoskeletal system with orthopaedics as well as neuro- and spinal surgery. In addition, it offers comprehensive regional health care with the specialist areas of gynecology, urology, general surgery, general internal medicine and an emergency department open during the day and in the evening. First-class medical quality and the well-being of patients are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Preparation of X-rays in cooperation with radiology specialists
  • Sterile assistance for procedures in cooperation with the radiologist
  • Reception of radiology
  • Preparation and assistance in minimally invasive procedures such as biopsies and punctures
  • Vein indwelling cannulas
  • Care and maintenance of the material storage and material orders

Your profile

  • Completed training for medical practice assistant/in
  • Ideally with the additional competence for dose-intensive X-ray or interest in further training (financial participation of further training by Hirslanden possible)
  • Professional experience in a comparable function in an acute hospital is advantageous
  • Good knowledge of German, computer user knowledge and experienced handling of a KIS system
  • Engaged, service-oriented, friendly and communicative personality as well as joy of organizing

In addition to an attractive workplace on the beautiful Zugersee, you will find a family clinic and a dedicated, experienced team. We attach great importance to a working environment that is characterized by mutual respect and appreciation and ensure that you feel comfortable with us. Our benefits include:

  • Free coffee, tea and daily fresh fruits
  • Healthy and inexpensive menus in the staff restaurant
  • Cool employee discount platform (often with e.g. 11% Zalando, Ikea vouchers or 40% Adidas)
  • Free entrances to the Hallenbad Röhrliberg, the Tierpark Goldau and the Verkehrshaus
  • Paid dressing time with a workload of 100%, four additional free days. With a reduced workload, the free days decrease accordingly
