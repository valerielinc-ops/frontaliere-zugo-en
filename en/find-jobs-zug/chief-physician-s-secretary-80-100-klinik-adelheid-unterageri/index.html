Full description

Clinic Adelheid is the rehabilitation center of Central Switzerland. We look after patients for further treatment after operations, diseases of the musculoskeletal system, nervous system and internal organs. The clinic with 140 beds and spacious therapy rooms is located in a dreamlike location above the lake and is a company of the non-profit company Zug. We are looking for an agreement Chief Medical Secretary 80 - 100%

Tasks

In this responsible position, together with your team, you ensure the smooth organization of our medical secretariat. This includes timely writing of medical reports, secretarial work for the cadre doctors, organization of medical consultation hours, billing of outpatient medical services, contacts with our referral, health insurance companies and patients, etc. You take over the personnel, professional and organizational leadership of 5 employees and actively drive the further development of the secretariat.

Requirements

  • Completed basic commercial training and training as chief medical secretary
  • Professional experience in a medical environment, ideally in a chief medical secretariat
  • stylish knowledge of German and very good MS-Office user knowledge
  • High willingness to provide services and work independently
  • Professional appearance, discretion and reliability

Offer We offer you an interesting, versatile area of responsibility in an experienced, committed and motivated team. In addition, we offer you the opportunity to develop both professionally and in management issues. An innovative and future-oriented rehabilitation clinic with attractive employment conditions and personal development opportunities awaits you. We look forward to meeting you personally.
