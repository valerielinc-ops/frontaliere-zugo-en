Role overview

The position holder will be office based and is expected to travel occasionally for internal and external meetings, trainings and conferences.

It will be the responsibility of the position holder to help develop the presence and recognition of Sobi as a credible partner within the rare disease community through supporting the development of relationships with key stakeholders (especially physicians), as well as the successful development and delivery of agreed pre- and post-license marketing projects.

To fulfil the assigned tasks, specific medical knowledge and clinical experiences concerning diagnosing, differential diagnosing, consideration of different therapy options for the treatment of specific diseases including side effects, and risks are crucial (e.g. in order to develop proper marketing and information material and ensure the accuracy of medical content).
