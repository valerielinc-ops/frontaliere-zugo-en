Together with our customers, we combine the real and digital worlds With technology, software and services for smart buildings, we transform the everyday for everyone. As an AI Architect, you will define the technical architecture for customer-facing AI products, and guide our product teams in their implementation If you enjoy turning AI prototypes into secure, maintainable products that run on AWS or portable cloud-native platforms, we would like to meet you. You will own the architecture, standards, and technical decisions for our customer-facing AI products on AWS and portable cloud-native platforms. Working with product managers, AI platform engineers, software architects, and cybersecurity teams, you will establish reusable reference patterns and guides so product teams build AI features in a consistent way. This is a hands-on architecture role. You will build Python prototypes and reference code to test key architectural decisions before teams build them at scale, and support AI platform engineers and product developers through design reviews and hard technical problems. Would you also like to play an active role in influencing current trends and developments and make a positive impact on the lives of millions of people in Switzerland?
