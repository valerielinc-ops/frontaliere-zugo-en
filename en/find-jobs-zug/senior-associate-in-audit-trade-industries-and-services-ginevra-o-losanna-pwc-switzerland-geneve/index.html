Full description

In audit, we provide independent and objective assurance that financial files, statements and processes are accurate, reliable and compliant, in order to establish stakeholder trust. We therefore contribute to the integrity of financial systems, facilitate informed decision-making, protect against fraud and promote trust in the business environment. ## Responsibilities

  • Support the mission manager in the planning, execution and finalization of group and local audits in various non-financial services industries.
  • Write audit reports and recommendations.
  • Review the content of mandatory ESG information, including country-specific reports, and advise on areas for improvement.
  • Gain in-depth knowledge of a wide range of clients.
  • Coordinate a network of international teams on your engagements.
  • Join an active and diversified team in a rapidly evolving sector. ## Requirements
  • Hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Economics.
  • Possess, or be about to obtain, a certified accountant diploma or similar (DEC, ACCA, etc.).
  • 2 to 3 years of experience in an accounting firm with extensive experience in international accounting standards (IFRS).
  • Highly motivated and passionate about sustainable development, climate change issues and associated regulations.
  • Fluent English.
  • Enjoy teamwork and client interaction.
  • Possess excellent analytical and synthesis skills. ## Contact No contact information provided.
