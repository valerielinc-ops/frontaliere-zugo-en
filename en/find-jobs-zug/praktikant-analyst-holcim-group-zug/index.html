Full description

Holcim is the leading partner for sustainable construction with net sales of CHF 15.7 billion in 2025, creating value across the built environment from infrastructure and industry to buildings. Headquartered in Zug, Switzerland, Holcim has more than 50 000 employees in 45 countries – across Europe, Latin America and Asia, Middle East & Africa – and has been recognized as a Global Top Employer by the Top Employers Institute. Holcim offers high-value end-to-end Building Materials and Building Solutions, from foundation and flooring to walling and roofing – powered by premium brands including ECOPact, ECOPlanet, ECOCycle, and Ytong.

Role Overview

Are you ready to use high-stakes financial modeling to solve the planet's biggest challenge? At Holcim, Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (CCUS) is at the heart of our net-zero transformation. We are seeking an ambitious, analytical, and structured Strategy & Business Development Intern Analyst to join our global Carbon Management team in Zug, Switzerland .

Working directly alongside the Head of Business Development and senior leaders across Finance, M&A, Legal, and regional business units, you will play an active role in evaluating, modeling, and executing high-impact decarbonization projects. The role is on a fixed term contract for 11 months.

Main Responsibilities

Financial Modeling & Business Cases: Develop and refine project-level financial models, assessing investment economics, value drivers, sensitivities, and risk scenarios for large-scale CCUS initiatives.

Strategic & Market Intelligence: Conduct competitive benchmarking, market analysis, and regulatory evaluations to steer commercial strategy and project structuring.

Deal & Financing Support: Assist in assessing capital structures, evaluating third-party financing options, and engaging with financial institutions, investors, and M&A teams.

Executive & Stakeholder Material: Draft high-impact board materials, strategy decks, and decision papers for senior management and public industry engagements.

PROFILE REQUIRED

Education: University degree in Finance, Economics, Business Administration, Engineering, or a related quantitative field.

Experience: Prior experience (1–2 years via full-time roles or high-impact internships) in Corporate Finance, Investment Banking, Strategy Consulting, Infrastructure/Project Finance, or Corporate Development.

Core Skills: Advanced quantitative modeling skills, structured problem-solving capabilities, and strong financial acumen.

Communication: Exceptional written and verbal English communication; ability to translate complex financial/technical data into clear executive insights.

Mindset: Proactive, collaborative, highly detail-oriented, and comfortable navigating dynamic, multi-stakeholder environments.

Mobility requirements

10% of international travelling worldwide required
