The Position As a Digital & Analytics Intern at Roche Diagnostics in Rotkreuz, Switzerland, you will join an innovative team solving operational challenges through data, digital products, and AI. Working in a hybrid model, you will contribute across the entire solution lifecycle—from problem identification to prototyping and adoption. Designed for candidates with backgrounds in Data Engineering or Data Analytics & Science, your assignment will be tailored to your strengths and project needs (expertise in every listed technology is not required). This internship position is located in Rotkreuz, Switzerland, with a hybrid working model.

The Opportunity

  • You work with business and process experts to understand operational challenges, identify opportunities, translate them into clear requirements and define what success looks like.
  • You design and build dashboards, analytical applications and lightweight web applications using technologies such as Python, R or TypeScript, with frameworks such as React or FastAPI where appropriate.
  • You develop and improve reliable data pipelines and transformations using technologies such as SQL, Airflow and dbt.
  • You contribute to automation, DevOps and CI/CD practices, including testing, deployment and maintainable development workflows.
  • You contribute to solution concepts, prototypes and feasibility assessments together with experienced team members and stakeholders.
  • You contribute to data governance, data quality and digital engineering standards to support reliable and compliant solutions.
  • You communicate technical concepts and findings clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders through documentation, demos and presentations.
  • You work with Product Care data across areas such as supply, quality and engineering, improving data availability and supporting better operational decisions.

Who You Are

  • You are currently enrolled in a Master’s degree or have completed a Bachelor’s degree within the last 12 months prior to the internship start date, preferably in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field.
  • You bring practical experience with at least one programming language such as Python, TypeScript, or R, alongside basic experience with SQL and working with data.
  • You bring experience developing an application, analytical solution, or substantial software project through your studies, work experience, or personal projects.
  • You possess strong communication skills and fluent English, enabling effective collaboration. German is nice to have.
  • You possess a high level of problem-solving competence, strong analytical skills, and the ability to work independently, learn quickly, and collaborate effectively with others.
  • An understanding of basic software-design principles and an interest in modern software engineering and AI is considered an asset.
  • Hands-on experience with Git, CI/CD, or automated testing, and exposure to technologies like React, FastAPI, dbt, or Airflow is preferred. Experience with AI agents, data visualization, or building data products is nice to have.

Additional information

  • Start Date & Duration: The preferred start date for the internship is January 2027 with a duration of 12 months or upon discussion.
  • Workload: Flexible working options from 80% to 100% full-time equivalent are open for discussion.
  • Thesis Note: Please note that the internship cannot be combined with a university thesis.

Application Process

  • Please upload your CV (in English) , a motivation letter with your application, and a certificate of enrolment (if you are currently studying) or your degree certificate (if you have already completed your studies).
  • Non-EU/Non-EFTA citizens : Must attach a confirmation from the university to the application documents, stating that a mandatory internship is part of the education and be continuously enrolled in their university for the whole duration of this internship.
