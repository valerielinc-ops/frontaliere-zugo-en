Full description

Your tasks

  • You will take care of the professional commissioning and repair work of heat pumps and Comfort-Clima.
  • You take over the careful maintenance of these devices according to the high Buderus quality standards. You analyze and repair disruptions – expertly, quickly and independently.
  • You advise customers on the ideal settings of the devices according to personal customer needs. You will inform plant owners about the comprehensive benefits of Buderus maintenance contracts.
  • You provide on-call service (piket) in consultation with the team.

Your profile

  • Education: EFZ training; preferably in the electrical, heating, machine or car industry, NIV-15 degree
  • Personality and working method: reliable, self-responsible, service-oriented, resilient and flexible
  • Experience: Sound experience in the field of heat pumps
  • Languages: stylish German skills
