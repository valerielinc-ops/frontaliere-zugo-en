Become part of our team!

We, Raiffeisenbank Cham-Steinhausen, are a successful and innovative bank, which is one of the largest Raiffeisen banks in Switzerland. At our locations in Cham, Steinhausen and Mettmenstetten, we employ around 80 people, including 5 apprentices. To complement our team at our headquarters in Cham, we are looking for a winning, committed and service-oriented personality.

What awaits you?

Controlling/Controlling

  • Preparation and analysis of key figures for the bank’s financial planning
  • Preparation of meaningful reporting, reports and evaluations
  • Analysis of earnings development and identification of causes and trends
  • Preparation of the budget, multiannual and investment planning in close cooperation with the bank management
  • Supporting the bank management in the supervision of asset and liability management (ALM) and liquidity management

Accounting

  • Management of internal bank accounts (main accounts and settlement accounts)
  • Accounting of accounts payable and accounts payable
  • Implementation of quarterly and annual accounts

Computer science

  • Central contact person for employees in IT issues as well as coordination with the IT of Raiffeisen Switzerland
  • Planning of the IT and telecommunications infrastructure including maintenance and lifecycle management
  • Responsibility for authorization and access management
  • Participation in projects and initiatives in the field of IT and digitalization

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training and several years of professional experience in the area of responsibility
  • Strong IT affinity and interest in digital developments and technologies
  • Analytical thinking and a structured, exact and efficient way of working
  • Willingness and motivation to develop professionally in the mentioned areas
  • Secure appearance, communication strength, teamwork, reliability and responsibility
