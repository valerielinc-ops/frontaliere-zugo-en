Andreasklinik Cham Zug is located in the heart of Cham – nestled in a quiet and beautiful environment. Characteristic architecture and stylish interiors meet modern medical infrastructure in a hotel-like environment. The clinic specializes in the musculoskeletal system with orthopaedics as well as neuro- and spinal surgery. In addition, it offers comprehensive regional health care with the specialist areas of gynecology, urology, general surgery, general internal medicine and an emergency department open during the day and in the evening. First-class medical quality and the well-being of patients are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Instrumentation and service in operations of various disciplines such as orthopaedics, urology, ENT, gynecology, general, visceral, plastic, neuro and spinal surgery
  • Implementation of all preparatory and follow-up measures of the operation
  • Professional privileged operation of the necessary equipment, instruments and sterile goods
  • Careful electronic documentation of all nursing activities and performance recording
  • Assistance in ensuring the smooth running of operations and readiness for piquets

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a specialist surgical technique with professional experience in the operating room
  • Rapid comprehension
  • High resilience and social skills
  • Strong service readiness and customer orientation
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary teamwork with doctors, employees
