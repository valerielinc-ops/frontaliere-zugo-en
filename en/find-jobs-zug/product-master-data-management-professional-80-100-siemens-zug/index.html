We are looking for a Product Master Data Management Professional for our Franchise & Governance Business Master Data Management (BMDM) team, within our Smart Infrastructure Buildings Global Headquarters The BMDM team is responsible for the global product master data lifecycle across SI Buildings, operating the MDM platform, managing data governance, and ensuring data quality for connected business processes and downstream systems. Location : This position will be ideally based in Zug – Switzerland The Role This position is created to strengthen the team's capacity in the context of increasing strategic demands. The role combines hands-on MDM operations with data governance coordination and project support, contributing directly to SI Building's data strategy and digital transformation Your new responsibilities -Shape and drive the future of Product Master Data Management across the BU as part of a global transformation journey -Contribute to Product Master Data initiatives and cross-functional programs, ensuring alignment between business objectives, product lifecycle processes, and digital solutions -Continuously evolve the global Product Master Data governance framework, defining standards, policies, and best practices that enable consistency, compliance, and operational excellence -Partner with business and IT stakeholders worldwide to define, prioritize, and implement enhancements for Product Lifecycle Management (PLM), Product Identification, and Master Data Management processes -Act as a key driver for data quality excellence by establishing governance measures, monitoring data quality performance, and promoting continuous improvement across the organization -Lead global change management activities related to Product Master Data, including feature rollouts, process harmonization, communication campaigns, and stakeholder engagement -Serve as a trusted advisor and subject matter expert for Product Master Data Management, providing guidance, consulting, and training to global and regional users Your talents and experience -Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Business, IT, or related field -Minimum 3 years’ experience in product data management or related IT or project management fields -Knowledge of SAP MM, SD or Stibo STEP is a plus -Experience with agile development -Experience in global support operations and training delivery -Proven analytical and structuring skills with the ability to quickly understand complex, cross-functional processes -Ability to explain complex data topics to non-technical audiences -High ownership mindset and willingness to step outside comfort zones -Strong communication skills and fluency in German and English is required Employee benefits
