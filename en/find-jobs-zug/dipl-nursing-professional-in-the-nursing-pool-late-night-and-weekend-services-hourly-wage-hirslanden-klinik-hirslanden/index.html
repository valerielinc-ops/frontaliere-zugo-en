Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden AndreasKlinik Cham Zug | Cham - Kt. Zug

Occupation by: Immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 65005

AndreasKlinik Cham Zug is centrally located in Cham, in a quiet and beautiful environment. The clinic combines modern medical infrastructure with a stylish, hotel-like atmosphere. She specializes in orthopaedics, neuro- and spinal surgery and additionally offers comprehensive regional care in gynecology, urology, general surgery and internal medicine as well as an emergency department open during the day and in the evening. First-class medical quality and the well-being of patients are our focus.

In the care pool of the AndreasKlinik Cham Zug you work without a fixed workload. You will be employed on an hourly basis and will jump in at short notice for outages – for example in late, night or weekend services. This allows you to flexibly support our teams and actively contribute to ensuring continuous and high-quality patient care.

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional care and support of our patients as well as independent implementation of the diagnostic and therapeutic measures prescribed by the doctor
  • Identification and control of emergency situations and initiation of adequate measures until the arrival of the doctor
  • Assuming responsibility for the correct patient documentation as well as information sharing in the assigned task area
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with our observing physicians and other areas
  • Active participation in projects and support of department management and the entire team to ensure the best possible quality of care

Your profile

  • Completed training for / dipl. Nurse / Nurse specialist HF / FH
  • Professional experience in acute care
  • Very good knowledge of German (verbal and written)
  • High social skills and team-oriented personality
  • Positive basic attitude, high patient orientation and independent and structured working methods

Our offer

  • Flexible, hour-based assignments in the care pool
  • Family working environment and a dedicated team
  • Attractive benefits: free coffee/tea, fresh fruit, discounted menus, employee discounts, free admissions to the indoor pool Röhrliberg, the Tierpark Goldau and the Verkehrshaus
  • Opportunity to gain experience in different departments
