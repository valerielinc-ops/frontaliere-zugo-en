Full description

Position Overview At Abbott Cancer Diagnostics, we are cancer fighters. We are united by our mission to change lives by providing earlier, smarter answers. Through advances in cancer detection and treatment guidance, we will help eradicate the disease and the suffering it causes.

The Manager, International Business Systems will lead a team of application analysts, system administrators, and testers and is a key leader in the International IT team at Abbott Cancer Diagnostics. The role is responsible for the support, stability, and continuous improvement of the business applications that underpin the International Business Unit’s critical Order to Cash (O2C) processes, principally Salesforce (SFDC), from customer and order management through to invoicing. These systems are central to how our International Business serves customers and partners across multiple countries, enabling orders for our cancer diagnostic tests to be processed accurately and on time.

This critical role turns business processes into effective technology solutions. It partners closely with other product teams, and with various stakeholders across commercial, customer service, finance and operations, to understand their needs, shape priorities and define clear specifications. The holder will also need to collaborate closely with technical teams from across IT, including Salesforce development, integration, architecture, infrastructure, security, data and global IT services, to ensure changes are well designed, delivered and supported.

As our International Business grows, this role will help our critical business systems scale to new markets, products and partners while continuing to deliver the value the business expects. The role is based in our office at Neuhofstrasse 22 in Baar (ZG), Switzerland.

Essential Duties Include, but are not limited to, the following:

  • Manage a team of application analysts, system administrators, and testers, who are responsible for the day-to-day application management activities, including backlog grooming, release planning, and enhancement prioritization. Ensure their work is in alignment with department objectives and informed by strategic directions from leadership.
  • Demonstrate an understanding of business strategy and the ability to translate this into user stories, business requirements, and feature requests.
  • Manage the International Order to Cash (O2C) application portfolio, principally Salesforce, and lead the team with business analysis and gathering user feedback to define application requirements.

  • Lead tracking product performance using metrics and analytics to support ongoing optimization and iteration.
  • Be a resource for and mentor team members of varying degrees of seniority by providing insight, recommendations, and guidance on application management best practices.
  • Maintain strong relationships and transparency with key stakeholders across multiple functions and at all levels of the company.
  • Represent the application management discipline in cross-functional initiatives, as assigned by Management, and act on its behalf as appropriate.
  • Effectively manage, support, and guide the team, including, but not limited to delegating tasks and responsibilities, assessing employee performance and providing helpful feedback and training opportunities.
