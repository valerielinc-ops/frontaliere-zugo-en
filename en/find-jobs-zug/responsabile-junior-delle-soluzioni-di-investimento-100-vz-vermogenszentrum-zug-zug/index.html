Full description

VZ is Switzerland’s leading independent financial services provider and a pioneer in rule-based investment. We offer our clients a wide range of asset management mandates that cover their different needs. For the operation of mandates, which are primarily implemented with ETF/index funds in the equity and interest value sector, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality for our headquarters in a central location in Zug.

  • You will become an expert for our asset management mandates and build up a comprehensive understanding of our investment solutions.
  • You monitor the mandates on an ongoing basis with regard to positioning, performance and other key figures and create comprehensive reporting on them.
  • You support the operational operation of our mandates. This includes, for example, updating documents, adjusting allocations and validating transactions.
  • You analyze competition offers and prepare the findings in a structured manner.
  • You will become the central point of contact for our investment solutions as well as for competitive offers and support consultants with technical questions.
  • You create useful documents for our consultants and for our customers, for example training, presentations and customer documentation.
  • You contribute to the further development of our investment solutions and prepare well-founded decision-making bases for the management.
  • You work on projects around the mandates and on process optimizations.
  • You communicate and coordinate tasks with various interfaces.
  • You are interested in investment solutions, financial markets and investment topics and quickly get involved in new topics.
  • You work reliably, accurately and structured – even if it gets hectic.
  • You like to work in a team and see yourself as a service provider.
  • You feel comfortable in a dynamic, service-oriented environment.
  • You want to continuously develop and expand your knowledge.
  • You work safely with Excel and PowerPoint. Power BI skills are a plus.
  • You have completed an education or study in finance, banking or economics.
  • The opportunity to pursue an exciting and versatile activity with direct customer benefit in the asset management of a successful and rapidly growing company.
  • An asset management in one of the most digital banks in Switzerland that knows how to take advantage of new technologies.
  • An environment that supports and promotes you in your constant further education and development.
  • A company that relies on agile working principles such as OKR and Lean Management.
  • An innovative, dynamic and collegial environment as well as a corporate culture of which we are proud.
