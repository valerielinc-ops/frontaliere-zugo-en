Company vision

At McDonald, every visit becomes a sunny experience. Our team loves to make guests smile and create a welcoming atmosphere in which everyone feels comfortable. If you like to be in contact with people and are looking for a job that is easy, dynamic and full of opportunities, you are exactly right for us!

Job description

As Guest Experience Leader, you are the bright heart of our restaurant. You greet our guests with a smile, introduce them to our latest offers and support them in ordering. With your positive energy, you motivate the team and make every guest happy. You have an eye for the small details and an open ear for the wishes and feedback of our guests, who you share with the management to make every visit even better.

Main tasks

  • Greetings to guests and creating a cheerful atmosphere
  • Presentation of new offers and support for orders
  • Sharing of guest feedback to team and management
  • Support of the team with enthusiasm and team spirit
  • Ensuring a perfect restaurant experience for every guest

Requirements

  • Sunny, open nature
  • Passion, making people happy
  • Team spirit and reliability
  • Attention for details and willingness to listen
  • Motivation to learn and develop
  • no experience necessary!

We offer

  • Flexible working hours for a good work-life balance
  • Dynamic, friendly and supportive team
  • Training and training opportunities
  • many managers start as team members!
  • Remuneration and working conditions according to the provisions of the general employment contract (L-GAV)
  • 50% discount on our products in our restaurants in Switzerland and Liechtenstein
  • Sunrise subscriptions at a reduced price
  • Access to exclusive discounts via the platform "61 3"
  • Additional holidays offered for over 50 years old
  • Free access to the largest online training library
