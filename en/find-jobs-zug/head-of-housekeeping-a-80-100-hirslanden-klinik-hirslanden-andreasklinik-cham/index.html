Occupation by 1 February 2027 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Andreasklinik Cham Zug is located in the heart of Cham – nestled in a quiet and beautiful environment. Characteristic architecture and stylish interiors meet modern medical infrastructure in a hotel-like environment. The clinic specializes in the musculoskeletal system with orthopaedics as well as neuro- and spinal surgery. In addition, it offers comprehensive regional health care with the specialist areas of gynecology, urology, general surgery, general internal medicine and an emergency department open during the day and in the evening. First-class medical quality and the well-being of patients are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Support of the Housekeeping Management in the management of the cleaning, cleaning and housekeeping teams with around 30 employees
  • Coordination of day-to-day operations and ensuring a smooth operation
  • Supervision and control of quality and hygiene standards in the clinic and care center
  • Active participation in the operational cleaning service and support of the various domestic sectors
  • Assuming administrative tasks such as ordering, warehousing and organisational activities
  • Deputy Head of Housekeeping in case of absences and participation in the further development of processes and employees

Your profile

  • Completed training as a specialist in housekeeping EFZ or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in housekeeping as well as initial management experience, ideally in the healthcare sector
  • Enjoyment of operational cooperation as well as a hands-on mentality and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Pronounced service orientation, high social competence and appreciative treatment of employees, patients and residents
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good user knowledge of the common MS Office programs
  • Flexible, resilient and organizationally strong personality with good communication skills and willingness to work different hours and occasional weekend services

In addition to an attractive workplace on the beautiful Zugersee, you will find a family clinic and a dedicated, experienced team. We attach great importance to a working environment that is characterized by mutual respect and appreciation and ensure that you feel comfortable with us. Our benefits include:

  • Free coffee, tea and daily fresh fruits
  • Healthy and inexpensive menus in the staff restaurant
  • Cool employee discount platform (often with e.g. 11% Zalando, Ikea vouchers or 40% Adidas)
  • Free entrances to the Hallenbad Röhrliberg, the Tierpark Goldau and the Verkehrshaus
  • Paid dressing time with a workload of 100%, four additional free days. With a reduced workload, the free days decrease accordingly
