Full description

Holcim has the vision to be the leading partner for sustainable construction. With net sales of CHF 15.7 billion in 2025, we create value across the built environment from infrastructure and industry to buildings. Headquartered in Zug, Switzerland, Holcim has more than 50 000 employees in 45 countries – across Europe, Latin America and Asia, Middle East & Africa – and has been recognized as a Global Top Employer by the Top Employers Institute. Holcim offers high-value end-to-end Building Materials and Building Solutions, from foundation and flooring to walling and roofing – powered by premium brands including ECOPact, ECOPlanet, ECOCycle, and Ytong.

Role Overview

Shape the future of sustainable construction. Drive external communications for a global industry pioneer.

Holcim is seeking a Head of External Communications to lead relations, drive corporate reporting and protect our reputation as we elevate our positioning as a leading partner for building solutions.

The Impact You’ll Make

You will drive media strategy, corporate reporting and manage issues across global markets. • Drive the Global Narrative: Position Holcim as the world’s leading partner in sustainable, circular, energy-efficient resilient and smart building solutions—from foundation and flooring to walling and roofing • Lead Executive & Media Presence: Brief senior leaders including ExCo and Country CEOs for teir 1 engagements; build high-impact relationships with top-tier international, national, and industry trade media • Master Issues & Reputation Management: Proactively steer corporate issues and act as senior counsel during high-stakes situations. • Transform Corporate Reporting: Elevate both financial and non-financial reporting into compelling omni channel communication. • Mobilize the Network: Mentor a direct team of communications specialists while leading and inspiring a global network of 200+ communicators.

Who You Are

• The Leader: 10+ years in corporate communications, PR agencies, or business journalism with a proven track record in a global corporate environment (Swiss / SMI experience preferred). • The Strategist: Expert in integrated omnichannel communications, public affairs, and crisis management. • The Spokesperson: Outspoken advocate with the executive presence to engage media, counsel senior leadership, and represent Holcim globally. • The Collaborator: Entrepreneurial self-starter who drives results through direct leadership and functional influence. • Languages: Fluent in English; proficiency in German is a strong plus.

Travel Requirements

• Ability to travel internationally (20%)
