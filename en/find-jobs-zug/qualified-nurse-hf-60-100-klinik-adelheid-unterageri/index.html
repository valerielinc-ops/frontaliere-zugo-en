Full description

The clinic Adelheid with 140 beds stands as the leading rehabilitation center of Central Switzerland for the highest quality in patient care. The range of services includes the inpatient rehabilitation of musculoskeletal, neurological, internistic-oncological and geriatric patients. The clinic is located in a dreamlike location above Lake Ägerise and is a company of the non-profit company Zug. We are looking for an agreement Dipl. Nursing professional HF 60 - 100% They actively participate in the rehabilitation process in the interdisciplinary team and thus make an important contribution to the reintegration of patients into their usual environment. You also find working with a fully electronic patient dossier attractive. Our desired profile

  • Completion as a dipl. Nurse with SRC recognition
  • Professional experience in the acute hospital is an advantage
  • Team-capable, communicative and organizational skills
  • Responsible, reliable and resilient
  • Good MS-Office user knowledge
