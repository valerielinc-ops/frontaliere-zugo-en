Equans Switzerland Facility Management AG. Shaping the future together.

We specialize in holistic facilities management services for every phase of the business real estate and area lifecycle. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve comfort in buildings.
