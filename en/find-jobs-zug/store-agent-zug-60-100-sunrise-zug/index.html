Full description

At Sunrise, we work closely together as a team and are close to our customers in the shop every day. Our culture combines respect, openness and new ideas with a strong team spirit. We support each other, take responsibility in everyday life and know that every voice counts and every perspective makes us better.

Our passion motivates us to try new things and to continuously develop – always with the aim of creating a positive customer experience.

You like to work in a team, take on responsibility and are in direct contact with customers: inside? Become part of our team.

What You Expect

Our front-line teams shape Sunrise’s customer experience. With personal advice, customized solutions and top service, every contact becomes a long-term customer relationship.

Your challenge

  • You actively approach our customers, understand their needs and offer suitable solutions.
  • You inspire sales with clear, honest advice and a convincing appearance.
  • You show a high level of self-motivation, achieve your monthly goals and actively contribute to the success of the shop.
  • You handle service and support issues (e.g. device service, questions about products/subscriptions) in a solution-oriented, competent and reliable manner.
  • Together with the team, you ensure a welcoming shop experience and a positive atmosphere.
  • You keep track of administrative and operational tasks (e.g. cash, warehouse, order).
  • Use downtimes for exciting outbound campaigns and generate additional sales opportunities
  • You live the Sunrise values Bold, Passionate and One in everyday life and actively promote cooperation, customer proximity and initiative.

Your kills

  • Ideally, you already have experience in a comparable role in retail or convince as a motivated:r lateral entrant:in with a pronounced sales flair and service orientation.
  • You bubble with enthusiasm for technology and digital products and explain them clearly.
  • You work goal-oriented and customer-oriented, are reliable and keep an overview even in hectic situations.
  • You show flexibility and are ready to be used regularly on Saturdays and Sundays
  • You have very good knowledge of German, every other language is a plus.

At Us, you work in a dynamic and multinational environment where every voice is heard, perspectives are shared and values are lived. Diversity is not just a program for us, but part of our DNA. Regardless of who you are or where you come from, at Sunrise you will find your place. We create an inclusive environment in which all employees feel belonging and can fully develop.
