Foundational Services Developer Enablement and Security (FDS) is building One Software Engineering System (OSES) — a unified, configurable developer platform that streamlines development workflows, reduces developer toil, and accelerates innovation across Siemens. OSES provides a paved path for developers with integrated onboarding, tools, and best practices that increase productivity and deliver measurable business value.
