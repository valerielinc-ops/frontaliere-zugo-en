Sales Representative - Infectious Diseases (m/f/d)

The position

This position will be joined to our Baar site (Abbott Rapid Diagnostics Schweiz GmbH) in the Infectious Diseases division and will be responsible for Switzerland.

Our diagnostic solutions are used in hospitals, laboratories and clinics around the globe. The crucial information that is gained from our tests, instruments and computer systems is often the first step in patient care decision-making for hundreds of health conditions, from heart attacks to blood diseases to infectious diseases and cancer.

Our fast diagnostic solutions help to address some of the world's biggest challenges in healthcare.
