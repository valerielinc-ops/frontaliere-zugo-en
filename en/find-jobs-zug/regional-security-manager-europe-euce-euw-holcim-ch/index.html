Role overview

The Regional Security Manager is responsible for managing and advancing Holcim’s Security operations across the region.

This position ensures the protection of Holcim’s employees, assets, ecosystem, and reputation against security risks, including criminal and malicious acts.

Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Security & Resilience governance related responsibilities:
  • Act as Group Security and Resilience liaison manager with the country security managers/coordinators (CSM/CSC), the country CEOs, and the Heads of Central and East Europe (EUCE) or West Europe (EUW).
  • Drive the consistent implementation of Holcim’s Security & Resilience governance in the Europe (EUCE or EUW) region.
  • Assist the CSM/CSC with the implementation and continuous improvement of Holcim’s Security & Resilience governance in their country.
  • Provide expert guidance to the business in the proactive management of security risks, ensuring the protection of people, assets, and reputation.
  • Deliver and support training and awareness programs to embed a culture of security and resilience in the region.
  • Maintain regional situational awareness, including internal and external risk assessments, to inform business decision-making.
  • Develop and maintain relationships with governments, authorities, international agencies, and private security providers. Crisis Management and Incident Response:
  • Oversee and coordinate regional security operations to ensure they are aligned with corporate standards and respond effectively to security incidents.
