Full description

The clinic Adelheid with 140 beds stands as the leading rehabilitation center of Central Switzerland for the highest quality in patient care. The range of services includes the inpatient rehabilitation of musculoskeletal, neurological, internistic-oncological and geriatric patients. The clinic is located in a dreamlike location above Lake Ägerise and is a company of the non-profit company Zug. Every year we offer training places starting in January, August or November: Studying for Dipl. Care professional HF You will visit the theoretical block at the educational center XUND in Lucerne and complete the practical training part with us. The training lasts 3 or 2 years depending on the previous knowledge. Our desired profile

  • completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ or gymnasium maturity
  • for cross-entry 2-day selection internship
  • Computer skills
  • Strong social and professional skills
  • Ability to think conceptually and interdisciplinary
  • High degree of independence, flexibility and responsibility
  • Organisational talent and enforcement
  • Physical and psychological resilience
