As an employee in our marketing team, you work on the pulse of our division. With your structured and networked way of thinking, you make an important contribution to implementing the global strategy into local measures for our FreeStyle Libre users. Thanks to your experience in the pharmaceutical or medical technology industry, you can do this in 4 of our national languages (German, English, French and Italian).

  • Local implementation of global consumer marketing strategy
  • Planning and implementing measures (digital & print) incl. Budget Planning and Control in Opti Channel Marketing
  • Define and analyze relevant KPIs as well as ongoing optimization of activities based on these results (ROI)
  • Contact person for our internal and external partners
  • Internal interface to other departments (local, European and global) and the field service

They have a reliable, independent and goal-oriented hands-on working style and have the following requirements:

  • graduate degree in marketing or business administration or sound, relevant professional experience in a comparable field
  • Several years of marketing experience in the Swiss pharmaceutical or medical technology industry with a focus on digital marketing
  • Language skills: Negotiable in word and writing in German, French and English as well as advanced knowledge in Italian
  • Ability to empathize with our B2B customers and to plan appropriate measures (digital & print)
  • Strength in the implementation of measures in 3 languages (German, French, Italian)
  • Affinity for digital health tools
  • Experience in working with agencies
  • Secure appearance in dealing with internal and external stakeholders
  • Understanding internal processes and their compliance
  • Strong team and customer orientation in an international environment
  • Situation-adapted flexibility for successful prioritization and your own demand for high-quality implementation in an agile environment
  • Willingness to travel up to 20%

If you are a flexible, open-minded and committed personality, Abbott offers you not only a varied and demanding task but also a pleasant and open working atmosphere in an exciting, international environment.
