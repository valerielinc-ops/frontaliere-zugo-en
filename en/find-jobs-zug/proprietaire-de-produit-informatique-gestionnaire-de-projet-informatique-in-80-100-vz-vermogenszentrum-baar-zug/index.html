Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With the VZ custodian bank, we offer our customers the opportunity to process transactions efficiently and cheaply. As IT Product Owner "Technical & Integration" and IT Project Manager, you are responsible for the technical conception, further development and integration of solutions around our banking-relevant application landscape. You act as a technical contact person between departments, IT and external partners and ensure stable, secure and sustainable solutions.

  • Technical development and optimization of the application landscape, especially in the finnova environment
  • Analysis of technical requirements and design of technical solution architectures
  • Modeling of business processes and technical implementation
  • Evaluation and integration of interfaces, services and modules
  • Technical advice to departments and support to IT teams
  • Management of technical projects (including planning, testing, introduction and transfer of operations) in compliance with time, scope and budget
  • Completed degree in computer science, business informatics or comparable qualification
  • Over five years of experience as a technical project manager in the banking sector
  • Experience with Finnova, Avaloq or comparable banking software is advantageous
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills as well as high affinity for digitization topics.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • The opportunity to contribute ideas and drive innovation in a young, growing company.
  • Modern working methods (e.g. OKR with sprints).
  • Individual support within the framework of the job profile and support for external further education.
  • You work in a company that Lucerne University of Applied Sciences counts among the most digital banks in Switzerland.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
