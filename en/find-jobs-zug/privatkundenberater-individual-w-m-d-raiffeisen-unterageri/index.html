Become part of our team!

You love personal contact, think entrepreneurially and want to accompany customers in important life decisions? Then you are exactly right with us. We are the Raiffeisenbank region Ägerital-Sattel: rooted in the region, modern and close to our customers. With us you do not expect an anonymous large company, but a committed team with short paths, mutual support and a lot of space for your own ideas. To strengthen our private customer team, we are looking for a personality who lives consulting, takes responsibility and wants to make a difference together with us.

What awaits you?

  • You take over your own customer portfolio and build long-term relationships
  • You accompany people in all financial phases of life, from the first home to the provision up to the investment
  • You develop individual and sustainable solutions instead of standard concepts
  • You win new customers for our cooperative values and our strong regional anchoring
  • You bring in ideas and actively shape the development of our market
  • You work in a team that supports each other and celebrates success together

What do you bring?

  • Banking education and further education in banking or finance (SAQ certification individual client advice is advantageous)
  • Enjoyment in consulting and building long-term customer relationships
  • An open, sympathetic and safe appearance
  • Entrepreneurship and initiative
  • Negotiation skills and enthusiasm for customer contact
  • Team spirit, motivation and the desire to make a difference

You inspire people, recognize opportunities and want to accompany customers in all phases of life? I look forward to meeting you personally. Andrea Anna Oeuvray Head of Private Client Consulting Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We enable the purchase of additional holiday weeks

Our employees can purchase up to two additional holiday weeks per year in addition to the regular holiday days.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
