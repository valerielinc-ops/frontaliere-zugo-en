Full description

The management of projects in the high and low-current sectors requires precise deadline and quality control. You maintain contact with demanding customers and successfully coordinate cooperation with other trades. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • management of small and medium-sized projects in the high and low current area
  • Responsible for order preparation and for the timely and professional execution of orders
  • Assuming coordination tasks with other trades and leading the subordinate employees
  • Creating and processing offers, supplements and dimensions as well as partial and final invoices
  • Acquisition of orders as well as active support of our demanding customers
  • Responsible for achieving the project goal regarding occupational safety, costs, deadlines and quality
  • Compliance with the specifications and previous life of occupational safety as well as training of employees
  • Requirements
  • Basic training as an electrical installer EFZ
  • Continuing education as an electrical project manager with federal law. FA or in training for the Federal Dipl. Electrical installer
  • Skilled handling of industry standards such as NIN, NPK and Steiger
  • Willingness to take responsibility and also lead employees
  • Self-employed, reliable and careful working methods
  • Organizational and negotiation skills
  • High social, technical, methodological competence
  • German in word and letter and driving licence Cat. B are mandatory
  • Good IT knowledge in MS Office as well as an industry application such as Messerli or similar offer
  • Multifaceted activity
  • Established team
  • Lively and challenging environment
  • Attractive social benefits
  • Internal and external training opportunities
