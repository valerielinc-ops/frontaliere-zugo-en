Physiotherapist (20–60 %) Permanent employment or share of turnover possible Therapie Vera Borghans GmbH – `therapiezentrum Baar` - Inwil near Baar, ZG To strengthen our new, dynamic practice, we are looking for a motivated physiotherapist immediately or by agreement.

What we offer

  • Flexible employment models: permanent employment in wage service, hourly wages or on a self-employed basis with a percentage share in turnover
  • Flexible working hours in consultation with the team
  • A motivated, dynamic team at eye level
  • New building with modern, bright practice rooms in Inwil near Baar
  • Support in professional and personal development as well as in administrative tasks
  • Kita available in the house – ideal possibility for child care if necessary
  • A relaxed, relaxed and humorous working environment – open communication, a respectful approach and work-life balance are a matter of course and especially important to us
  • Bright, modern treatment rooms and a separate, spacious private gym for active rehabilitation and medical training.

What you bring

  • Completed training as a BSc physiotherapist (with SRK recognition or pre-check)
  • Professional experience is beneficial – but also committed career starters are welcome
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Team spirit, personal responsibility and an open being
  • Independent, reliable and patient-oriented working method
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Completed further training in manual therapy, Dry Needling and/or sports physiotherapy, are desired – if there is no qualification, interest in further training is advantageous.

About us

The Baar Therapy Center stands for personal and holistic therapy. In our practice, we treat people of all ages with heart, expertise and high quality standards. We focus on people – both our patients and our employees. Knowledge and focus of our practice are among others in manual therapy, in Dry Needling, in osteopathy and in orthomolecular therapy. We would like to expand and strengthen this offer in the future with sports physiotherapy, manual therapy and / or lymphatic drainage (infrastructure is available). Interested?
