Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position At Roche and within RDCH, we create a work culture based on trust, mutual appreciation and open feedback culture. Consistent compliance with all Group standards and the process specifications in the Integrated Management System (IMS) forms the binding basis of our work. In addition to the continuous optimization of workflows to increase quality and efficiency, disciplined data management is at the heart of our actions. All employees actively contribute to strategic priorities, assume responsibility in overarching projects and jointly promote continuous development along clearly defined frameworks.

The possibility

  • Scheduling and controlling service activities, internal and external inquiries and complaints, including forwarding complaints to expert support.
  • Long-term resource and deployment planning in close coordination with stakeholders for maintenance, instrument modifications, installations and external service.
  • Reliable administration and essential input for the absence, holiday and spare planning of the Field Service Representatives (FSR).
  • Management and support of telephone systems as well as the central online support platform.
  • First point of contact for Post Market Quality Service topics and central link between customers, customer service and sales.
  • Targeted use of CRM and digital systems for controlling all services as well as for continuous monitoring of defined service KPIs.
  • Continuous optimization of service processes from an entrepreneurial point of view in accordance with stakeholders as well as active participation in overarching projects.

Who you are

  • Strong organizational talent and forward-looking planning competence for the targeted scheduling of service activities, maintenance and installations as well as for the optimal coordination of internal and external resources.
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in the diagnostic environment (in the technical or application field), ideally with sound practice in point-of-care diagnostics (near patient care).
  • Strong customer and solution orientation as a central, communication-strong link between customers, customer service, sales and expert support with the goal of highest customer satisfaction
  • High digital affinity and discipline in data management for the accomplished use of CRM, telephone and support platforms as well as ensuring excellent data quality and continuous KPI tracking
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and continuous optimization in order to continuously develop service processes, consistently adhere to standardized processes (IMS) and use resources economically
  • Reliability, personal responsibility and decision-making power in an empowering environment, coupled with the willingness to actively participate in overarching projects
  • Lived team and feedback culture with an appreciative, transparent communication at eye level as well as the motivation for continuous personal development
  • Very good communication skills in word and writing in both German and English round off your profile ideally.
