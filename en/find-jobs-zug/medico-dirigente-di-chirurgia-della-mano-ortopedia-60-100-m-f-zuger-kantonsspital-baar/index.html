Full description

The Zug Canton Hospital serves the health of the population of the Canton of Zug and the surrounding regions. Around 1,200 employees, 180 of them in education and training, work in a wide range of fields. Every year we treat and care for more than 11,500 inpatient and over 70,000 outpatients. In the interdisciplinary emergency center, more than 27,500 people are treated annually. In our clinic for orthopaedics and traumatology, about 2,500 procedures are performed per year and about 2,000 inpatients and 9,000 outpatients are treated. The clinic is divided into subspecialized teams: shoulder and elbow, hand and wrist, hip, knee, foot and ankle, and traumatology. Spinal surgery is offered in cooperation with a center hospital. The Clinic of Orthopaedics and Traumatology has a qualification for the specialist title Orthopaedic Surgery and Traumatology of the musculoskeletal system in category B1. For our clinic for orthopaedics and traumatology we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by appointment a senior hand surgery / orthopaedics 60 - 100% (m/f) What you do with us You take responsibility for our hand surgery and orthopaedic patients - from the first encounter to the completion of the treatment.

  • You independently conduct an outpatient hand surgery session on 3 days (60%)
  • If desired, it is possible to conduct an orthopaedic consultation on a further 2 days per week.
  • They treat our patients conservatively and holistically
  • If qualified and interested, there is also the possibility of combined conservative and surgical hand surgery.
  • Together with us, you shape the further development of hand surgery and contribute your own ideas and strengths. What is important to us We are not looking for a perfect biography. We are looking for a personality that works carefully, takes responsibility and suits us humanly. For an operative activity, you have a specialist title in hand surgery and/or orthopaedic surgery and traumatology of the musculoskeletal system. For a purely conservative activity, you have many years of experience in speaking hours in the field of the musculoskeletal system. A specialist title is not a prerequisite for this. They meet patients and employees with empathy, respect and appreciation. They are loyal and team-oriented and keep track even when it becomes demanding. They are open, cooperative and flexible – and enjoy sharing knowledge, developing the team and shaping new paths together. Good medicine arises where professional competence and humanity come together. What we offer you With us, you do not expect a workplace where you simply fulfill a function. You will become part of a small, collegial, dedicated hand surgical team, which works closely with different disciplines such as orthopaedics, rheumatology, neurology, etc. She expects:
  • a broad spectrum of elective and traumatological hand surgery
  • a versatile activity with its own creative scope
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with the other clinics
  • regular internal training
  • Support and opportunities for external training
  • No services
  • Contemporary employment conditions and very good social benefits
  • a workplace in a central location, in the immediate vicinity of the Baar station and the motorway connection Perhaps we are looking for you. If you live hand surgery with expertise, curiosity and humanity and want to make a difference together, we look forward to meeting you. Please send your electronic application (via applicant link) including updated operation catalogue to Daniela Burri, Head of Human Resources if you are interested in a combined job.
