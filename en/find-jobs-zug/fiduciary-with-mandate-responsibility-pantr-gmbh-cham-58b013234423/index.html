Full description

For a renowned, service-oriented trust and auditing company with national and international customers, we are looking for a committed and customer-oriented personality as a trustee:in / mandate manager:in. In a dynamic environment with flat hierarchies, you gradually take over the holistic support of your own customer portfolio, actively shape processes and benefit from targeted professional and financial further education. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Mandate support: Independent management and holistic support of an own national and international customer portfolio – with gradual assumption of responsibility depending on experience.
  • Financial statements & VAT: Preparation and review of interim and annual accounts and preparation of VAT accounts.
  • Taxation: Processing and auditing of tax returns for natural and legal persons.
  • Customer advice: Competent professional advice and support of clients in all fiduciary and financial matters.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: Completed further training in escrow or accounting (ideally with a federal certificate) or currently on the way there.
  • Experience: In-depth professional experience in trust or in finance and accounting.
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing for the care of international mandates.
  • Personality: pleasure in direct customer contact, entrepreneurial and networked thinking as well as an independent, structured and responsible way of working.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Culture & Leadership: A collegial, helpful team, flat hierarchies, short decision paths and supervisors who take time for your questions.
  • Development & perspective: Long-term perspective in a stable company with generous financial and professional support for further education.
  • Workplace & Goodies: Modern workspace with the best public transport and road connections, excellent social benefits and additional benefits such as a free garage space, fruit and drinks.
