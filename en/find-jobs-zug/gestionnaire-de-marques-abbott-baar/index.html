Original description

Brand Manager for Abbott Diabetes Care, office Baar

Primary Objective of Position

  • To develop marketing strategies, using all elements of the marketing mix, including brand development with the aim of increasing sales.
  • Have a broad and deep product knowledge and be responsible for product

trainings as appropriate.

Major Accountabilities

  • To localize global/EMEAP marketing strategy to match the position of the brand in Switzerland and maximize brand potential.
  • Input into developing plan to market product through the promotional campaigns, utilising the full marketing mix as appropriate.
  • Develop or localize multichannel marketing projects in accordance with relevant functions.
  • Budget planning and tracking for ensuring promotional campaigns are within

agreed spending limits and A&P reporting is accurate, incl. accruals, for owned projects.

  • Assist in preparing plan, LBE and LRP & S&OP as required.
  • Liaising closely and influencing the sales management, brand team and sales team to ensure promotional campaigns are developed on strategy and executed effectively.
  • Prepare, organize and conduct trainings to ensure sales teams are well trained and able to execute strategies effectively.
  • Liaising closely with all support functions (including Medical, Regulatory, Customer Services, Manufacturing, External Agencies and Global/EMEA Marketing).
  • Collating Competitor Intelligence from the market.
  • Reporting Marketing KPIs as deemed necessary by the ACH management team and Global/EMEAP Marketing.
  • Analyze KPIs to optimize campaigns and spendings/ROI
  • Creating or localizing marketing material: ensure marketing material is developed, approved and produced according to branding guidelines and approval processes.
  • Collaborating with external vendors and stakeholders regarding quotes,

negotiations, contracts, POs.

  • Working with relevant leaders and managers as well as peers to create and track in-country marketing plans and to communicate to the commercial organisation as appropirate.
  • Ensures that all activities are performed in compliance with quality system requirements

Qualifications

  • University degree in Marketing or Business or relevant experience
  • Languages: German (native), French (Good), English (good). Italian as a big plus.
  • At least 5 years Pharmaceutical and/or Medical Device Industry experience
  • Minimum 3 years in Marketing and/or Sales including working with

advertising/creative agencies

  • Experience of working with MS Office
  • Customer facing and/or sales experience in healthcare environment
  • Competent use of computers (spreadsheets, graphics package) incl. website

management (e.g. AEM) and basic graphics design knowledge

  • Presentation, communication and motivational skills
  • Ability to build and maintain excellent level of product, sales and competition

knowledge
