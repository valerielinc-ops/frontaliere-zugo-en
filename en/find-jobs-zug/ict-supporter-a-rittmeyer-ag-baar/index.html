Full description

As a BRUGG group company, the BRUGG IT department of Rittmeyer offers infrastructure services for the entire international group of companies. This benefits three organizations with a total of 1600 end users. The central service desk is the first point of contact for our users and interface to the remaining IT disciplines. You are the first point of contact for ICT questions and ensure that our employees can work reliably and efficiently in everyday work. With your technical know-how, your hands-on mentality and your solution-oriented approach, you actively tackle challenges and find pragmatic solutions. ICT Supporter (a) Baar, Switzerland 80-100% Your area of 1st and 2nd-level support for the entire BRUGG Group reception, initial analysis and processing of fault messages in the ticket system initial support and error analysis via telephone and remote setup, configuration and software installation of devices via Microsoft Intune as well as support of the entire IMACD process on-site support at our locations – mainly in Baar, if necessary also in Brugg, Birr and Kleindöttingen Creation and maintenance of documentation, instructions, knowledge base articles and checklists Training and support of our users What you bring successfully completed training as computer scientist EFZ, ICT specialist card or a comparable qualification Well-founded knowledge in Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Azure AD/Entra ID, Windows operating systems and network technologies Experience in IT support as well as in-depth knowledge in client management Joy of telephone and personal customer contact as well as a service-oriented support hands-on mentality: You are a maker, actively tackle challenges and find solutions Teamplayer with independent, service- and solution-oriented working methods Very good German and good English skills in word and writing Driver's license category B What we offer you What we Our flat hierarchy and short decision paths characterize us and bring us quickly to our goals.
