Full description

In the role as an employee*in buffet/cass/office, you contribute to offering our customers a positive experience! You are responsible for the preparation and the appealing presentation of our buffet. With your commitment, you make sure that our clientele feels comfortable.

Mansioni

  • Service of the customer service in compliance with the cash rules and the issue of customer information
  • buffet construction and care
  • Securing Mise en Place
  • Various cleaning work
  • Working with the Office

Requisiti

  • First professional experience: in catering and/or on sale
  • Completed basic or advanced training: beneficial in gastronomy
  • High quality and responsibility
  • Service-oriented personality with host flair
  • Reliable and accurate operation (including good HACCP knowledge)
  • Self-employment and flexibility
  • German (very good knowledge)
