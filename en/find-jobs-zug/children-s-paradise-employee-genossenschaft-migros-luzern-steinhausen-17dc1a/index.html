Full description

Are you caring, responsible and happy to care for young children? Even with a larger group of children, do you keep track? Then we look forward to your application for our children’s paradise Purzelbaum in the shopping center Zugerland Steinhausen.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Lucerne, shopping centre Zugerland Steinhausen, Hinterbergstrasse 40, 6312 Steinhausen

Mansioni

  • You look after the children of our customers during the children's paradise opening hours
  • You play, craft and paint with the kids
  • You ensure cleanliness and order in the children's paradise
  • You are creative and help with the decoration of the children’s paradise

Requirements

  • Professional experience is beneficial: when dealing with children
  • Completed initial or continuing education: training in the field of care beneficial
  • You are a well-placed and sensitive person
  • You are creative, playful and responsible
  • You are flexible regarding working hours and ready to help out with representatives
  • You live in the region
  • German (very good knowledge)

  • Part of the Migros Group: As part of the Migros Group, you benefit from stable structures, diverse career opportunities and attractive benefits
  • Cumulus points: You collect additional Cumulus points in the Food and Non Food section
  • Club School Migros: We participate in club school courses up to max. CHF 1000.– (at 100% fee)

  • Leisure and cultural offers: We offer various discounts and free tickets in the area of leisure and culture

Contatto

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
