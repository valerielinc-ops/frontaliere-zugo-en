The Position The security department is responsible for the physical security of the Rotkreuz site, implements the global Roche security requirement and operates an alarm center, the security service and the access office in addition to the regular tasks of a security department.

The possibility You are a member of the security team within the Safety, Security, Health & Environment (SHE) department, work in a highly motivated independent team with security experts and report to the head of the security department.

Together with your teammate:innen you implement the security requirements of the location and have interfaces to our 4'200 location users:innen. You work in a team of proven experts.

As Head of Access Management & Security Technology, you are responsible for the following topics:

  • You manage the specialist office Access with 1,000 readers and 120 zone-responsible contacts: inside. You are responsible for all processes, technical facilities and IT/software systems used and their interfaces in the area of access management.
  • As a central contact person, you are one of the master users of the access system, coordinate it and ensure smooth operation. Operationally, the access management is managed by two employees from your team.
  • You are the security contact: in construction projects and are responsible for the planning and implementation of all security requirements of all systems and facilities of the security department. You work closely with the safety planners and ensure quality assurance.
  • You drive the digitalization and simplification of the security landscape, analyze the system architecture, define the technological roadmap and represent the security requirements in IT projects as a central contact person.
  • You take over the conceptual and technical input as well as examination of the door compartment planning and ensure its quality-appropriate implementation.
  • You proactively maintain close relationships with internal and external stakeholders and consider their needs.
  • You actively shape your area of responsibility, identify fields of action and develop strategies.
  • You actively seek the exchange with business partners, understand their needs and at the same time convincingly represent your own solutions and recommendations.
  • You will be able to efficiently manage complex security processes and ensure that daily operational requirements are met smoothly. You make informed decisions in implementation and monitor operational implementation to ensure the highest safety standards.

Who you are

  • Ideally, you will bring at least 5 years of experience in a comparable position, preferably in a large company or in an international environment.
  • You have a strong IT understanding and technical understanding for the planning and operation of complex digital systems (access control systems, bathroom handling systems locking technology).
  • Further training in the area of safety planning, project management of safety systems and experience in door management are advantageous.
  • You feel comfortable in a dynamic, lively environment and keep a cool head even during labor-intensive phases, prioritize efficiently and always keep track. You will use your diplomatic skills to manage the tension between security requirements and the needs of our stakeholders.
  • You drive change forward with energy: You question existing things constructively, bring fresh ideas and actively shape the area of security.
  • As an independent and structured personality, you develop complex content independently, make employees fit for handling in the access system and act as a solution-oriented:r Impulsegeber:in. You negotiate skillfully with different stakeholders and make decisions with a high level of business understanding.
  • You are a:e team player who relies on the strength of the collective and is open to different perspectives. You bring a high degree of self-reflection and distinguish yourself by the claim to constantly develop.
  • German as a mother tongue as well as very good oral and written English skills (at least level B2).
