Full description

Mission Your future playing field You will become part of the store in Baar. Located in the lively Zugerland shopping center and surrounded by Lake Zug and the mountains of central Switzerland, we accompany an extremely sports-loving clientele - from the after-work trail to the weekend trip to the mountains. Become part of an adventure that is constantly growing Decathlon in Switzerland is a success story that began in Neuchâtel in 2017. Since then, we have opened around ten branches every year and now have 50 sales outlets. And we don't plan on stopping there! Our expansion continues, particularly in German-speaking Switzerland, and offers career opportunities nationwide. And now about your job: Are you looking for a job where you don't just stand behind a workbench and tighten screws? Do you want to make decisions and be in direct contact with athletes? Do you dream of combining your technical expertise with real customer service? Then this position is just right for you. Why?

  • Your workshop is your own business: At Decathlon, we like to give our teammates responsibility. You don't just carry out repairs: you have to anticipate periods of high utilization, manage and guarantee the accuracy of your spare parts warehouse, and keep an eye on your key performance indicators (growth, repair rate, customer satisfaction).
  • You are the link between technology and people: the customer is the focus. Whether it's making an accurate diagnosis, offering the right service selection (second life, maintenance) or simply reassuring athletes about their equipment – you're there. Your technical expertise is only worth something if it's shared with a smile.
  • You give trust and get it back: Do you have an idea to improve the efficiency of the workshop or reduce our processing times? You have the right to try things, make mistakes and learn from them. You grow by taking the initiative. In addition, this position is an excellent stepping stone: if you are ambitious, there are many development opportunities waiting for you (workshop manager, regional technician...). This job is NOT for you if...
  • You ONLY want to repair bicycles (with us, a workshop is versatile: skis, fitness, flock printing/personalization, etc. Even though bicycles make up the majority of our repairs).
  • You are ONLY interested in pure technology and you are bored with customer contact.
  • You like "top-down" structures and wait for others to make decisions for you.
  • You're not willing to work on Saturdays (because that's the time when customers need us the most). What does a workshop technician do? Your mission is two-pronged: to ensure the impeccable quality of your technical services while guaranteeing an exceptional customer experience. On a day-to-day basis, your responsibilities are divided into two main areas: 1. Diagnosis and customer relationship:
  • To welcome customers, listen to their needs and make a reliable and precise diagnosis.
  • Apply the standards of workshop acceptance and follow the diagnostic procedures.
  • Sell our products and services (physical and digital) and systematically offer the right range of circular economy services.
  • Propose a cost estimate and deadline adapted to the needs of the athletes.
  • The checkout and handover of the material, including care tips, is taken care of.
  • Your success indicators (KPIs): % of correct diagnoses, % share of local order acceptance (PEC), growth in the number of procedures (PEC), average shopping cart paid. 2. Technical and operational services:
  • Carry out repair, maintenance and overhaul work in accordance with Decathlon safety standards.
  • Ensure the personalisation of products (flock printing, engraving...).
  • Guarantee the correctness of your spare parts warehouse and order what is missing.
  • Maintain a safe, clean, and secure work environment.
  • Your success indicators (KPIs): repair rate, local turnaround time, inventory accuracy, workshop efficiency, GMV growth in personalization and awards, NPS (Net Promoter Score) personalization. Profile What we expect from you:
  • You have technical training (such as EFZ, EBA) and demonstrable experience in mechanics (ideally in the two-wheeler sector).
  • You are a truly sports enthusiast and active person.
  • You are solution-oriented: If there is a mechanical problem or a dissatisfied customer, you will always find a way to clarify things.
  • You are organized, thorough, and know how to handle stressful situations with emotional intelligence.
  • You have a perfect command of German. Good English/French skills are a big plus. What we offer you: (Small note: The amounts and benefits mentioned below are for a 100% workload and are calculated on a pro rata basis for a lower level of employment!). Below we list the most important benefits.
  • Salary and bonus: We offer you a fixed monthly gross salary between 4,300 CHF and 5,600 CHF, paid in 13 months' wages. In addition, there is a monthly bonus (up to 10% of your gross salary) directly linked to the collective performance of the store. Not forgetting the annual profit-sharing: it is not guaranteed, but we do everything we can to achieve it throughout the year!
  • Employee shares: Once a year, you can become a shareholder of the company. This is an excellent investment: the value of the stock has never been negative since Decathlon was founded 50 years ago!
  • Holidays: Because relaxation is part of performance, you benefit from 5 weeks of paid holiday per year.
  • Passion for sport: we fully support your sporting activity! Benefit from a 25% discount on your Decathlon purchases (and even an additional 25% discount if you buy a soft mobility product for your commute). We'll also give you a voucher worth 225 CHF/year for your sports or fitness subscription.
  • Your development: With us, you will never stop learning thanks to our hundreds of training courses. In addition, you will have a special exchange with your leader every month to talk about your goals and wishes.
  • Mobility support: (ONLY ADDED FOR CERTAIN REGIONS WITH A SKILL SHORTAGE: Stans, Chur etc.) We are looking for the best talents and are ready to support them! If you have to move for this job, a mobility package can be put together. To make your life easier, you can enlist the help of an external relocation agency to find your new apartment and get support with administrative steps. Depending on your personal situation (number of kilometers, children, etc.), you may also be granted a bonus and the coverage of certain costs. Your career: For us, further development is not a myth Are you starting out in our workshop? You can take over the workshop management, develop towards sales as a department manager or even become a trainer. The possibilities are diverse! With thousands of possible careers in Switzerland and abroad, your career is in your hands. Joining Decathlon means becoming part of a global network where real development opportunities exist. The application process: How do we get to know each other? We rely on an objective and human assessment. These are the steps to become part of our team:
  • Your application: We do not require a letter of motivation. Simply send your CV and answer our short questionnaire.
  • The assessment: As soon as you apply, you will receive an email from AssessFirst (remember to check your spam folder!). It is a questionnaire of maximum 30 minutes that will help us get to know your personality and soft skills. Don't worry, it's not AI that sorts your CV: our teams read each application personally and take care of the pre-selection! The real plus? You will receive your results immediately, a super bonus that will help you in your research or future interviews.
  • Exchange on "Cultural Fit" (via video): An initial conversation with the Talent Acquisition team to check if we share the same values. You can read about Decathlon's values here.
  • The technical interview (in store): Meet your future manager to get specific: We talk about your future playing field and your technical skills. (Sometimes a final step is added with the store management). Practical info:
  • Start of the job: immediately or by appointment Ready to take on the challenge? Then join us!

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zug's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zug's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
