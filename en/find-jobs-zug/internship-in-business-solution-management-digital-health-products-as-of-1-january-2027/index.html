The Position As a Business Solution Management - Digital Healthcare Products Intern at our Rotkreuz site, you will join Roche Information Solutions (RIS) to support the development and delivery of innovative digital solutions for lab and healthcare professionals. Digitalization is reshaping healthcare, and in this role, you will work within a collaborative, international environment to drive project execution, analyze market trends, and support day-to-day operations that ultimately improve patient care worldwide.

The Opportunity

  • You assist a solution leader in planning, coordinating, and executing cross-functional solution projects, creating project plans, preparing workshops, drafting minutes, and tracking action items with stakeholders.
  • You conduct market research and analyze data trends in digital health - focusing on diabetes, cardiometabolic, oncology, and neurology - to provide insights for business decisions and affiliate team growth.
  • You support the team in daily operational activities, including organizing internal team meetings, managing events, and tracking budgets.

Who You Are

  • You are currently enrolled in or have completed a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree within the last 12 months prior to the internship start date, preferably in field of Business Administration, Business Management, International Business, or Business Economics.
  • You bring first internship experience in a corporate environment.
  • Your professional written and spoken English enables effective communication across global teams. German is nice to have.
  • You possess a proactive, well-organized mindset accompanied by strong interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Familiarity with Microsoft Office, the Google Suite (Google Docs, Sheets, Slides) and virtual collaboration tools like Lucidspark or Monday is preferred. Miro, Trello are nice to have.
  • Familiarity with Large Language Models (such as ChatGPT) is considered an asset.
  • Understanding of the healthcare sector and systems, including diagnostics, pharma, health insurance, and digital health is preferred.

Application Process

  • Please upload your CV (in English), a motivation letter with your application, and a certificate of enrolment (if you are currently studying) or your degree certificate (if you have already completed your studies).
  • Non-EU/Non-EFTA citizens must attach a certificate from their university stating that the internship is mandatory, specifying the required duration, and maintaining continuous enrolment for the entire duration of the internship.

Additional Information

  • Start Date & Duration: The preferred start date for this internship is January 2027 for 12 months in Rotkreuz or upon discussion.
  • Thesis Note: Please note that the internship cannot be combined with a university thesis.
