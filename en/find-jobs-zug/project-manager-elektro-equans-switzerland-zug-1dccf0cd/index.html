Full description

Are you experienced in project management and would like to develop professionally and personally? Then this position offers you an exciting perspective. You start with us as a project manager: in electric in our service department in Zug and take responsibility for your projects, our customers and the employees involved. At the same time, we are already thinking about the future: As part of our succession planning, you have the prospect of developing further in the medium term to manage the train section. For this, you will gradually be introduced to additional leadership and result responsibility.

You can move that with us.

  • You will independently manage and coordinate service and electrical projects from planning to successful completion.
  • You are responsible for the technical, scheduled and economical handling of your projects.
  • You create offers, calculations and billings and keep an eye on costs, deadlines and quality.
  • You look after our existing customers and continue to develop long-term customer relationships.
  • You recognize additional customer needs and actively support the acquisition of new orders.
  • You coordinate the work of the employees and ensure efficient project management.
  • You ensure compliance with our quality and safety standards.
  • You bring your own ideas and actively contribute to the further development of the train section.
  • With a view to the planned succession, you will gradually assume additional management, customer and earnings responsibility.

That's what makes you.

  • Completed basic training as an electrical installer:EFZ or equivalent electrotechnical training
  • Further education in the field of electrical project management or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in project management in the electrical sector
  • Experience in the coordination of employees and interest in taking on additional management responsibility in the future
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and a good understanding of economic connections
  • Delight in the support and further development of customers as well as a pronounced service concept
  • Organizational talent and an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strength of communication and a secure and binding attitude towards customers and employees
  • Motivation to develop yourself professionally and personally and to assume responsibility for a sub-area in the medium term
  • Category B driving licence

That's what we offer you.

With us you do not just take on a classic project manager function. We offer you the opportunity to prepare your next professional step.

  • You will receive:
  • a varied and responsible job as a project manager:in
  • a concrete development perspective as part of our succession planning
  • the opportunity to gradually assume additional management and result responsibility
  • A lot of personal responsibility and scope for design
  • An experienced and collegial environment
  • Exciting customers and projects in the Zug region
  • Long-term development and career opportunities within the company
