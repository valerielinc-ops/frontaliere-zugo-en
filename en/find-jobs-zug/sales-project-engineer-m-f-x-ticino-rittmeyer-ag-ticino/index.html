Full description

Control systems, control systems and measurement technology from Rittmeyer are the basis for innovative solutions in energy and water supplies, hydropower plants and waste disposal plants worldwide. As an engineering and service company with the highest quality standards, Rittmeyer specializes in software and system integration for turnkey systems in the infrastructure sector. For our Products team, we are looking for a person who actively shapes and develops our measurement technology products and solutions. Our solutions are used worldwide, especially in hydropower plants and water supply, and enable high-precision and robust measurements of flow, pressure, levels and angles and positions of mechanical shut-off devices. Do you want to take responsibility for products where quality, precision and long-term reliability are crucial? Do you want to accompany the solutions from design to production and application to customers? We look forward to your application! Solution Owner Measuring Products Baar, Switzerland 80-100% You take responsibility for our high-precision measurement solutions in the areas of flow, level, angle and position measurement You actively shape the further development of our solutions – from the idea to prototypes and tests to later application at the customer You are the central contact person for metrology and bring your expertise in technical issues, customer-specific solutions and challenging projects You work closely with product development, product management, engineering and international sales and can incorporate your ideas directly into the solutions You accompany prototype construction, verification, quality assurance and customer returns and ensure that our solutions work reliably and practically You develop technical documentation and application concepts, pass on your knowledge and support internal and external partners

  • What you bring You have completed training in mechatronics, mechanical engineering or electrical engineering and have at least 5 years of professional experience in your field of expertise You have a strong affinity for electromechanics, control and measurement technology as well as physics and ideally have experience in the development and further development of technical solutions You understand technical relationships across system and product boundaries and can translate customer requirements, market needs and technological developments into practical solutions Knowledge in manufacturing and production technology or prototype construction as well as experience in the coordination of interdisciplinary teams are advantageous You work independently, carefully and solution-oriented, are curious and ready to build up long-term sound industry and product know-how You have a good understanding of business value, costs, quality and technical feasibility and confidently communicate in German and English
