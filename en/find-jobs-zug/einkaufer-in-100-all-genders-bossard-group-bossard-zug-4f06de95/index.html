Junior Controller 80 - 100 % (all genders), Bossard Group

You want to analyze not only figures but also actively shape the future of reporting and financial processes? As part of our BEMEA Finance team, you support the management of Bossard Europe, Middle East & Africa (BEMEA) in achieving strategic and operational goals. You are moving at the interface of controlling, data analysis and digitalization and independently take on a variety of tasks and projects in an international environment.

Your Tasks

  • Preparation of analyses, key figures, and management reports as a basis for the management of BEMEA
  • Participation in monthly, half-yearly and annual financial statements and support for the Group reporting according to Swiss GAAP FER
  • Implementation of financial reviews, deviation analyses and cooperation in the internal control system (ICS) and risk management
  • Support and advice for our international subsidiaries on financial and business issues
  • Participation in international and cross-sectoral projects and acceptance of subproject responsibility
  • Further development and automation of reporting, analysis and controlling processes with a focus on digitalization, data processing and business analytics

Your requirement profile

  • Completed business studies (FH/University) with focus on Accounting, Finance or Controlling
  • Initial professional experience in the industrial environment and sound knowledge of financial relations and balance sheet security
  • High IT-A efficiency and enthusiasm for data processing, business analytics, digitalization and process automation
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office (especially Excel and PowerPoint); experience with Power BI, Power Apps and Microsoft Dynamics 365 advantage
  • German and English are in good hands in word and writing; other languages are advantageous
  • Analytic, curious and responsible personality with independent working methods, high willingness to learn and willingness to travel within Europe (approx. 10-20 %)
