Full description

Their tasks

  • Introduction and further development of outpatient tariff models such as TARDOC and outpatient packages
  • Support of clinics and departments with questions about the correct application of tariffs and central contact person for ambulatory hospital tariffs
  • Translation of tariff requirements into clear technical and technical specifications for our billing and IT systems
  • Participation in projects

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in the Swiss healthcare system, ideally in tariff application, performance recording or billing
  • In-depth knowledge of outpatient hospital or practice tariffs (especially with the new outpatient tariff system consisting of outpatient flat rates / TARDOC)
  • Good IT affinity
  • Enjoy comprehensible preparation of complex tariff requirements and transfer them into practice
  • Experience with tariff projects, system adjustments or the optimization of billing processes is advantageous
  • Strength of communication and a professional approach to different internal and external stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Our offer

We offer you a varied and demanding job in a collegial team with flexible working hours and on request a relevant share of home office. They benefit from attractive employment conditions, such as more than 5 weeks holidays, various benefits, generous participation in professional training, and very good social benefits.
