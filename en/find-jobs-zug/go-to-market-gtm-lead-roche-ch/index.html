The Position

Summary

The GTM Lead role is expected to drive business growth and market success for RIS Digital Health Solutions by identifying new market opportunities, developing and executing go-to-market strategies, and translating deep market and customer insights into guidance for product and commercial teams, including exploring strategic partnerships. This position is the primary point of contact for global GTM strategy within the Product Core Team, focusing on sustainable value creation, enabling pilots, and generating relevant market intelligence, in alignment with the Product Lifecycle Framework.

The Opportunity

  • Accelerate market growth and digital adoptio n across priority geographies by empowering affiliates and fostering data-driven, collaborative decision-making.
  • Ensure full commercial readiness of the portfolio by embedding end-to-end GTM strategies (via GTM Blueprint) that connect value proposition, deployment, and capability building—driving coherent and scalable market launches.
  • Achieve strategic alignment and integration of Go-to-Market objectives within the specific Value Stream, ensuring shared accountability for commercial outcomes and digital product success.
  • Strengthen market competitiveness by enabling seamless collaboration between GTM, Product, and Value Stream teams, resulting in clear product positioning and consistent global execution.
  • Shape future market trajectories by identifying mid- to long-term growth levers and influencing roadmap decisions through systematic insights and customer feedback integration.
  • Provide actionable market intelligence and strategic guidance to Value Stream stakeholders, serving as the key interface between business strategy, product development, and commercialization.
  • Collaborate with the GTM Market Adoption team identifying foundational drivers to access markets in collaboration with the regions and selected third-party partnerships
