Full description

The Zug Canton Hospital serves the health of the population of the Canton of Zug and the surrounding regions. Around 1,200 employees, 180 of them in education and training, work in a wide range of fields. Every year, we treat and care for more than 11,500 inpatient and more than 70,000 outpatients. In the interdisciplinary emergency center, more than 27,500 people are treated annually. For our department of therapies we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by appointment a physiotherapist neurology / intensive care unit 80 - 100% (m/f) Your tasks

  • Professional implementation of individual patient-related physiotherapeutic treatments with targeted and diagnostic measures
  • Treatment of inpatient and outpatient patients mainly from neurology (incl. Stroke Unit), medicine and intensive care
  • Ensure interdisciplinary cooperation and communication within the therapy team and between the various professional groups
  • Experience in or Readiness for training physical therapy students your profile
  • Completed training as a physiotherapist
  • Additional training and professional experience in neurology and medicine
  • High patient orientation and social competence Our offer We offer you a varied and demanding job in a collegial team. They benefit from attractive employment conditions, such as more than 5 weeks of holidays, various benefits, generous participation in external professional training, weekly internal training and very good social benefits. Interested? We look forward to your electronic application to Gaby Jans, Stv. Head of Human Resources. Only direct applications will be considered.
