The Position The Marketing Technology organization drives the strategic architecture, evolution, and cross-functional alignment of our global digital tools and platforms. Working closely across Marketing, Sales, Support, and development teams, the department bridges business strategy with technical execution to ensure our technology stack meets evolving customer needs, supports organizational priorities, and delivers lasting commercial value. The Product Manager is the strategic architect of our marketing technology ecosystem, responsible for defining the product vision, long-term roadmap, and market alignment. Serving as the vital bridge between high-level business strategy and technical execution, this role ensures our Martech initiatives achieve maximum product-market fit, drive user adoption, and support the organization's broader financial and operational goals.

The Opportunity

  • Own the product vision and develop the long-term roadmap for marketing technology platforms, driving timely progression to address evolving customer needs and deliver affiliate commercial business impact.
  • Conduct in-depth research into Martech market trends, competitor landscapes, and customer requirements to shape an effective, forward-looking product strategy.
  • Partner with the Business Owner to synchronize product goals with organizational priorities and broader financial objectives.
  • Engage and collaborate with stakeholders across Marketing, Sales, and Support to ensure technology solutions are correctly positioned, provide measurable value to internal users, and meet organizational adoption goals.
  • Define and prioritize high-level initiatives to ensure execution aligns with overarching strategic goals.
  • Prioritize high-level initiatives to translate the strategic vision into actionable items for development, working effectively with the Product Owner and development team to deliver valuable product increments.
  • Establish the product strategy and key objectives that guide the development team’s tactical efforts and execution.

Who You Are

  • You bring 5–7 years of relevant experience with proven strategic product leadership to define a product strategy that aligns with complex organizational goals and business outcomes.
  • You possess strong Martech domain expertise, including a deep understanding of marketing technology, customer insights, and the competitive landscape.
  • You demonstrate collaborative stakeholder management experience, successfully working with leadership to translate business context into technical direction.
  • You are proficient in Agile frameworks, with a demonstrated ability to translate high-level vision and strategy into actionable development items.
  • You are skilled in market research and customer insights analysis to define what needs to be built and drive measurable product-market fit and value delivery efficiency.
