Full description

Create the shopping experience at Migros! As an all-rounder* in sales, you advise our customers and ensure a positive sales experience with full shelves and fresh products. Your commitment and personality make the difference.

Mansioni

  • Disposal, presentation and maintenance of goods
  • Quality, data and freshness control
  • Customer service in compliance with the cash regulations and providing customer information
  • Settlement of monetary transactions with the branch office
  • Assistance in branch logistics

Requirements

  • First professional experience is advantageous: in the retail trade
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • The joy of dealing with people
  • Self-employment and flexibility
  • Very good physical strength
  • Reliable and accurate working methods
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: As part of the Migros Group, you benefit from stable structures, diverse career opportunities and attractive benefits
  • Healthy work: Benefit from our company health management and attractive insurance benefits
  • Handy subscription: Employees receive highly discounted conditions for the private Handy subscription incl. Family and friends
  • Occupational pension fund: Migros Pension Fund offers attractive pension fund services with high employee participation
  • Cumulus Points: You collect additional Cumulus Points in the Food and Non Food section
  • Club School Migros: We participate in club school courses up to max. CHF 1000.– (at 100% fee)
  • Leisure and cultural offers: We offer various discounts and free tickets in the area of leisure and culture
  • Sports and fitness offers: With us you get discounted fitness subscriptions in the Activ Fitness and Migros Fitness Park
