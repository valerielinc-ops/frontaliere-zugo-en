Full description

Cham Swiss Properties is a real estate company based in Cham (ZG). We focus on the value-creating development of attractive living and working spaces at central locations with good public transport connections in Switzerland. Our team is building and expanding a high-quality, sustainable and diversified real estate portfolio. The skills of the approximately 70 employees of Cham Swiss Properties comprise the entire value chain of the real estate cycle. Cham Swiss Properties is listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange SIX Swiss Exchange. In order to successfully advance the upcoming construction projects of our innovative site developments in German-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for responsible, energetic and experienced personalities as project managers to expand our realization team immediately or by agreement: in client representatives (80–100%)

Their tasks

  • Controlling the planning and execution of large, complex new and conversion projects in the context of complex area developments, heavy in the area of housing, supplemented by office and commercial buildings
  • Managing external planners, construction and total contractors
  • Foresighted project management from the preliminary project to and with the handover of the construction projects
  • Ensuring the client-side project goals regarding costs, deadlines and quality

Your profile

  • University or university of applied sciences degree with specific further education, e.g. in planning and construction management and/or in construction economics
  • Comprehensive planning and realization experience of large construction and civil engineering projects at a construction management / general planner company or a total company
  • Several years of experience as a client representative with sound customer skills
  • Responsibility, independence and reliability
  • Flexibility and entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Secure appearance, assertiveness and negotiating skills
  • Good expression skills in words and writing as well as good MS-Office skills

We offer

  • An exciting and future-oriented working environment
  • Individual promotion and training support
  • Modern workplaces with ergonomic equipment and small office units
  • Home office opportunities and flexible working conditions
  • Regular team events and a pleasant working atmosphere
  • Free parking for all employees
