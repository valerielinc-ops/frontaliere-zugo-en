Full description

The Zug Canton Hospital serves the health of the population of the Canton of Zug and the surrounding regions. Around 1,200 employees, 180 of them in education and training, work in a wide range of fields. Every year we treat and care for more than 11,500 inpatient patients and more than 70,000 outpatients. In the interdisciplinary emergency center, more than 27,500 people are treated annually. To strengthen our radiology team, we are looking for a radiology specialist HF 80 – 100% as of January 1, 2027 or by appointment Their tasks

  • Conducting radiological examinations with state-of-the-art MR and CT scanners
  • Collaboration in angiography, conventional X-ray, mammography and sonography
  • Co-responsibility for the examination quality as well as responsibility for the well-being of our patients
  • Work assignments for core working hours as well as night and weekend services – flexibility and teamwork are a matter of course for us Your profile
  • Completed training as radiology specialist HF
  • Interest and pleasure in modern medical technology
  • Commitment and willingness to work as an all-rounder
  • Re-enters, professionals and experienced professionals are welcome Our offer
  • Modern radiology department with highly developed infrastructure
  • Work with innovative photon counting detector technology
  • Use of Siemens NAEOTOM Alpha.Pro and Siemens SOMATOM X.ceed for advanced CT imaging
  • Wide range of services:
  • Magnetic resonance imaging (3.0T GE, 1.5T Siemens)
  • Interventional radiology including angiography (Philips)
  • Digital X-ray diagnostics (DR)
  • Mammography (including CEM and VAB)
  • Sonography interested? Then apply now and actively shape the future of our radiology – we look forward to your electronic application (via applicant link) to Gaby Jans, Stv.
