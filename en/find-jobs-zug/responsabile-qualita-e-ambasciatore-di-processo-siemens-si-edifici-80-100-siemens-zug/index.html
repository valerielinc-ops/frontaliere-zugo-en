Role Overview You will serve as a value-adding orchestrator, responsible for defining and continuously enhancing quality throughout the entire product life cycle of a product line within the Automation Products portfolio Contribute and improve activities across the entire product life cycle, promoting effective collaboration among Product Owners, Project Management, Supplier Quality Management, Product Development, Manufacturing, Procurement, and Logistics Support the assurance of compliance with legal, regulatory, and customer requirements for the defined product lines, contributing to high product quality and reliability Location: this position will be based in Zug/Switzerland Your new responsibilities

  • Ensure, evaluate, and monitor the implementation of Built-in Quality principles, quality methods, and risk management practices across processes, projects, and product development activities — including 8D, Root Cause Analysis (RCA), Quality Gates, and FMEA
  • Drive continuous improvement, lessons learned, and the sharing of best practices to strengthen quality culture and organizational learning across the portfolio
  • Coach and train project team members in process application and specific quality areas
  • Act as Process Ambassador by driving process maturity, ensuring process adherence, and fostering efficient and harmonized ways of working across the product portfolio
  • Develop and adapt communication approaches to effectively convey quality-related topics internally and to external customers
  • Analyze customer quality requirements, feedback, and satisfaction to support continuous improvement and meet customer expectations
  • Execute internal assessments and audits to enhance transparency, effectiveness, and efficiency in internal project (or product) development, thereby driving measurable business impact
  • Collaborate with the QM network and business accountable units (e.g. Product Management, R&D, Procurement, Manufacturing etc.) to achieve quality business goals Your Profile
  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering
  • Extended knowledge of quality management and prevention oriented quality methods
  • Several years of experience in a similar role
  • Experience in cross-functional stakeholder management and change management
  • Experience working in Agile and SAFe development frameworks to support adaptive and value-driven product development
  • Analytical thinking, well organized person with good communication and intercultural skills
  • Knowledge of AI applications in Quality Management, including Quality Agent creation, is a plus
  • Fluent in English and a good level of German (verbally and in writing) desired Employee benefits
