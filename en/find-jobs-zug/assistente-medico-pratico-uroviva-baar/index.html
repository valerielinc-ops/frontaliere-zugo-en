Full description

Uroviva stands for a modern medical network and includes a special clinic for urology, eleven urological practices and an andrology center. With approximately 70,000 consultations each year in primary, special and emergency care and more than 5,000 operations of all severity levels, Uroviva is an important provider of urological services in Switzerland. For our practice in Baar (canton train) we are looking for an engaged person immediately or by agreement Medical / Practical Assistant / 100%

Their tasks

  • Organisation and preparation of consultation hours
  • Care of our patients
  • Self-conducting blood sampling, urine analysis and injections
  • Assistance in urological examinations and minor procedures (e.g. cystoscopies, prostate biopsies, vasectomies)
  • Performing Bladder Instillations and Catheter Changes
  • Ensuring hygiene and order in treatment rooms
  • Reception and administration services (scheduling of appointments, telephone, patient reception, reports)
  • Management of consumables

Your profile

  • Completed training as MPA EFZ or specialist health EFZ
  • Professional experience is beneficial, but not mandatory
  • Apprenticeship graduates and motivated lateral entrants with further training in office hours assistance are also welcome.
  • Friendly, resilient and reliable personality
  • Joy in dealing with people and in teamwork
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
  • Knowledge of Axenita is an advantage
