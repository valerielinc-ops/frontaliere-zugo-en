Their tasks shall include

  • Neuropsychological diagnostic investigations and evaluations
  • Outpatient assessment of mentally ill
  • Participation in the expansion and further development of our test psychological offer
  • Accompaniment and further training of female assistant psychologists with regard to neuropsychological knowledge and skills
  • Participation in training events

They bring it with them.

  • Completed university degree in psychology
  • Very good knowledge of clinical psychology incl. Psychopathology
  • In-depth knowledge and experience in the application of test- and neuropsychological-diagnostic procedures (specialist title is a prerequisite)
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous
  • Good communication skills and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Interested in the accompaniment and further education of assistant psychologists

We offer them

  • Collegial Team, Opportunity for Intervision
  • A lot of quality of life with at least 5 weeks holidays, from 55. Age 6 weeks
