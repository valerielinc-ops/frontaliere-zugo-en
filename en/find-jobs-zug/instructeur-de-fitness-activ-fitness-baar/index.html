Are you looking for a new challenge? Do you want to share your passion for health and fitness? Mansioni

  • You support and accompany our customers throughout the training process

  • At the same time, the umbrella company movemi AG covers the costs of your training, which is part of working hours
  • You actively participate in further education and meetings

Requirements

  • You enjoy working in a team
  • You have a valid work permit
  • You have advantageous experience in the service sector and have a sports-loving personality

German (good knowledge)
