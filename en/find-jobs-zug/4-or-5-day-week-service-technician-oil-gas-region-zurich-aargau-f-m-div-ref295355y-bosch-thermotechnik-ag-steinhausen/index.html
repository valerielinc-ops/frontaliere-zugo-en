Full description

Your tasks

  • You take care of the professional commissioning of oil / gas heating systems, hot water systems and control systems.
  • You take care of the careful maintenance of these devices according to the high Buderus quality standards. You analyze and repair disruptions – expertly, quickly and independently.
  • You advise customers on the ideal settings of the devices according to personal customer needs.
  • You inform our customers about the comprehensive advantages of the optional Buderus maintenance contracts.
  • You organize your service assignments and reliably report them back to the customer and our dispatch.
  • You provide on-call service (piket) in consultation with the team.

Your profile

  • Education: EFZ training; preferably in the electrical, heating, machinery or car industry
  • Personality and working methods: reliable, self-responsible, service-oriented, resilient and flexible
  • Experience: Experience in the industry is desirable but "a positive mindset, great willingness to learn and interest in the matter" is much more important to us
  • Languages: stylish knowledge of German
