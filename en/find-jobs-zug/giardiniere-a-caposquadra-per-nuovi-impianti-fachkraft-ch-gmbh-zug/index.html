Full description

You plan and lead teams directly on the construction site. The work around patches, lawns, beds and plantings requires your craftsmanship. On behalf of our customer, we are looking for an activity

  • Professional implementation of new plants (pavement work, lawn, beds, planting, shrubs and trees)
  • Organization and guidance of assembly teams on site
  • Ensuring compliance with labor and safety regulations
  • Planning and controlling the use of materials and equipment
  • Quality control of the work carried out
  • Close cooperation with construction managers and customers
  • Requirements
  • Completed training in horticulture and landscaping or comparable qualification
  • Further training as a foremen
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally with experience in new investments
  • Leadership experience of teams of advantage
  • Self-employed, reliable and solution-oriented working methods
  • Craftsmanship and sense of responsibility
  • Driving license class B / BE Offer
  • in a responsible position with room for manoeuvre
  • A motivated, collegial team
  • Performance-based remuneration and attractive social benefits
  • Further education and development opportunities
  • Modern work equipment and equipment
