Full description

Control systems, control systems and measurement technology from Rittmeyer are the basis for innovative solutions in energy and water supplies, hydropower plants and waste disposal plants worldwide. As an engineering and service company with the highest quality standards, Rittmeyer specializes in software and system integration for turnkey systems in the infrastructure sector. Our service technicians are used where others go on holiday – in special places, in the middle of nature or in high-tension facilities that are indispensable for our care. With high-precision Rittmeyer measuring systems and the appropriate automation solutions, you ensure that customers in the water, wastewater and energy industry can count on reliable and accurate operating data at any time. To strengthen our team, we are looking for qualified and dedicated service technicians. Service technician (a) Fürth, Germany 100% Your responsibilities You take care of the assembly, commissioning and maintenance of measurement technology and systems in the area of automation Your service missions are independently planned, carried out and documented by you. You support our customers with the diagnosis and correction of faults or technical problems on site or via remote support You can independently make minor adjustments in our automation and process control systems You have successfully completed training in the fields of electrical engineering or mechatronics, with further training as a technician or master Ideally, you already have professional experience in the technical field service or service You bring affinity to PLC, PC and network knowledge with you are a team player and it characterizes you great personal responsibility and an open personality. You are about 70 % willing to travel throughout Germany and have a car driver's license What we offer you A self-employed job in a modern and environmentally conscious company with a motivating and collegial work culture. Our flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths bring us quickly and efficiently to our jointly developed goals. You can expect attractive employment conditions from a well-positioned company in a future-oriented market environment. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application!
