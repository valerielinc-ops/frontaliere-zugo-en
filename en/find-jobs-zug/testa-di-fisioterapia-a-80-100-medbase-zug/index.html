Welcome to Medbase Zug

Our Medical Center is located in the beautiful city of Zug, also known as "Chriesi" city. The Medical Center is very easily accessible by public transport, parking is also available. Here we offer patients general medical and therapeutic services. We maintain a good cooperation door to door with the Migros Fitnesspark Eichstätte for a comprehensive and modern range of movement medicine. At Medbase Zug, we ensure a balanced work-life balance. Working part-time is possible in all functions and working hours can be flexible.

You can move that

As Head of Physiotherapy at Medbase, you are at the head of a department that forms the foundation of our company. With your commitment and your eye for quality, you shape the further development of physiotherapeutic offers and lead a team of dedicated physiotherapists. Your work not only shapes everyday life, but also drives the interdisciplinary cooperation that distinguishes Medbase.

  • Independent treatment and care of patients from musculoskeletal physiotherapy
  • Guidance for patients in rehabilitation and prevention
  • Active exchange with other disciplines in the interdisciplinary team
  • Independent documentation and performance recording
  • Professional and personnel management of the physiotherapy team and complementary medicine (20-40%)
  • Support for quality development and compliance with standards

What you bring

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist/in HF/FH with SRK recognition
  • Structured and independent working methods
  • Experience or interest in a management function
  • Commitment to quality development and team development
  • Leadership skills and team skills
  • Good knowledge of German (minimum B2 level)

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation.
