Full description

We are looking for an independent and service-oriented personality to strengthen our team in the Central Switzerland region (Zug/Luzern) by agreement.

Tasks

As a travelling housekeeper:in a team you will take care of the buildings of our customers and their users in different regions. You will actively ensure a clean appearance of the properties. Your task area includes the following activities:

  • General maintenance work (e.g. switching of light bulbs)
  • Technical tours on the objects
  • Implementation of minor repairs
  • Piketdienst
  • Various cleaning work on various areas (e.g. emptying of waste bins, removing spider nets, cleaning of public toilets, removing graffitis etc.)
  • Support for special cleaning and removals
  • Gardening, environmental care, disposal, provision of containers
  • Contact person for customers
  • Performing acceptances and keeping of records
  • Representation of team members

Requirements

You call yourself a service provider:in conviction. A positive feedback about your achievements motivates you and encourages you to further top performance. You also bring the following requirements:

  • Basic manual training, specialist man:woman Business maintenance or cleaning of buildings advantage
  • Good computer skills
  • Very good German knowledge
  • Driving license Cat. B mandatory
  • High customer and solution orientation
  • Reliability, load capacity and flexibility
  • Communicative personality and well-kept appearance

We specialise in comprehensive services in the field of facility management for every phase of the life cycle of commercial properties and areas. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve the comfort of buildings. This way we minimize costs and create optimal conditions for our customers to concentrate on their core business.

As a company of E Quant Switzerland, the leading comprehensive provider of holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers forward and working together at the energy transition.
