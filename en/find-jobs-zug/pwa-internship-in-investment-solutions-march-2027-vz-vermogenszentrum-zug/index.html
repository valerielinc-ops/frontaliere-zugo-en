Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With the VZ custodian bank, we offer our customers the opportunity to process transactions efficiently and cheaply. Within the framework of the two-year PWA training, committed graduate students have the opportunity to complete a versatile practical training at a central location in Zug.

  • Your business education starts on 01. March 2027 and lasts 24 months. In the first few months you will visit the Kaderschule Zürich with a focus on business, finance and communication. Afterwards you will gain practical experience at the VZ custodian bank. Upon successful completion, you will receive the diploma Wirtschaftsfachfrau/Wirtschaftsfachmann KSZH. Further information can be found under PWA economic program.
  • You support our investment advisors in day-to-day business and get a comprehensive insight into investment and custodial consulting.
  • You accept customer orders for securities transactions and accompany their processing.
  • You will help create individual investment proposals for our customers.
  • You will work closely with our investment solutions team, analyze various investment tools and support us in maintaining our lists of recommendations.
  • You are the contact person for professional and organizational inquiries about our services in investment and custodial consulting.
  • You will learn about different asset classes and financial instruments and learn how the financial markets work.
  • You take on more responsibility step by step and work on your own tasks independently.
  • You have graduated, are enthusiastic about financial topics and are looking for entry into the banking industry.
  • You are communicative, open and motivated and bring a flair for numbers.
  • Your goal is to bring your talent to a dynamic and uncomplicated company, discover your professional strengths and continuously expand and apply your knowledge.
  • A team you can count on and a climate that promotes initiative.
  • Successful interns are given the opportunity for permanent employment after graduation.
  • You work in a company that Lucerne University of Applied Sciences counts among the most digital banks in Switzerland.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
