Become part of our team!

The Raiffeisenbank Cham-Steinhausen Cooperative is a locally anchored, successful bank with a strong focus on holistic customer advice. Especially in the area of asset succession, we rely on competence, trust and individual solutions. In order to strengthen our team at our headquarters in Cham, we are looking for a motivated, reliable and team-oriented personality as an assistant inheritance consultancy & execution of will.

What awaits you?

  • Support of currently two estate consultants in the preparation and processing of documents such as wills, marriage and inheritance contracts, proposals for division, provision orders, etc.
  • Acceptance and coordinated processing of customer concerns
  • Administrative support in day-to-day business
  • Organisation and coordination of appointments
  • Clarifications at offices, authorities and institutions
  • Ensuring and implementing internal processes
  • Opening and settlement of mandates

What do you bring?

  • Basic commercial training
  • Experience in an assistant role in banking or law
  • Strong quality awareness and a structured, careful, detailed and precise working method
  • Strength of communication in word and writing (German)
  • High organizational skills as well as the ability to prioritize tasks and to work in a structured and conscientious manner even with changing requirements
  • Strong IT affinity and very good knowledge of Microsoft Office and AI-supported applications
