Full description

For our customer – a future-oriented company with a modern and dynamic team – we are looking for a young, flexible and open personality as a clerk: in trust. In this exciting and varied activity, you support the mandate managers in the care of national and international customers. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Finance & Wages: Self-employed management of financial and payroll accounts.
  • Financial statements: Active assistance in the preparation of interim and annual accounts.
  • Taxation: preparation of tax returns for natural and legal persons.
  • VAT: Reliable preparation of VAT bills.

This is what you bring with you

  • Foundation: Completed CV apprenticeship (ideally in the trust industry) and/or several years of professional experience in the trust sector.
  • Working method: A quick understanding, an exact working method and a customer-oriented perspective.
  • Mindset: You are an established personality, are motivated for new tasks and actively promote a good team spirit with your positive nature.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Job profile: A varied job and a future-oriented challenge in a modern environment.

  • Development: Interesting and versatile training opportunities for your career.
