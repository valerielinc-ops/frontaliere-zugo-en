Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Zug, with its locations in Baar, Zug and Walchwil, is a successful, dynamic bank with a high level of customer confidence and a strong presence in the population. Our corporate client team needs reinforcement and is looking for a committed and entrepreneurial organizational talent. Together with another person, you are the hub, hold the strings together and support our corporate customer consultants.

What awaits you?

  • Support of corporate client consultants in operational business
  • Support of the head of corporate clients in administrative and organizational tasks
  • Pre- and post-processing and accompaniment of customer meetings
  • Cooperation in the preparation of loan applications and balance sheet analyses
  • Completion of telephone and written customer inquiries as well as cooperation and coordination with internal and external bodies

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training
  • Knowledge of credit and corporate banking is an advantage
  • Enjoyment of planning and organization
  • High degree of independence, initiative and customer orientation
