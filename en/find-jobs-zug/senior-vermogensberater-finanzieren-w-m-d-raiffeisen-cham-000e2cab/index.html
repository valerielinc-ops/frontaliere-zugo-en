Become part of our team!

We, Raiffeisenbank Cham-Steinhausen, are a successful and innovative bank, which is one of the largest Raiffeisen banks in Switzerland. At our locations in Cham, Steinhausen and Mettmenstetten, we employ around 75 people, including 5 apprentices. We attach great importance to a holistic and sustainable advice for our customers and are looking for a sympathetic, committed and reliable personality as a Senior Asset Advisor Financing (m/f/d) to complement our team.

What awaits you?

  • Comprehensive, needs-based advice to customers with a focus on financing
  • Further development of the existing customer base
  • Creating customer-oriented solution proposals for the entire advisory and service offering with the involvement of bank-internal specialists (professional provision, estate planning, etc.)
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential
  • Cooperation in market development measures and performance of representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • Banking and financial training completed
  • In-depth experience in the field of private credit consulting
  • Sales and customer-oriented personality with a high willingness to provide services
  • Strong communication skills, negotiating security
  • Team-oriented and entrepreneurial action
