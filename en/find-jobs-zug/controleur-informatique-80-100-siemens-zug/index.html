  • Education : Bachelor’s degree (university/university of applied sciences) in business administration, business informatics or a similar field of study

  • Some professional experience in a comparable commercial position

  • Ways of Working : Ownership mentality and a strong ability to work under your own initiative; reliable team player who works in a precise and analytical way
