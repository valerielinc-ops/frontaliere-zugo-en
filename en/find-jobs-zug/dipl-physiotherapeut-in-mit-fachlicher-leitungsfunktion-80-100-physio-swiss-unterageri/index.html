Then you are a top addition to our team! From January 2027 or by appointment, we are looking for a Physio (m/f/d) with a professional management function, who not only enjoys the profession, but also likes to think and shape - and can support a team.

What we offer you

  • A young and highly trained team of therapists
  • Generous salary conditions with annual adjustment and bonus
  • Modern infrastructure with EGYM, Sensopro and high-tech measuring devices
  • Exclusive team events and regular internal exchange rounds
  • Fixed scheduled admin times and of course common fluctuations
  • Extensive internal/external training offers with time/cost sharing
  • Free coffee, tea, mate and healthy snacks - logical!

What we expect from you

  • Master or CAS training with SRC recognition completed
  • Several years of professional experience and a good feeling for people and teams
  • First leadership experience or the clear motivation to develop into this role
  • You take on professional mentoring, pass on your knowledge and stay curious yourself
  • The close exchange with doctors: inside and other specialist areas is a matter of course for you

In addition to your therapeutic work, you take on professional responsibility: You are the contact person in the team, help to develop internal standards and contribute your know-how where it is needed. Always with the goal that everyone – team, patient, company – develop further. Does that suit you? Then contact us very easily! A CV is not necessary for the beginning – just send us a few lines by mail to [email protected] why you want this job. We look forward to seeing you!
