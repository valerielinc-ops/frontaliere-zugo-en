Role overview

In this role, you will sit at the important intersection of medical science, regulatory requirements and clear, responsible communication.

As Medical Review and Affairs Manager for Switzerland, you will provide medical, scientific and regulatory expertise and act as the legally required person responsible for advertising in accordance with Art. 25 AWV (Arzneimittel-Werbeverordnung).

Your work will help ensure that promotional and non-promotional materials are scientifically accurate, balanced and aligned with Swiss regulatory requirements.
