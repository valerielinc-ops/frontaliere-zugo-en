Full description

Control systems, control systems and measurement technology from Rittmeyer are the basis for innovative solutions in energy and water supplies, hydropower plants and waste disposal plants worldwide. As an engineering and service company with the highest quality standards, Rittmeyer specializes in software and system integration for turnkey systems in the infrastructure sector. For our small warehouse & logistics team, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality who keeps an overview, gladly assumes responsibility and combines leadership with operational activity. You ensure smooth operations in the warehouse, continue to develop processes and ensure that material is in the right place at the right time. Team Leader Warehouse & Logistics (a) Baar, Switzerland 100% Your area of responsibility Professional management and coordination of two employees organization and operational cooperation in the warehouse as well as in the acceptance and shipping of goods Ensure a smooth material flow for production, projects and service Responsibility for warehouse organization, stocks and workflows Further development of processes, standards and workflows Ensure a smooth flow of goods and a functioning warehouse infrastructure Coordination of the interfaces to purchasing, production, project planning and service What you bring with you Completed training as a logistics specialist EFZ or a comparable qualification Several years of experience in warehouse and logistics, ideally in an industrial environment First management experience or the willingness to assume management responsibility Technical understanding as well as a pronounced process thinking Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method Secure handling of Navision and Business Central Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, English skills What we offer you A very independent activity in a modern and environmentally conscious company with a motivating and collegial work culture. Our flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths bring us quickly and efficiently to our jointly developed goals. You can expect attractive employment conditions from a well-positioned company in a future-oriented market environment. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application!
