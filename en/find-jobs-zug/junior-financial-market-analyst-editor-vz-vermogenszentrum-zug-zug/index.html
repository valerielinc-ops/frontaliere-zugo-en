Full description

Are you interested in the financial markets, like to write and want to reach thousands of investors every day with your analyses? Then this job could be just right for you. For our research team in Zug, we are looking for a committed personality with an interest in financial markets and a flair for understandable communication. In this versatile role, you combine financial market analysis, research and financial journalism and actively shape the content of vzbörse.ch.

  • Together with the research team, you write articles and analyses for the financial market portal vzbörse.ch: You make financial market topics understandable and tangible for investors.
  • You are a member of the core editorial team, which prioritizes the portal content and writes the daily stock market newsletter.
  • You are responsible for our daily stock market ticker and prepare current developments on the financial markets in a concise and comprehensible way.
  • You will contribute to other publications of the VZ, including monthly and quarterly reports as well as studies.
  • Your content reaches a large readership every day.
  • You have an in-depth understanding of financial markets.
  • You have a college degree.
  • Initial analyst experiences are beneficial.
  • You enjoy writing and communicating and are able to bring complicated topics clearly and simply to the point.
  • You like to work independently and take on responsibility in the editorial team.
  • Team spirit and joy of new things distinguish you.
  • A dynamic media house at the heart of the financial markets
  • Individual support and support for external training
  • With us you can contribute ideas, take responsibility and write our success story.
  • Modern working methods and the possibility to work independently and conceptually.
  • A company in which you can develop: VZ performs above average in surveys to the satisfaction of its employees.
