Occupation by 1 January 2027 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Andreasklinik Cham Zug is located in the heart of Cham – nestled in a quiet and beautiful environment. Characteristic architecture and stylish interiors meet modern medical infrastructure in a hotel-like environment. The clinic specializes in the musculoskeletal system with orthopaedics as well as neuro- and spinal surgery. In addition, it offers comprehensive regional health care with the specialist areas of gynecology, urology, general surgery, general internal medicine and an emergency department open during the day and in the evening. First-class medical quality and the well-being of patients are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional care and support of our patients as well as independent implementation of the diagnostic and therapeutic measures prescribed by the doctor
  • Identification and control of emergency situations and initiation of adequate measures until the arrival of the doctor
  • Assuming responsibility for the correct patient documentation as well as information sharing in the assigned task area
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with our observing physicians and other areas
  • In your function as a vocational trainer, you accompany and support our learners and students on their way to a vocational degree as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF or specialist employee health EFZ
  • Active participation in projects and support of department management and the entire team to ensure the best possible quality of care

Your profile

  • Completed training for/for dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH
  • Professional experience in acute care
  • Completed vocational trainer course or willingness to complete it
  • Very good knowledge of German (verbal and written)
  • Engaged and team-oriented personality with a high sense of responsibility
  • Positive basic attitude, high patient orientation and independent and structured working methods

In addition to an attractive workplace on the beautiful Zugersee, you will find a family clinic and a dedicated, experienced team. We attach great importance to a working environment that is characterized by mutual respect and appreciation and ensure that you feel comfortable with us. Our benefits include:

  • Free coffee, tea and daily fresh fruits
  • Healthy and inexpensive menus in the staff restaurant
  • Cool employee discount platform (often with e.g. 11% Zalando, Ikea vouchers or 40% Adidas)
  • Free admissions to the Hallenbad Röhrliberg, the Tierpark Goldau and the Verkehrshaus
  • Paid dressing time with a workload of 100%, four additional days off. With a reduced workload, the free days decrease accordingly
