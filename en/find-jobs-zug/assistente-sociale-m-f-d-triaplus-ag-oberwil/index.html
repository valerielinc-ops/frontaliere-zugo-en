Full description

Their tasks shall include

  • Counselling and support of patients with mental illness and their relatives
  • Creating social history and treatment plans together with patients
  • Independent assessment and assessment of difficult social problems
  • Completion of demanding administrative tasks
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with institutions, advisory bodies and authorities
  • Organization and implementation of coaching discussions with and for patients looking for a follow-up solution in the area of living
  • Support and promotion of patients in social and professional integration with the aim of regaining their independence in everyday life
  • Active participation and further development of clinical social work

They bring it with them.

  • Diploma in Social Work FH with in-depth expertise in social work
  • Strong social and communication skills with empathy towards people in difficult life situations
  • High sense of responsibility as well as safe appearance and flexibility
  • Rapid comprehension, reliable and independent working method
  • Ability to keep track even under high workload
  • Joy and commitment to working together in a motivated, long-term team, active exchange of knowledge and experience
  • Good user knowledge in MS-Office
  • Proper knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage

We offer them

  • A team in which cooperation is highly valued and in which diversity is desired
  • A lot of quality of life with at least 5 weeks of holidays, from 55. Age 6 weeks
  • We contribute to your public transport costs
  • In the Triaplus you have the possibility of internal rotation
  • A varied job in an interprofessional team
