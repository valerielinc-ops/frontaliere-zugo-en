Full description

Control systems, control systems and measurement technology from Rittmeyer are the basis for innovative solutions in energy and water supplies, hydropower plants and waste disposal plants worldwide. As an engineering and service company with the highest quality standards, Rittmeyer specializes in software and system integration for turnkey systems in the infrastructure sector. Artificial intelligence opens up new opportunities for our employees, processes and business models. In order to turn potential into tangible added value, we are looking for a committed personality who empowers people, develops use cases and actively supports the responsible introduction of AI in the BRUGG Group. Do you want to translate AI opportunities into concrete solutions? Then you are right with us. AI Enablement Specialist (a) Baar, Switzerland 100% You develop and establish a group-wide AI literature and enablement program for different target groups. You support specialist areas in identifying suitable AI use cases, describing them in a structured manner and assessing them in terms of benefits, effort and feasibility. You will moderate workshops, training sessions, use-case clinics and community formats and translate complex AI topics into understandable practice. You work closely with Lead AI Governance, AI Technology Lead, Business Units and other functions. You make successes, boundaries and learning experiences visible and promote the reuse of good solutions within the group. You measure reach, skills growth, adoption and business value and develop the program further based on data. You have a completed degree or comparable qualification in business, business informatics, computer science, data/AI, organizational development, adult education or a related field. Several years of experience in at least two of the areas AI enablement, digital transformation, business analysis, process management, change, training/moderation or product/project management. Practical experience with generative AI and common enterprise AI tools, preferably in the Microsoft environment.and the implementation or support of digital use cases Strong understanding of business processes and economic added value as well as an awareness of the importance of governance, data protection and information security in the responsible use of AI. Communication-strong, pragmatic personality with the ability to combine business, technology and governance and align AI with concrete business benefits Very good knowledge of German and English. A newly created, Group-wide effective role at the interface of business, technology and governance. You actively shape the development of our AI competencies, work with committed colleagues from different business units and contribute to using AI responsibly and with measurable benefits. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application! Your contact HR Team hr@rittmeyer.com
