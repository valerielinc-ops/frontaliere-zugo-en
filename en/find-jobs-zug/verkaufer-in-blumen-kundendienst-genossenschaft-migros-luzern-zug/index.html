Full description

Enchant our customers with your creativity! As a salesman*in flowers/customer service, you can create artistic bouquets, gestures and decorations. With your expertise and dedication, you advise our customers and make their shopping a flourishing experience!

Mansioni

  • Professional customer service and consulting as well as customer service
  • Set up flower bouquets, gestures and decorations
  • Disposition of goods, presentation and care
  • inventory management

Requisiti

  • Professional experience: in the field of flowers and customer service
  • Completed basic or advanced training: as a flower sellerin or floristin
  • Joy in dealing with people and creative work
  • Self-employed and reliable operation
  • Customer-oriented personality with pleasure in selling
  • German (very good knowledge)
