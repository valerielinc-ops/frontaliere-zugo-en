Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With the VZ custodian bank, we offer our customers the opportunity to process transactions efficiently and cheaply. Within the framework of the two-year PWA training, committed graduate students have the opportunity to complete a versatile practical training at a central location in Zug.

  • Your business education starts on 17. August 2026 and lasts 24 months. In the first six months you will visit the Kaderschule Zürich with a focus on business, finance and communication. Afterwards you work for 18 months at the VZ custodian bank. Upon successful completion, you will receive the diploma Wirtschaftsfachfrau/Wirtschaftsfachmann KSZH. Further information can be found under PWA Economic Program.
  • You take on interesting tasks in relationship management. There you and your team are the first point of contact for the advisors of the VZ VermögensZentrum and support them in all matters relating to our banking services and asset management mandates.
  • You will learn a lot about securities, payment transactions, loans, e-banking, portfolio management, compliance topics and much more.
  • You will be in close contact with the colleagues of the VZ Group and you will also have the opportunity to accompany our consultants in customer discussions.
  • You have completed your graduation, are enthusiastic about financial topics and are looking for entry into the banking industry.
  • You are communicative, open and motivated and bring a flair for numbers.
  • Your goal is to bring your talent to a dynamic and uncomplicated company, discover your professional strengths and continuously expand and apply your knowledge.
  • A team you can count on and a climate that promotes initiative.
  • Successful interns are given the chance of permanent employment after graduation.
  • You work in a company that Lucerne University of Applied Sciences counts as one of the most digital banks in Switzerland.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
