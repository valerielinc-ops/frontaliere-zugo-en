You will be working in a team that is doing cutting-edge research in Industrial AI. Your tasks would involve deploying and maintaining AI agents on AWS and Edge devices. Typically, you enjoy setting up and configuring cloud services or like to experiment with distributed systems architecture. Ideally you would be a "hands-on" person who is willing to explore and learn on demand. Your new responsibilities

  • You support the deployment of local large language models (LLMs) using frameworks such as Ollama, contributing to the implementation and optimisation of AI infrastructure
  • Building on this, you assist in setting up and maintaining cloud-based AI services, particularly leveraging Amazon Web Services (AWS) Bedrock and related tools
  • In this context, you collaborate with the team to automate infrastructure processes through scripting and ensure smooth operation of Linux-based systems
  • Additionally, you contribute to documenting deployment processes and infrastructure configurations in a clear and structured manner, including the creation of diagrams Your talents and experience
  • You are currently enrolled at a Swiss university in a relevant field such as computer science, engineering or data science
  • You have hands-on experience with frameworks for local large language models (LLMs) such as Ollama
  • You are familiar with AWS Bedrock and have a fundamental understanding of services in Amazon Web Services
  • You have some practical experience with agentic artificial intelligence and scripting infrastructure setups
  • You work in a structured and solution-oriented manner and communicate technical topics clearly in English Employee benefits
