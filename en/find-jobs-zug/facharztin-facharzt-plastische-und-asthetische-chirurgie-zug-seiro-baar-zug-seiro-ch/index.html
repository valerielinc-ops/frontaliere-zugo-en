Full description

Plastic and aesthetic surgery in one of the most modern dermatology centers in Switzerland, with its own consultation and the entire range of operations. In Zug, with a second location in Zurich, we are looking for a specialist in plastic and aesthetic surgery immediately or by appointment. It expects an interdisciplinary skin and aesthetic center with state-of-the-art infrastructure, certified operating room and a rapidly growing, versatile patient asset. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • You advise patients in your own consultation and discuss the best possible treatment
  • They perform surgical procedures throughout the spectrum of plastic surgery
  • You work in trust with a well-established team of surgical assistant and anesthesia
  • You advise the team on processes, materials and organizational issues of the plastic surgical spectrum
  • They look after patients at two locations in two cantons.

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title in Plastic, Reconstructive and Aesthetic Surgery FMH with appropriate recognition
  • Completed specialist training with recognition for Switzerland for foreign diplomas
  • High level of professional competence and experience in performing surgical procedures for plastic surgery
  • Ideally own professionally responsible approval with admission to the OKP
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, at least level C1

That sets them apart.

  • Self-employed, responsible working with the highest quality standards
  • High social competence as well as patient- and service-oriented thinking
  • Good organizational capacity and strong process affinity
  • Careful appearance and very good manners

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Large, growing and versatile patient goods in all areas of aesthetics with high demand for plastic surgery
  • Independent care of patients in their own consultation hours
  • State-of-the-art infrastructure with state-of-the-art treatment rooms, certified operating room, medical laser equipment and medical cosmetics
  • Very well organized, process-driven practice that optimally supports doctors
  • Young, dynamic team with high social intelligence and attractive, flexible working models
