Full description

As a company of the BRUGG Group, Rittmeyer's BRUGG IT Department provides infrastructure services for the entire internationally active corporate group. Three organizations with a total of 1600 end users benefit from this. The central service desk is the first point of contact for our users and serves as an interface with the other IT disciplines. You are the first point of contact for ICT-related questions and ensure that our employees can work reliably and efficiently in their day-to-day activities. With your technical know-how, hands-on mentality and solution-oriented approach, you actively tackle challenges and find pragmatic solutions.

ICT Supporter (a) Baar, Switzerland 80-100%

Your responsibilities

  • 1st- and 2nd-level support for the entire BRUGG Group
  • Receiving, initially analyzing and processing incident reports in the ticket system
  • Initial support and error analysis by telephone and remotely
  • Setting up, configuring and installing software on end-user devices via Microsoft Intune, as well as managing the entire IMACD process
  • On-site support at our locations – primarily in Baar, and in Brugg, Birr and Kleindöttingen as needed
  • Creating and maintaining documentation, instructions, knowledge base articles and checklists
  • Training and supporting our users

What you bring

  • Successfully completed training as an IT specialist EFZ, ICT certificate or comparable qualification
  • Sound knowledge of Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Azure AD/Entra ID, Windows operating systems and networking technologies
  • Experience in IT support and sound knowledge of client management
  • Enjoyment of telephone and personal customer contact, as well as service-oriented support
  • Hands-on mentality: You are a doer, actively tackle challenges and find solutions
  • Team player with an independent, service-oriented and solution-oriented approach to work
  • Very good German and good English skills, both written and spoken
  • Category B driver's license

What we offer you

  • A varied and exciting role in a modern, broad-based IT department.
  • Our flat hierarchy and short decision-making paths set us apart and help us reach our goals quickly.
  • We also offer attractive employment conditions and are a well-positioned company in a market environment with future potential.
