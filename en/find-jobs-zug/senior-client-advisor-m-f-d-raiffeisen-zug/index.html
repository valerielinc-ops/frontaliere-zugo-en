Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Zug, with its locations in Baar, Zug and Walchwil, is a successful bank with a high level of customer confidence and strong roots in the population. To strengthen our customer consulting team Zug/Walchwil, we are looking for an independent and committed personality as a customer consultant. You take responsibility for the welcome desk and are jointly responsible for the consulting and development of our team-supported customers.

What awaits you?

  • You are the host and first contact person for our customers
  • You advise our team-supported customers in the basic products "Savings, figures, investing & pensions"
  • You can expect a well-functioning and well-functioning team at your side.

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training
  • Several years of experience in the customer service of a bank
  • Communicative and open personality, pleasure in customer contact and ability to keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • Anchorage in the Canton of Zug is advantageous
