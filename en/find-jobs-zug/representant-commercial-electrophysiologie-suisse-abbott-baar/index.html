The Opportunity For our Electrophysiology (EP) division, we are looking for a motivated and committed Sales Representative EP (m/f/d) for Switzerland. In this sales role, you will manage existing customer accounts, build and strengthen long-term customer relationships, identify new business opportunities, and make a significant contribution to achieving regional sales objectives.

Your Responsibilities

  • Achieve agreed sales targets through the management of existing customers and acquisition of new accounts.
  • Develop and implement regional sales strategies to increase market penetration.
  • Build and maintain sustainable business relationships with healthcare professionals and healthcare institutions.
  • Analyze sales performance metrics, prepare reports, and perform administrative sales activities.
  • Ensure compliance with internal policies regarding pricing, quotations, credit terms, quality standards, and expense management.
  • Maintain and utilize account profiles, including relevant customer and market information.
  • Monitor market and competitive activities and communicate relevant insights within the organization.
  • Conduct product training for customers and internal colleagues and support knowledge-sharing initiatives.
  • Represent Abbott as a professional and ethical partner while adhering to all applicable compliance and healthcare professional (HCP) guidelines.

Your Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent qualification gained through relevant professional experience.
  • Minimum of five years of successful sales experience within the medical device industry.
  • Strong knowledge of a relevant therapy area and a solid understanding of market and customer needs.
  • Excellent organizational skills and experience managing multiple projects in a dynamic environment.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, Outlook, and PowerPoint.
  • Experience working in matrix organizations and geographically dispersed teams.
  • Outstanding communication, presentation, and relationship management skills.
  • Self-motivated, results-oriented, and customer-focused working style.
  • Willingness to travel regularly, with a minimum travel requirement of 25%.
