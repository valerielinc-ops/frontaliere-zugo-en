About the Role As part of our Corporate Communications and Investor Relations team , you will help shape how we communicate with our stakeholders. We are looking for a strategic thinker, talented writer, and creative mind who knows how to find the right words, develop compelling content, and turn ideas and complex business topics into meaningful communication. You have a passion for storytelling, both written and visual, and understand how to bring messages to life across different channels and formats. You are comfortable taking ownership, contributing fresh perspectives, and turning ideas into action. This is a temporary position for 6 months.

Your Responsibilities

  • Create, write, and edit compelling content for our stakeholders across different channels and formats
  • Translate complex business topics into clear, concise, and engaging communication tailored to different target audiences
  • Proactively identify and develop content ideas, storytelling approaches, and communication concepts with a strategic mindset
  • Support and contribute to ongoing communication projects and initiatives, taking ownership of assigned tasks and deliverables
  • Ensure all content is consistent, accurate, audience-focused, and aligned with the company's tone of voice and brand identity
  • Collaborate with colleagues and stakeholders across functions and international markets to develop relevant and impactful communication

Your Skills and Experience

  • Degree in Communications, Journalism, English, Marketing, Public Relations, or a related field
  • 5+ years of professional experience in corporate communications, editorial content, journalism, content creation, or a comparable role
  • Experience working in an international or corporate environment is an advantage
  • Excellent writing and editing skills, with a strong sense of language and the ability to find the right words for different audiences and purposes
  • Excellent command of English, ideally at native or near-native level
  • A strategic mindset combined with strong hands-on content creation skills
  • The ability to quickly understand complex topics and translate them into accessible and compelling communication
  • Strong interpersonal and collaboration skills, with an open-minded and team-oriented approach
  • Attention to detail, sound judgment, and a good understanding of what makes communication relevant and effective

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemicals company with a globally leading position in the development and production of systems and products for bonding, sealing, damping, reinforcing, and protection in the building sector and industrial manufacturing.
