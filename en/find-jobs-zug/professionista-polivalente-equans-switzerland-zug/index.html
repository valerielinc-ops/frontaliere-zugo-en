Full description

We are looking for a service-oriented, dynamic and flexible personality to strengthen our team.

You can move that with us.

In a small team, you manage several buildings and properties of demanding customers. Your responsibilities include the following activities:

  • Taking over all infrastructural tasks (e.g. environmental work, winter service, outdoor cleaning)
  • Office removal operations
  • General housekeeper and mobile phone work (e.g. change of lighting, etc.)
  • Carrying out minor repairs
  • Gardening/environment
  • Disposal and recycling, cooperation at the in-house Oekihof
  • Responsibility for the allocated vehicle
  • Winter Picquet Service (Nov.-April)

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances. You also bring:

  • Completed basic training, beneficial in the field (cleaning of buildings, maintenance, logistics or similar)
  • Several years of professional experience desired
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good computer user knowledge (mandatory)
  • Cat B driving licence (mandatory), with trailer test
  • Craftsmanship (mandatory)
  • Customer-oriented, efficient and independent working method
  • They are well-groomed and well-groomed.
  • Teamplayer

That's what we offer you.

We offer you a responsible and interesting task with plenty of space for initiative in an exciting professional environment. We look forward to your complete application.
