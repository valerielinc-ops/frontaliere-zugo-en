As of January 1, 2027, we are looking for a service-oriented and motivated personality to strengthen our team in Zug.

You can move that with us.

  • Receive visitors, customers, employees from other locations and suppliers and notify internal contacts
  • Issuance, management and return of visitor IDs, temporary staff IDs and contractor IDs
  • Ordering office supplies and coffee
  • Contact point for all questions in the office
  • Responsible for order in the reception area
  • Rapid and reliable receipt and routing of telephone calls

  • Booking and management of meeting rooms

That's what makes you.

  • Compelling professional experience at the reception and in a larger, multicultural service environment
  • Flowing language skills in English
  • Competent PC and MS Office skills
  • Strong communication and team skills
  • Willingness and flexibility for more use in the absence of holidays and illnesses in the team

That's what we offer you.

We offer you a responsible and interesting task with plenty of space for initiative in an exciting professional environment. We look forward to your complete application with photo.
