Role overview

Tasks

In this varied and responsible position, you are an important part of the construction administration team and ensure the smooth contract and procurement process in the company.

  • Co-responsibility in contract management in construction projects
  • Preparation of planner and entrepreneur contracts as well as coordination, control and filing of contract documents in the internal systems
  • Collaboration in higher-level procurement and support of project-leading client representatives in submission and procurement
  • Participation in contract negotiations in cooperation with building owner representatives and ensure company standards
  • Development of framework and contracting contracts
  • Advice and support in contractual issues of the building owner representatives
  • Identification and evaluation of opportunities and risks arising from contracts
  • Leading and further developing standards, templates and processes in contracting

Requirements

  • Higher technical college (HF, CAS) in construction management or university degree (FH/ETH/uni), construction management, business administration or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in contract management
  • In-depth knowledge of SIA standards and Swiss contract law in construction
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High communication capability and negotiating security
  • Reliable, resilient and team capable personality
  • High commitment and commitment to demanding tasks
  • Ability to work calmly and efficiently even under time pressure
  • Team-oriented thinking and proactive participation in the company
