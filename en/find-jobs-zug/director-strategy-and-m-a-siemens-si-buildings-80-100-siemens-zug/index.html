Join our Buildings Business Unit within Siemens Smart Infrastructure We are looking for a Director Strategy & M&A to shape the strategic direction, drive portfolio development, and accelerate growth of our Buildings business. This role offers the opportunity to influence the future of our Buildings portfolio and drive strategic initiatives in a dynamic, evolving market Location : this position will be based in Zug/Switzerland The Role As Director, you are a key member of our Strategy and M&A team at the SI Buildings business Your focus will be to actively manage our business portfolio through acquisitions and divestments. Depending on the transaction workload, you will lead in addition strategic projects to drive profitable growth of our Buildings business Specifically, you will:

  • Identify and evaluate potential businesses for acquisitions and divestments
  • Take ownership of M&A initiatives by assessing portfolio, evaluating strategic fit, synergies, and performing preliminary valuations
  • Lead strategic discussions with targets and buyers to shape proposals that you bring forward to our senior leadership team
  • Collaborate with M&A specialists on due diligence, carve-out/separation planning and execution to secure successful outcomes throughout the Siemens investment & divestment processes
  • Assess operational dependencies, stand-alone considerations and potential separation requirements to ensure that proposed transactions are strategically and operationally executable
  • Lead strategic projects to drive profitable growth
  • Advise our executive leadership team on complex strategic and transaction decisions. Act as sparring partner to senior leaders in our countries and business segments
  • Systematically collect and review learnings from projects with the team, and incorporate them to continuously improve our strategy and M&A approach
  • Be a leader, expert and coach in our business unit on strategy and M&A Your talents and experience
  • Strategic thinker with ability to structure and solve complex problems and lead projects in Strategy and M&A (buy & sell side) independently
  • MBA or Master's degree in Business Administration / Economics / Finance, Electrical / Mechanical / Industrial engineering or similar
  • 7+ years of total work experience in Strategy, M&A and/or Consulting with experience in M&A projects (buy / sell side, integration / separation) from a strategic lens. Sell-side and separation experience is a must
  • Ability to identify and detail-out value creation levers in M&A
  • Experienced in determining strategic fit, synergies, developing valuations, elaborating feasibility analyses incl. buyer universe, and adept at forging relationships with potential sellers, buyers and partners
  • Strong analytical skills to assess companies, review financial statements, challenge their consistency, and make meaningful assumptions
  • Knowledge of the Building and Infrastructure, or IoT and Software domain, as basis to be able to independently judge target-fit with our business
  • Existing network and ability to liaise with relevant third parties including investment banks and potential targets/buyers
  • Team player with a faithful character, intercultural competence, growth mindset and resilient personality who drives varying team constellations to success
  • Willingness and flexibility to work in a global business environment, spanning across global time zones
  • Fluent language skills in English and strong communication skills on senior management level Employee benefits
  • 2–3 days per week of mobile working is standard
  • Mobility allowance
  • Varied further training opportunities
  • Access to employee share programs
