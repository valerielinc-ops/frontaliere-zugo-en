Full description

Their tasks shall include

  • Professional, human and organizational leadership of a psychologist team
  • Ensure professional training and supervision of psychological staff
  • Participation in management meetings
  • Ensuring the ambulatory emergency service in the form of regular background services (weekdays, no nights)
  • Psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of a diverse group of patients
  • Case management of complex outpatient cases
  • Professional management of an offer focus
  • Completing administrative work, including writing and targeting correspondence
  • Coordination of interdisciplinary cooperation in your own area and with other departments/areas
  • Commitment to the continuous development of the therapeutic offer
  • Networking with other bodies and providers in the field of children and youth
  • Cooperation with allocators
  • Public relations and participation in internal and external training and information events

They bring it with them.

  • Graduate degree in psychology and completed further education as a federally recognized psychotherapist
  • Several years of professional experience in the child and adolescent psychiatric environment and in therapeutic work with families
  • Leadership experience desired, but not a requirement
  • High affinity and interest in social psychiatric and psychotherapeutic issues
  • High social competence and empathetic treatment of patients
  • Personal responsibility and inclusive working and leadership style
  • Networked thinking skills and proven leadership experience
  • Joy of leadership and teamwork

We offer them

  • We are small enough to be “family”, for short distances and fast decisions – and so big that you can find all the settings here and you can specialize / develop professionally
  • A lot of quality of life with at least 5 weeks holidays, from 55. Age 6 weeks
  • We contribute to your public transport costs
  • In the Triaplus you have the possibility of internal rotation

Many reasons speak for us – these are the bestsellers. To the bestsellers
