Full description

The Zug Canton Hospital serves the health of the population of the Canton of Zug and the surrounding regions. Around 1,200 employees, 180 of them in education and training, work in a wide range of fields. Every year we treat and care for more than 11,500 inpatient and more than 70,000 outpatients. In the interdisciplinary emergency center, more than 27,500 people are treated annually. In addition to high medical and nursing skills, we also offer our patients modern and versatile hotel services.
