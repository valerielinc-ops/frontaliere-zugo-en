Full description

The Bossard Group is a leading strategic partner for industrial fastening and assembly technology solutions to OEM customers globally with proven expertise in engineering and logistic services. Bossard was founded in Zug in 1831. The Bossard Group employs over 3,000 people in 33 countires world-wide and is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange. Choose Bossard as your employer and benefit from attractive employment conditions.

Buyer:in 100% (all genders) | Bossard Group

As a buyer: in our team you are responsible for the timely supply of our central warehouses in Switzerland, France and Denmark. You process orders, maintain supplier relationships and keep our data up-to-date – so that everything is in the right place at the right time.

Your tasks 📝

  • Responsibility for the optimal availability of goods and services taking into account the objectives and the current procurement market situation
  • Processing of inquiries, orders, order confirmations, offers and reminders, as well as notifications and complaints
  • Further development of an optimal supplier base and relationship
  • Updating of supplier and item data
  • Close cooperation with the stakeholders of the subsidiaries within Europe (sales, quality, engineering, range team)
  • Participation in supplier visits

Your profile 🙂

  • Commercial or technical basic training with commercial training
  • Preferably further training in procurement (e.g. federal dipl.) Buyer/woman
  • At least 5 years of experience in purchasing or AVOR/disposition, preferably in an international environment
  • Good communication skills, responsibility and an analytical approach
  • Target-oriented, self-responsible and independent working method
  • IT affinity with very good MS Office skills
  • Experience in dealing with Microsoft Dynamics 365 is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
