Our neuromodulation business includes implantable devices compatible with mobile technology to help people suffering from chronic pain and movement disorders. These non-opioid therapies allow us to offer interventional pain therapy to patients throughout the pain continuum.

In order to strengthen our neuromodulation team in Switzerland, we are looking for a

Business Development Manager (m/f/d) Neuromodulation – Switzerland home office based in Switzerland

In this position, you will be responsible for the further development and establishment of our neuromodulation therapies in the field of Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS), Dorsal Root Ganglion (DRG) and Radiofrequency Therapy (RF) in Switzerland. Develop together with Sales, Clinical Specialists and Marketing You will develop sustainable growth strategies, build a strong network of key opinion leaders and ensure the successful implementation of our sales and market goals.

Tasks

  • Development and implementation of market and sales strategies for the successful positioning of DBS, DRG and RF therapies in Switzerland
  • Identification, development and expansion of strategic customer accounts to increase market penetration in DBS
  • Segmentation of the market and prioritization of suitable target customers in DBS &DRG in close cooperation with Territory Managers, Clinical Specialists and Marketing
  • Establishment, maintenance and further development of a national Key Opinion Leader (KOL) network in the field of DBS &DRG
  • Responsibility for the therapy strategy in the area of DRG as well as initiation and management of relevant clinical customer events, workshops and round tables
  • Creating and implementing individual account plans to achieve revenue and growth targets
  • Function as national trainer and specialist contact person for DRG and RF therapies in Switzerland
  • Support for European neuromodulation training and education activities
  • Training and professional support of sales staff as well as support of doctors and nurses during implantations and follow-up appointments
  • Analysis of market, competition and customer developments to identify new business opportunities and growth areas
  • Implementation of product presentations and representation of the company at congresses, trade events and trade fairs
  • Ensuring up-to-date expertise in therapies, products, clinical applications, regulatory requirements and quality standards
  • Active support of marketing activities and regular reporting of relevant market and sales information to management
  • Promoting close and successful collaboration within the sales and cross-functional team

Your profile

  • Completed studies in the medical, scientific, technical or business sector or a comparable qualification
  • At least 5 years of successful sales experience in healthcare, ideally in the area of neuromodulation or with complex medical devices
  • In-depth experience in business development and in building long-term customer and network relationships
  • Strong understanding of market and competitive dynamics in the medical technology or neuromodulation environment
  • Experience in dealing with key opinion leaders and clinical stakeholders
  • Strong commercial orientation and strong relationship management skills
  • Convincing presentation and communication skills both in large groups and in personal exchange
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills
  • Independent, structured and results-oriented working method with a high level of initiative
  • Team-oriented personality with a professional and customer-oriented appearance
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage
  • High willingness to travel within Switzerland
