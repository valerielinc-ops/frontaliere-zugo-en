Full description

As an employee buffet / cashier you make sure that our guests feel completely at home! You prepare the buffet and present it in an appetizing way.

Mansioni

  • Customer service in compliance with the cash regulations and providing customer information
  • Buffet Construction and Care
  • The Mise and Place
  • Miscellaneous cleaning work
  • Cooperation in the Office

Requirements

  • in gastronomy and/or sales: first professional experience
  • Completed basic or further education: an advantage in gastronomy
  • High quality and responsibility awareness
  • Service-oriented personality with host flair
  • Reliable and accurate working methods (including good HACCP knowledge)
  • Self-employment and flexibility
  • German (very good knowledge)
