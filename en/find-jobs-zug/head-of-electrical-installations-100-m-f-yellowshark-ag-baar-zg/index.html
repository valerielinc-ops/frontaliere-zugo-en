Your role

As head of the electrical division, you will have overall responsibility for the professional and operational management of your area.

Your varied range of responsibilities includes in particular

  • Overall responsibility for electrical engineering projects and services within the assigned area
  • Management, support and further development of an existing team
  • Promoting the professional and personal development of employees
  • Planning and management of human and operational resources
  • Ensuring high-quality, timely and cost-effective service delivery
  • Monitoring projects, orders, budgets and costs
  • Analyzing and managing relevant business performance indicators
  • Ensuring compliance with legal requirements and internal quality and safety standards
  • Responsibility for the consistent implementation of occupational safety measures
  • Planning and organization of necessary training and awareness-raising measures
  • Maintaining existing customer relationships and actively developing the customer portfolio
  • Supporting the acquisition of new customers and orders
  • Reviewing and supporting contracts, as well as ensuring professional service delivery
  • Optimization and further development of internal procedures, standards and processes
  • Close collaboration with internal specialist departments and other areas of the company
  • Active involvement in the strategic and operational further development of the electrical division

04

Your profile

You are an experienced electrical professional with management experience and possess the necessary technical and business expertise to manage a division independently.

For this position, you ideally meet the following requirements:

  • Completed vocational training as an electrical installer EFZ
  • Completed further training as an electrical project manager for installation and safety with a Federal Certificate of Competence
  • Required qualifications in electrical safety
  • Several years of professional experience in the planning, implementation and supervision of electrical systems
  • Experience in a management role or demonstrable management skills
  • Strong interpersonal skills and enjoyment of managing and developing employees
  • Good knowledge of relevant electrical engineering standards and regulations
  • Strong understanding of economic and operational interrelationships
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and a high degree of initiative
  • Experience in project and order processing
  • Proficient use of MS Office and common digital work tools
  • Knowledge of an ERP system is an advantage
  • Very good command of German, both written and spoken
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented approach to work
  • Assertiveness combined with a cooperative leadership style
  • Professional and confident manner when dealing with employees, customers and business partners
