Role overview

Introduction/Purpose

The position holder will be office based and is expected to travel occasionally for internal and external meetings, trainings and conferences.

It will be the responsibility of the position holder to help develop the presence and recognition of Sobi as a credible partner within the rare disease community through supporting the development of relationships with key stakeholders (especially physicians), as well as authorities and patient access colleagues as well as DACH and HQ staff in a cross functional approach to Sobi’s interactions with all stakeholders (physicians, payers, nurses and patients).
