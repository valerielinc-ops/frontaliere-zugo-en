Full description

Together with our customers, we connect the real and the digital world. As one of the leading technology companies, Siemens employs around 6,000 people in Switzerland. With our pioneering solutions in the areas of building technology, energy, mobility, health and industry, we are shaping the future. Do you also want to actively participate in the pulse of time and make the lives of millions of people in Switzerland better? Then you are exactly right with us! For our location in Zug, we are looking for a dedicated test engineer to support our test and test systems in electronics production and device assembly. Together with an experienced team of seven colleagues, you ensure smooth operation, drive continuous improvements (CIP) and accompany exciting innovation projects at the interface between R&D and production. Your new tasks

  • Ensuring the availability of our measurement and testing systems including 2nd-level support
  • Systematic troubleshooting of electronic assemblies (flat assemblies) and analysis and evaluation of test and measurement data
  • Active initiation and implementation of improvement measures to increase test coverage and plant efficiency
  • Maintenance and further development of existing in-circuit (ICT) and functional test programs as well as adapters as a result of product changes
  • Cooperation in the introduction of new products and test systems
  • Training and professional support of production teams in the field of testing Your talents and experience
  • training: basic training as an electronics engineer, test planner (or equivalent); An FH degree in electrical engineering is a strong plus
  • Practice: In-depth experience in measuring and testing technology as well as in the safe handling of common measuring instruments
  • Methodology: Structured, analytical approach to troubleshooting complex electronic circuits and test systems
  • Software affinity: Good knowledge or affinity for programming (e.g. C#, VB.Net, LabView or TestStand)
  • Soft Skills: Communicative, flexible personality who keeps a cool head even in a dynamic production environment and enjoys working in a team
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of German and English (for reading technical manuals and data sheets) Your advantages
