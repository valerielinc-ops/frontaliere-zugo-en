Become part of our team!

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a winning, service-oriented and committed personality.

What awaits you?

  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous customer portfolio support

  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the customer’s living situation as well as within the segment-specific / customer-specific product range

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zug's migration office after the contract is signed.
