Full description

Start our internal training program and learn the bakery craft. In the 12-month apprenticeship starting in February 2027, we train you to become an employee of the house bakery. After a successful completion, we offer you a permanent job.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, house bakery Zugerland/Steinhausen, Hinterbergstrasse 40, 6312 Steinhausen

Mansioni

  • You are jointly responsible for the production of our versatile baked goods.
  • Monitor production and carry out dough, table and oven work.
  • You implement the quality and hygiene requirements and are the ambassador of the products to our customers.

Requirements

  • Readiness for shift work and Saturday work
  • Joy at work as well as a reliable working method and resilience even in hectic situations
  • No education/training required
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.
  • Club School Migros: Refund of 70% of the costs per course (max. CHF 1,000 per course).
  • Fair wages: FFB-Group pays its employees a fair and market-driven wage based on our personnel compensation system.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages.

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications (FFB-Group): We continuously review applications received, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email.
  • Personal video or telephone conversation (FFB Group): For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round.
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (FFB-Group): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post.
