Full description

The Zug Canton Hospital serves the health of the population of the Canton of Zug and the surrounding regions. Around 1,200 employees, 180 of them in education and training, work in a wide range of fields. Every year we treat and care for more than 11,500 inpatient and more than 50,000 outpatients. In the interdisciplinary emergency center, more than 27,500 people are treated annually. For our hospital pharmacy we are looking for a hospital pharmacist 60 - 80% (m/f) as of January 1, 2027 or by appointment. Their tasks

  • Ensuring a modern, economical and high-quality pharmaceutical supply within the framework of legal and operational requirements
  • Information on medicinal products and professional advice to doctors, patients and nurses
  • Depending on the interest and suitability, cooperation in various areas of hospital pharmacy is possible (spital pharmacy, clinical pharmacy, public pharmacy, central cytostatics production)
  • Ensuring drug therapy safety throughout the medication process from hospital entry to exit
  • Development and maintenance of internal guidelines and quality standards
  • Supporting projects to further develop the field of hospital pharmacy Your profile
  • Eidg. Diploma as a pharmacist or recognised foreign diploma
  • Professional experience in a hospital pharmacy and/or public pharmacy
  • Completed further training in hospital pharmacy/clinical pharmacy of advantage
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation with all hospital areas
  • Competence, communication skills and safe presence
  • High quality and responsibility, reliability, teamwork and resilience
  • Process-oriented thinking, independent and structured working method
  • Initiative, flexibility and interest in the further development of hospital pharmacy Our offer We offer you a varied and demanding job in a professional, innovative and interdisciplinary working environment in a collegial team. They benefit from attractive employment conditions, such as more than 5 weeks holidays, various benefits, generous participation in professional training, and very good social benefits. Interested? Please send your electronic application (via applicant link) to Gaby Jans, Stv. Head of Human Resources. Only direct applications will be considered. Christoph Rosen, Head of Hospital Pharmacy, T +41 41 399 44 95, is happy to provide you with further information about the function.
