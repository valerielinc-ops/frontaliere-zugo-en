Full description

For our client in Zug, we are looking for an experienced Semantic HVAC & Ontology Engineer to strengthen an innovative R&D team focused on intelligent buildings, semantic data models, and digital building technologies.

Your Responsabilities

  • Model HVAC equipment, systems, and their relationships within a semantic building ontology to create a structured representation of HVAC domain knowledge
  • Map existing engineering data and application templates to a unified semantic data model using established industry standards
  • Integrate Web of Things (WoT) Thing Models into semantic building models to simplify device integration and commissioning
  • Leverage Artificial Intelligence to accelerate semantic mapping processes and develop innovative applications in analytics, optimization, and autonomous building operations
  • Ensure interoperability across different product lines and support the integration of new devices and systems
  • Collaborate closely with interdisciplinary teams, product management, and other stakeholders within an agile R&D environment
  • Actively contribute to knowledge sharing and the advancement of technological standards and solution

Your Profile

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Building Technologies, Mechanical Engineering, or a comparable technical discipline is an advantage
  • Strong hands-on experience in HVAC engineering, controls engineering, and the development of HVAC systems and applications
  • Solid understanding of semantic technologies and ontologies, particularly RDF , RDFS , and OWL
  • Experience with semantic building models such as Brick Schema , RealEstateCore , or similar frameworks
  • Experience in mapping and modelling technical data structures
  • Good knowledge of modern programming languages and development practices, ideally with Python
  • Experience working with Siemens Control Engineering Libraries is a plus
  • Fluent English, both written and spoken, German language skills are considered an advantage
