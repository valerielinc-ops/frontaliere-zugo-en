As Head Customer Service, you will play a pivotal role in ensuring smooth end-to-end customer and order operations, connecting commercial teams, supply chain and distribution partners. You will combine people leadership with operational excellence, using SAP S/4HANA, automation and artificial intelligence to simplify processes, strengthen service quality and help our teams deliver an outstanding experience for customers.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead end-to-end customer service and order operations, ensuring accurate and timely order processing, returns, credits and invoicing.
  • Lead, develop and support the customer service team, aligning resources and capabilities with business priorities.
  • Drive effective collaboration with sales teams, healthcare customers, wholesalers, distribution partners, supply chain and warehouse teams.
  • Oversee commercial operations, including pricing, customer master data, product launches and commercial documentation.
  • Champion SAP S/4HANA enhancements, automation and artificial intelligence solutions to improve customer and operational processes.
  • Lead cross-functional improvement initiatives that strengthen customer experience, operational effectiveness and process efficiency.
  • Ensure adherence to quality standards, financial controls, regulatory requirements and established customer service procedures.

Essential Requirements

  • University degree in economics, business or a related discipline, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Relevant customer service experience within the pharmaceutical industry, including order-to-cash or commercial operations.
  • Proven people leadership experience, with the ability to develop teams and drive operational excellence.
  • Strong experience with SAP S/4HANA, particularly Sales and Distribution and Materials Management modules, and Microsoft Office.
  • Fluency in German, English to effectively collaborate with customers and stakeholders.
  • Strong customer orientation, structured problem-solving skills and the ability to manage complex processes and competing priorities.
