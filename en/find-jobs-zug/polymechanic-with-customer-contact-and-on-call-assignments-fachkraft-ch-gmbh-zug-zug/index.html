Full description

You like to ensure the smooth functioning of technical systems through scheduled maintenance and troubleshooting. The company offers you comprehensive training and an environment that actively supports further training and work-life balance. We are looking for a job for our client:

  • Independent planning and execution of maintenance work according to a specified cycle
  • Rectification of faults
  • Execution of minor repairs
  • Interesting on-call operations
  • Customer communication
  • Requirements
  • Completed apprenticeship in the mechanical, electrical or hydraulic field (e.g. polymechanic, car mechanic, plant and apparatus engineer or electrician)
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally with customer contact
  • Impeccable manners and a high willingness to learn
  • Good knowledge of the local language, other languages are an advantage
  • Valid driver's license – you can't do without it Offer
  • 2-month induction with full pay in our Lift Camp for your optimal preparation for your new job.
  • Your own company car, which you can also use privately.
  • Compensation for your lunch, fair on-call compensation for on-call and the cleaning of your work clothes, which will of course be provided to you free of charge by us.
  • Excellent development opportunities: We live and breathe personnel development. We provide our employees with various national and international talent programs and support them in terms of time and money for training and further education.
  • Work-life balance: 40-hour week, 25 to 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours, part-time work and unpaid vacation.
  • Healthy working environment: High health and safety standards and in-house occupational health management, including social counselling and health promotion.
  • Employee benefits: Generous participation in public transport and fitness subscriptions, fleet discounts, discounted withdrawal of Reka money, discounted insurance premiums and various discounts.
