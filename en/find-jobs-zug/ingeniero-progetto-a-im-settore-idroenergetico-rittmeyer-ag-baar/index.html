Full description

Are you ready for an exciting challenge in the world of hydropower? We are looking for a motivated talent who is enthusiastic about innovative automation projects in hydropower plants. If you like to take responsibility, work solution-oriented and be a real team player, then we want to get to know you.

The Rittmeyer Group

rethink. automatic machines. innovate.

As part of the BRUGG Group, as a specialist, we supply our global customers with innovative measurement and automation solutions as well as data-based services with a focus on the energy transition, sustainability and infrastructure security.

What you can do with us

  • You create and develop plant-specific application software for automation and process control systems
  • You take over the testing of power plant plants in the test field, including customer acceptance on site
  • You are commissioning machine controls, turbine regulators, automatic water management systems as well as auxiliary and auxiliary systems, IT networks and operating systems in hydropower plants
  • You work closely with project management, service technicians and customers and suppliers
  • You provide a timely, economical and high-quality project implementation

What you bring

  • You have a technical education or study with an electrotechnical focus or have acquired appropriate knowledge through several years of practical experience
  • You are ready to actively participate in the commissioning of hydropower plants
  • Ideally, you will have several years of professional experience in automation technology, preferably in the areas of hydropower plants, switchgear and/or control technology.
  • You bring enthusiasm for demanding technical tasks and have a structured and solution- and practice-oriented working method
  • Your reliability, self-initiative, independence, teamwork skills distinguish you
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, other languages such as Italian or French are a plus
  • A willingness to travel of about 30% as part of on-site commissioning rounds off your profile

What we offer you

  • Jobs with a future: meaningful work in a future-oriented and environmentally conscious company with a long tradition.
  • Trusting work culture: DU culture, communication at eye level and open doors (up to CEO)
  • Flexibility and work-life balance: annual working time model, 5-6 weeks holidays, paid bridge days and non-statutory holidays (pre-cooking days), flexible working, home office, third place, part-time work
  • Mobility: Top location of our locations with motorway connection and public transport connection. Three locations in Switzerland: Baar, Avry and Camorino. Worldwide locations: Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Croatia, Italy, France, USA and Singapore
  • Workplace: Great office space with ergonomic workstations and modern IT infrastructure
  • Perks: From free fruit to discounted lunch menus, provided work clothes to the individual use of velos and pool vehicles
