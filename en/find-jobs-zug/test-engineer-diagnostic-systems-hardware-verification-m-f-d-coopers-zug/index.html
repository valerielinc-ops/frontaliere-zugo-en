Full description

Join an innovative engineering environment where you will contribute to the verification and validation of advanced diagnostic systems throughout the entire product lifecycle. In this hands-on role, you will play a key part in ensuring the reliability, quality, and performance of complex hardware components and laboratory automation solutions within a highly regulated and dynamic setting.

Key Facts

  • Start Date: ASAP
  • Duration: 12 months (extension very likely)
  • Workload: 100%
  • Location: Rotkreuz, Switzerland
  • Work Model: Primarily onsite, with up to 1 day per week home office for administrative tasks
  • Travel Requirement: None
  • Team Size: 10+ professionals
  • Industry: Medical Diagnostics / Life Sciences
  • Language: English required, German is a plus

Your Responsibilities

  • Create clear, comprehensive test cases and technical requirements based on system specifications and stakeholder needs.
  • Execute hands-on verification and validation activities on hardware components, laboratory instruments, manual output modules, and connected transport systems.
  • Perform complex test campaigns, including calibration and integration testing across various system components.
  • Analyze test results, document findings, report defects, and verify corrective actions.
  • Investigate root causes of technical issues in close collaboration with developers and cross-functional engineering teams.
  • Differentiate between actual hardware defects and operational or usage-related issues.
  • Identify edge cases and potential risks that may not yet be covered by formal specifications.
  • Support the setup, maintenance, and continuous improvement of compliant test environments and testing processes.
  • Contribute to quality, reliability, and performance improvements throughout the product lifecycle.

Qualifications

Must-Have Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Design, Digital Engineering, Data Science, or a related technical discipline, alternatively a completed technical education with relevant professional experience.
  • At least 1 year of hands-on experience (including internships) in hardware testing, instrument testing, verification & validation, or hardware development.
  • Strong mechanical engineering mindset with an intuitive understanding of how hardware and mechanical components interact.
  • Experience working within regulated environments such as MedTech, Pharmaceuticals, Automotive, Aerospace, or other quality-driven industries.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret complex test results and identify root causes.
  • Ability to work effectively in fast-changing environments with evolving priorities and test setups.
  • Excellent communication skills and fluency in English.

Nice-to-Have Qualifications

  • Background combining mechanical engineering, product design, digital engineering, data science, or similar interdisciplinary fields.
  • Experience with diagnostic instruments, laboratory automation, transport systems, or calibration testing.
  • Familiarity with quality metrics, data analysis, or trend identification within testing environments.
  • Knowledge of verification and validation processes across multiple product lifecycle stages.
  • German language skills.
