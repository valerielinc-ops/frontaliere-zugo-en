Sales & Administration Assistant

This position works out of our location in Baar, Switzerland in the Diabetes Core division where we are helping people with diabetes manage their health with life-changing products that provide accurate data to drive better-informed decisions.

Primary Objective of Position

  • Coordinates and executes all relevant sales and administrative tasks to ensure optimal support of General Manager ACH and Sales Manager CH.

Major Accountabilities

  • Ensures that all activities are performed in compliance with quality system requirements.
  • Position reports to the Business Unit Director and interacts closely with the Sales Manager, the Sales team and Marketing in compliance with Abbott Quality­ /Regulatory-/Medical-/Legal requirements.
  • Sales budget for sales area: Tracking & Reporting
  • CRM System: Administration of the customer base and analyses per sales area
  • Customer segmentation and targeting: Creation of customer lists & statistics
  • Sales Meetings and Training: Organization and Logging
  • Contracts, Sponsoring: Development and documentation according to compliance guidelines
  • Inquiries from physicians or partners: Processing or forwarding to the responsible office
  • Patient training: Organization, administration and reporting of KPIs, in cooperation with the sales force quotation system
  • Purchase orders for General Manager ACH & Sales Management projects: Create, Manage and Accruals
  • Samples: Orders, Tracking and Inventory
  • Assistant to General Manager ACH and Sales Manager Switzerland: agenda coordination; expenses, travel bookings, meeting coordination. Occasional travel bookings for sales team
  • Development or adaptation of project or strategy presentations based on inputs from General Manager ACH
  • Coordination of employee interviews, new entries and exits
  • Representation marketing assistance, or possibly for selective needs: organization of congresses, events, e.g.

Qualification

  • Concluded commercial apprenticeship
  • Good language skills, independent correspondence in German, French, English, Italian language is a plus
  • 4-5 years' experience in a similar position
  • Practical knowledge and understanding of administrative tasks in sales and marketing.
  • Committed, independent personality who keeps the overview even in hectic times
  • Knowledge of medical device industry and local market conditions
  • Strong organizational skills and ability to perform tasks independently and reliably
  • Knowledge of common office software (e.g. MS Office) is a plus
  • Good communication skills with external (HCP, patients, agencies) and internal partners is a plus
  • Knowledge of software programs for customer relationship management (CRM), accounting and ordering is a plus
