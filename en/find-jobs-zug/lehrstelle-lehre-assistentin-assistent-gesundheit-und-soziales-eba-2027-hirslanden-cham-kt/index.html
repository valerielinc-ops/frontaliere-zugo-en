Place of work: Hirslanden AndreasKlinik Cham Zug | Cham - Kt. Zug

Occupation by 1 August 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 71779

Andreasklinik Cham Zug is located in the heart of Cham – nestled in a quiet and beautiful environment. Characteristic architecture and stylish interiors meet modern medical infrastructure in a hotel-like environment. The clinic specializes in the musculoskeletal system with orthopaedics as well as neuro- and spinal surgery. In addition, it offers comprehensive regional health care with the specialist areas of gynecology including breast cancer medicine, urology, general surgery, general internal medicine and an emergency department open during the day and in the evening. First-class medical quality and the well-being of patients are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • You care for the patients and accompany and support them in their everyday life
  • You carry out housekeeping activities taking into account the hygiene principles and the clinic internal guidelines
  • You carry out logistical activities
  • You complete administrative tasks and support cooperation in the team

Your profile

  • Compulsory schooling completed
  • Motivation, commitment and commitment for a 2-year vocational training
  • Interest in working with people in need, good observation, patience, reliability, resilience, flexibility, empathy and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Readiness for irregular working hours and weekend services
  • Completed trial theory in the field of care

DOCUMENTS TO BE SUBMITTED

  • Letter of motivation and CV with current photo
  • Higher secondary school certificates and, where applicable, last education
  • Reports or confirmations of all trial internships, internships and employment certificates
  • Copy Result of Basic-Check, Multicheck or Signalling Test

In addition to an attractive workplace on the beautiful Zugersee, you will find a family clinic and a dedicated, experienced team. We attach great importance to a working environment that is characterized by mutual respect and appreciation and ensure that you feel comfortable with us. Our benefits include:

  • Free coffee, tea and daily fresh fruits
  • Healthy and inexpensive menus in the staff restaurant
  • Cool employee discount platform (often with e.g. 11% Zalando, Ikea vouchers or 40% Adidas)
  • Free entrances to the Hallenbad Röhrliberg, the Tierpark Goldau and the Verkehrshaus
  • Paid dressing time with a workload of 100%, four additional free days. With a reduced workload, the free days decrease accordingly

--> Further benefits for employees can be found on our website.
