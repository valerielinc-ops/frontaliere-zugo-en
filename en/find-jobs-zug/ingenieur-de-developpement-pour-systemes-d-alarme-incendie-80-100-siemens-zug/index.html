Together with our customers, we connect the real and the digital world. As one of the leading technology companies, Siemens employs around 6,000 people in Switzerland. Apply as a Development Engineer today – your team is looking forward to you! Your new tasks

  • Design and development of fire detection devices and platforms. The task range extends from pre-development and product specification to the introduction of production and support of products in the field
  • Bring your experience in hardware and embedded firmware development in the industrial environment to the team. In early project phases, you quickly create functional patterns and prototypes. In the implementation for mass production, you ensure reliability, costs and producibility. You master digital circuit design as well as analog sensors
  • Our products work networked, via fieldbus and radio, and find their image in the cloud. A broad technical understanding allows you to work successfully with the responsible teams
  • You work in a team with colleagues from development, production, purchasing, sales and customer support. In addition, you are in daily and intensive contact with colleagues in the global Siemens network
  • You put the goal achievement of the team first and take on a wide range of tasks. This assumes that you appreciate learning new things every day.
  • In agreement and if you are interested, there are many ways to take on further responsibilities Your talents and experiences
  • Bachelor or Master degree in Electrical Engineering or in a comparable field
  • Several years of professional experience in the development of electronic circuits and software for embedded systems (C, C++, Python)
  • Experience in development for series production: optimization for high-volume production, cost optimization, cooperation with purchasing and production
  • We presuppose high motivation, good communication skills, structured work and determination.
  • You are ready to take responsibility and look forward to new challenges
  • Intercultural teamwork in a global company you see as an enrichment
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing Your advantages
