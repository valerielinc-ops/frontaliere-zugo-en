Full description

We are looking for an independent and service-oriented personality in the Central Switzerland region (Zug/Luzern) to strengthen our team.

Tasks

As a travelling housekeeper: in a team you look after the buildings of our customers and their users in different regions. You actively ensure a clean appearance of the properties. Your responsibilities include the following activities:

  • General housekeeping work (e.g. change of lamps)
  • Technical tours on the objects
  • Carrying out minor repairs
  • Pictet service
  • Miscellaneous cleaning work on various areas (e.g. emptying waste bins, removing spider webs, cleaning public toilets, removing graffiti, etc.)
  • Assistance in special cleaning and removal operations
  • Gardening, environmental care, disposal, provision of containers
  • Customer contact person
  • Perform acceptances and maintain protocols
  • Deputy team members

Requirements

You call yourself a service provider: out of conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances.

You also have the following requirements

  • Basic craftsmanship, specialist:for business maintenance or building cleaning
  • Good computer user knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Cat B driving licence mandatory
  • High customer and solution orientation
  • Reliability, resilience and flexibility
  • Communicative personality and well-groomed appearance

We specialize in holistic facilities management services for every phase of the business real estate and area lifecycle. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve comfort in buildings. In this way, we minimize costs and create optimal conditions so that our customers can concentrate on their core business.

As a company of Equans Switzerland, the leading full-service provider for holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers and working together on the energy transition.
