Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Do you love variety, are flexible and like to take action? Then we offer you an exciting challenge as a logistics jumper for our locations in Central Switzerland – by agreement.

Look forward to a versatile working day in which you actively control the flow of goods and optimize processes. As an organizational talent, you alternately support the locations of Cham and Hägendorf and ensure smooth processes.

Task area

  • Your main task includes handling goods incoming and outgoings as well as checking delivery notes
  • Other tasks include picking, packaging and provision of our products (building materials and tools).
  • In addition, you help with loading and unloading the trucks and customer vehicles
  • You are responsible for inventory work at the Cham and Hägendorf locations
  • Occasionally you also support in our shops and keep the overview there too

Profile

If your heart beats faster in the task area described above and you bring the following skills, you are our desired candidate in:

  • Training as a logistician EFZ, logistics assistant EBA or craftsman with professional experience in logistics > Forklift certificate mandatory!
  • Experience in the construction materials trade is advantageous
  • Independent and thoughtful working methods as well as a high level of commitment
  • Flexible, resilient and physically fit personality that can deal with weather-related circumstances
  • Awareness of occupational safety and a high sense of responsibility
  • Reliable gum
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Willingness to travel between Cham and Hägendorf locations

We offer

  • a challenging, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where the emphasis is on active cooperation and at the same time self-employment
  • intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company
  • Support our employees in their professional and personal development
  • No shift and weekend use
  • 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits

We look forward to your complete application documents in electronic form.
