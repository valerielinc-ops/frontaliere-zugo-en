Their tasks shall include

  • Professional and personnel management as well as conceptual further development of the treatment center for acute and general psychiatry together with the care management
  • Promoting high quality of treatment, training and supervision of the team
  • Participation in background medical services
  • Active networking with our other locations as well as external cooperation partners

They bring it with them.

  • Specialist title Psychiatry and Psychotherapy, in-depth clinical experience in the field of acute psychiatry
  • Patient, team, and network-oriented work, integrative leadership style
  • Sound leadership experience at psychiatric institutions
  • Evidence-based working methods, high social skills, networked thinking and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
