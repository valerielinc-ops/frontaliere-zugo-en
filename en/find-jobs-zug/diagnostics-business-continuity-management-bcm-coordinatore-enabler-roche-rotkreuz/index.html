Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position In this role, you architect, establish, and continuously evolve the Business Continuity Management (BCM) framework across our Diagnostics division. Working in an environment rooted in trust, collaboration, and high performance, you partner closely with senior stakeholders and cross-functional teams to strengthen our enterprise resilience.

The Opportunity

  • Design and evolve frameworks: Architect, implement, and optimize a fit-for-purpose, harmonized BCM framework across Diagnostics, aligning Group tools, templates, and methodologies with divisional needs
  • Champion risk and resilience: Support and contribute to the broader Risk & Resilience framework, integrating Business Continuity, Risk Management, and Emergency & Crisis Management
  • Drive metrics and insights: Establish and deliver a transparent reporting process for BCM metrics and KPIs across leadership levels to enable informed strategic decision-making
  • Ensure governance and compliance: Guide divisional compliance with relevant international regulations (such as NIS2 and CER) and coordinate audit readiness across assurance lines
  • Build communities and capability: Create and maintain dynamic communities of practice across research, manufacturing, and commercial areas to share best practices, lessons learned, and continuous learning
  • Empower through training: Develop comprehensive training curricula and facilitate tabletop simulation exercises to ensure organizational readiness and operational confidence
  • Act as a trusted deputy: Represent the function as a deputy on key BCM topics and strategic steering initiatives

Who You Are

You are an empathetic, collaborative, and strategic leader who thrives on turning complex challenges into sustainable, reliable solutions. You believe in open communication, build partnerships built on trust, and bring a proactive mindset to global healthcare resilience. Furthermore:

  • Extensive professional experience designing, establishing, and maturing Business Continuity, Risk, or Crisis Management systems within complex, international environments (healthcare, diagnostics, or regulated industries preferred)
  • Strong working knowledge of enterprise resilience standards and frameworks (such as ISO 22301) and relevant regulatory landscapes
  • Proven ability to partner, communicate, and build rapport with senior management, board-level stakeholders, and cross-functional peers
  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business Continuity, Risk Management, Emergency Management, Business Administration, or a closely related field (or equivalent practical experience)
  • Professional fluency in English with exceptional interpersonal and consultative communication skills
