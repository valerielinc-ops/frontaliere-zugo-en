Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals and retailers).

Tasks

  • Active sales and consultancy activities
  • Compliance with the prescribed internal and legal QS and ASIB guidelines
  • Ensure cleanliness of your own work area
  • Identifying, capturing and forwarding customer needs
  • Ensuring freshness and presentation of goods
  • Ensuring the labeling according to the labeling concept
  • Ensuring 100% availability of goods (goods carrier, 2nd VP, action area)
  • Inventory recording according to specifications
  • Accept complaints and forward them accordingly
  • Check-out – scanning goods

Requirements

  • Completed training in the food industry (cook, baker, retail trade, butcher)
  • Reliable, efficient and correct work
  • Careful appearance and pleasure in customer contact
  • Enthusiasm for new challenges
  • Physical capacity
  • Enjoy working in a team
  • Appropriate knowledge of German
