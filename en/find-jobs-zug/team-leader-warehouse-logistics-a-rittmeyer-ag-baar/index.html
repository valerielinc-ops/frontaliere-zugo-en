Full description

Control systems, control systems and measurement technology from Rittmeyer are the basis for innovative solutions in energy and water supplies, hydropower plants and waste disposal plants worldwide. As an engineering and service company with the highest quality standards, Rittmeyer specializes in software and system integration for turnkey systems in the infrastructure sector. For our small warehouse & logistics team, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality who keeps an overview, gladly assumes responsibility and combines leadership with operational activity. You ensure smooth operations in the warehouse, continue to develop processes and ensure that material is in the right place at the right time.
