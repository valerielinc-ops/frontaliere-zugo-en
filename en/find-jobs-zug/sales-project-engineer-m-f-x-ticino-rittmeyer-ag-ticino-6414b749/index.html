Full description

For our seven-member inside sales team, we are looking for a committed personality who combines proactivity with precise working methods and fills the role with energy. In this position, you are in an interface function between our sales and operations, which is responsible for the execution of our projects. Do you have experience in plant construction, preferably in the water or wastewater industry and are automation systems familiar to you? We look forward to your application! A collegial team and a modern working environment awaits you.

The Rittmeyer Group

rethink. automatic machines. innovate.

As part of the BRUGG Group, as a specialist, we supply our global customers with innovative measurement and automation solutions as well as data-based services with a focus on the energy transition, sustainability and infrastructure security.

What you can do with us

  • You accept quote requests from the sale
  • You create implementation concepts, calculate the costs necessary for implementation and create commercial offers
  • You hold consultations directly with the customer and make any visit on site
  • For larger projects, you set up an internal offer team and lead it
  • You decide with Bid or No-Bid in agreement with the sale
  • You identify and document risks
  • You ensure a clean transfer of the project after order by customers to the operations team

What you bring

  • You will bring basic technical training, ideally in electrical engineering or water hydraulics
  • Experience in plant engineering is an advantage
  • You have professional experience in automation and control systems or in a similar field
  • You are distinguished by teamwork, communication strength, independent work and economic thinking
  • You have very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English is an advantage

What we offer you

  • Jobs with a future: meaningful work in a future-oriented and environmentally conscious company with a long tradition
  • Trusting work culture: DU culture, communication at eye level and open doors (up to CEO)
  • Flexibility and work-life balance: annual working time model, 5-6 weeks holidays, flexible working, home office, part-time work
  • Mobility: Top location of our locations with motorway connection and public transport connection. Three locations in Switzerland: Baar, Avry and Camorino. Worldwide locations: Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Croatia, Italy, France, USA and Singapore
  • Workplace: Great office space with ergonomic workstations and modern IT infrastructure.
  • Perks: From free fruit to discounted lunch menus, provided work clothes to the individual use of velos and pool vehicles
