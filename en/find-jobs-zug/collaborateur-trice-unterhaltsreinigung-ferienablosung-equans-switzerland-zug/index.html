To strengthen our security department, we are looking for a service-oriented, motivated and competent, team-capable and communicative personality.

Tasks

  • Occupation of the operation centre (3 shifts, 24 hours, 365 days)
  • Monitoring and operation of alarm, building control and parking systems
  • Receiving and forwarding calls on Picettstation and Helpdesk
  • Activation of service technicians, guards and blue light organisations
  • Recording of messages in various softwares (MS-Office, SAP, Planon)
  • Badge and key output

Requirements

You describe yourself as a service provider: with a technical flair and stylish knowledge of German in word and writing. You also have the following requirements:

  • Basic vocational training with further training and/or experience in guarding/security service beneficial
  • Good repute
  • Readiness for irregular working hours (shift operation with night and weekend inserts)
  • Psychological resilience in stressful situations
  • Good computer user knowledge
