Full description

Cham Swiss Properties is a real estate company based in Cham (ZG). We focus on the value-creating development of attractive living and working spaces at central locations with good public transport connections in Switzerland. Our team is building and expanding a high-quality, sustainable and diversified real estate portfolio. The skills of the approximately 70 employees of Cham Swiss Properties comprise the entire value chain of the real estate cycle. Cham Swiss Properties is listed on the Swiss stock exchange SIX Swiss Exchange.

Their tasks

  • Independent preparation of the quarterly, half-yearly and annual accounts of one to two companies according to Swiss GAAP FER and OR
  • Cooperation in consolidation (quarterly, half-yearly and annual accounts)
  • Support and representation of the accounts payable incl. Current payments
  • Support for accounts receivable
  • Accounting of installations (fixed assets)
  • Preparation of VAT accounts and annual votes
  • Preparation of forecasts, budgets and control of budget compliance
  • Support in the preparation of half-yearly and annual reports
  • Cooperation in the Internal Control System (ICS)
  • Miscellaneous analysis, reconciliation and account maintenance
  • Cooperation in projects (including digitalization projects)

Your profile

  • Commercial education, financial and accounting certificate or at least equivalent training
  • Several years of professional experience in a similar capacity, ideally in a real estate company
  • Responsible, structured and conscientious personality
  • Flexible, analytical and entrepreneurial way of thinking and acting
  • High commitment and commitment to demanding tasks
  • Ability to work calmly and efficiently even under time pressure
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good IT application knowledge (Microsoft range) and ideally experience with the accounting software Abacus

We offer

  • An exciting and future-oriented working environment
  • Individual promotion and training support
  • Modern workplaces with ergonomic equipment and small office units
  • Home office opportunities and flexible working conditions
  • Regular team events and a pleasant working atmosphere
  • Free parking for all employees
