Role Overview

The Operational Technology Engineer is a specialized strategic role focused on the design, deployment, standardisation, and governance of Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) - internally identified as Technical Information Systems (TIS). Acting as the critical bridge between TIS, ICS and the ERP, this role ensures the integrity, cyber-resilience and continuous availability of operational data across the global organization.

This role requires an innovative engineer to drive automation and digital transformation by serving high-fidelity data to advanced analytics, IIoT platforms, and optimization tools. The ideal candidate thrives in a high-performance culture, demonstrates a strong commitment to operational sustainability, and acts as a catalyst for continuous learning and technology adoption.

Main Responsibilities

The Operational Technology Engineer plays a critical role in standardizing and optimizing automation systems to capture, analyse, and leverage production data, driving operational efficiency across all industrial processes.

  1. Strategy & Standardization

• Collaborate in define and implement corporate-wide ICS/TIS standards to ensure global consistency in KPIs, reporting structures, and data architecture across 100+ plants • Align OT strategy in line with corporate guidance with business goals, identifying emerging technology needs and establishing multi-year technical roadmaps. • Drive innovation and industrial transformation: implement Plant of Tomorrow (PoT) solutions to improve efficiency, productivity, and competitiveness. • Train and mentor the OT plant teams: build high-performing operational plant teams through training and development, focusing on ICS and TIS systems. • Educate and advise plant OT related risks and opportunities • Support the product roadmap for new functionalities and features within the PCS/MES ecosystem. • Design and maintain robust interfaces between the shop floor (PCS/DCS), laboratory (LIMS), and business layers (SAP).

  1. Global Deployment & Operations

• Support the full lifecycle of ICS/MES deployments, from initial site assessment and vendor coordination to post-go-live support and version upgrades. • Ensure reliability of the ICS/TIS environment, coordinating with IT and external vendors. • Develop, optimize, and troubleshoot industrial data communication protocols and networking standards (e.g., Modbus, OPC UA/DA/HDA). • Optimize production data streams to improve overall process efficiency and support decarbonization/sustainability goals.

  1. Data Governance & Security

• Uphold the highest standards of data quality, integrity, and confidentiality to support "Smart Operations" and advanced analytics. • Collaborate with the Cybersecurity team to implement robust protective measures for OT data systems against evolving cyber threats. • Maintain comprehensive documentation for data architecture and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

  1. Leadership & Knowledge Management

• Design and execute global training programs to upskill plant-level PCS, TIS, PIMS, and LIMS champions. • Manage a centralized corporate knowledge base, including engineering standards, operational manuals and troubleshooting guides. • Influence senior stakeholders and plant management to ensure buy-in for digital initiatives and technical standards.

  1. Collaboration & Communication:

• Work cross-functionally with Maintenance, Process/Production, Quality Control, Corporate IT, and PM&E to ensure end-to-end system integration and alignment.

  1. Operational Management

• Oversee site-level OT performance, ensuring smooth, secure, and efficient multi-plant operations. Implement and enforce cybersecurity measures: protect OT systems from cyber threats and vulnerabilities through OT cybersecurity controls.
