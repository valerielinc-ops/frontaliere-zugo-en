Full description

Create the shopping experience at Migros! As an all-rounder* in sales/checkout, you advise our customers and ensure a positive sales experience with full shelves and fresh products. Your commitment and personality make the difference.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Lucerne, MMM Zugerland Steinhausen, Hinterbergstrasse 40, 6312 Steinhausen

Important information

Applications are only made with a complete dossier incl. employment certificates and diplomas are taken into account.

Mansioni

  • Disposal, presentation and maintenance of goods
  • Quality, data and freshness control
  • Customer service in compliance with the cash regulations and providing customer information
  • Settlement of money transactions with the branch office

Requirements

  • First professional experience is advantageous: in the retail trade
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • The joy of dealing with people
  • Self-employment and flexibility
  • Very good physical strength
  • Reliable and accurate working methods
  • German (very good knowledge)

Contatto

Application and contact Christina Erni HR consultant

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
