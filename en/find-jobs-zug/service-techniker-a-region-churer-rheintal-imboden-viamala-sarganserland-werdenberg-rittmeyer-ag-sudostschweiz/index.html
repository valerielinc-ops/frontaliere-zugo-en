Full description

To strengthen our team, in the area of services, we are looking for a team player with network affinity for Graubünden.

The Rittmeyer Group

rethink. automatic machines. innovate.

As part of the BRUGG Group, as a specialist, we supply our global customers with innovative measurement and automation solutions as well as data-based services with a focus on the energy transition, sustainability and infrastructure security.

What you can do with us

  • Installation and commissioning of sophisticated control systems for water and energy supply plants, hydropower plants and water disposal plants
  • Willingness to take on responsibility in the team and actively contribute to further development
  • Competent advice to our customers for the maintenance of the control systems
  • Service and maintenance work with expert support from our headquarters in Baar
  • You're ready to do picket service

What you bring

  • Successfully completed electrotechnical training (e.g. automatic technician, electric installer or electrical installer)
  • Ideally, several years of professional experience as well as further training, for example at the level of HF (Higher Technical School)
  • You bring affinity to PLC, PC and network skills
  • You have good user knowledge in Word/Excel and basic IT knowledge (network knowledge)
  • You are a team player and it distinguishes you great personal responsibility and an open personality
  • You have very good knowledge of German
  • You can reach your locations from your home.

What we offer you

  • Jobs with a future: meaningful work in a future-oriented and environmentally conscious company with a long tradition
  • Trusting work culture: DU culture, communication at eye level and open doors (up to CEO)
  • Flexibility and work-life balance: annual working time model, 5-6 weeks holidays, paid bridge days and non-statutory holidays (pre-cooking days), flexible working, home office, third place, part-time work
  • Mobility: Top location of our locations with motorway connection and public transport connection. Three locations in Switzerland: Baar, Avry and Camorino. Worldwide locations: Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Croatia, Italy, France, USA and Singapore
  • Workplace: Great office space with ergonomic workstations and modern IT infrastructure
  • Perks: From free fruits and discounted lunch menus to provided work clothes to the individual use of velos and pool vehicles
