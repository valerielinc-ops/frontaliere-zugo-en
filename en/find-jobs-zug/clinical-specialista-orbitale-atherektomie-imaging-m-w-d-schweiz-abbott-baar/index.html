Position You are a clinical specialist in the cardiac catheter laboratory and want to actively support the treatment of complex, highly calcified coronary stenoses? In this role you combine modern intravascular clear-cut-in-the-salary-in-the-alcohol therapies – from the orbital achectomy to the intravascular lithotripsia. Your goal: to precisely recognize lime, to evaluate the lesion optimally together with the treating team and to select the appropriate therapy for each patient.

Your tasks

  • Training of our customers - cardiologists and heart catheter laboratory personnel on products requiring explanation, in particular on our portfolio for lime detection (OCT-Calcium System "Ultreon,") and treatment (Agrectomy-System "Diamondback, and IVL).
  • Training, certification and documentation of customers on the above-mentioned products
  • Monitoring of on-site interventions in hospital
  • Implementation of training events and product-related projects
  • Processing of complaints
  • Reporting on training, projects and market developments
