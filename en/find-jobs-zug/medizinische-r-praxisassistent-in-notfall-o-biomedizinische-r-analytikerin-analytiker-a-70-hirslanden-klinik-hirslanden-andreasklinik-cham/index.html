Andreasklinik Cham Zug is located in the heart of Cham – nestled in a quiet and beautiful environment. Characteristic architecture and stylish interiors meet modern medical infrastructure in a hotel-like environment. The clinic specializes in the musculoskeletal system with orthopaedics as well as neuro- and spinal surgery. In addition, it offers comprehensive regional health care with the specialist areas of gynecology including breast cancer medicine, urology, general surgery, general internal medicine and an emergency department open during the day and in the evening. First-class medical quality and the well-being of patients are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • You are responsible for the daily, weekly and monthly quality checks of the POCT analysis devices in the emergency department. This includes maintenance, service, calibration and solving IT problems
  • Read and carefully check laboratory values in the M-KIS system to ensure correct data collection
  • You are the contact person for cooperation with Bioanalytica, organize the courier service for sample logistics and ensure a smooth process
  • Management and ordering of laboratory materials
  • Writing medical reports and taking on administrative tasks such as scanning documents, orders and telephone coordination
  • Independent performance of laboratory analyses in various specialist areas in accordance with working regulations, including timely validation of laboratory results and independent quality controls
  • Flexible working hours: possibility to work part-time (half-day)

Your profile

  • Completed training as biomedical/analyst HF or training as medical/practical assistant/EFZ
  • Emergency and hospital experience is beneficial
  • You are flexible, capable of teamwork and keep a cool head in stressful situations
  • Reliable and accurate work
  • High commitment and own initiative
