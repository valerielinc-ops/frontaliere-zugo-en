Full description

The Bossard Group is a leading strategic partner for industrial fastening and assembly technology solutions to OEM customers globally with proven expertise in engineereing and logistic services. Bossard was founded in Zug in 1831. The Bossard Group employs over 3,000 people in 33 countires world-wide and is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange. Choose Bossard as your employer and benefit from attractive employment conditions.

Project Manager Facility Management 100% (all genders) | Bossard AG

Actively shape the future of our location: You lead challenging facility projects, work closely with internal and external partners and gradually assume more responsibility with the prospect of taking over the management of facility management at the Zug location within 2 to 3 years.

Your tasks

  • Management and implementation of infrastructure, conversion, renovation and optimisation projects
  • Planning, control and monitoring of costs, deadlines and quality
  • Coordination of internal and external stakeholders, service providers and craftsmen
  • Cooperation in investment and maintenance planning and budget processes
  • Implementation of projects for the optimization and digitalization of facility management processes
  • Environmental responsibility at the Zug site
  • Further development of our sustainability and energy strategy
  • Support and representation of the Head of Facility Management

Your requirement profile

  • Technical or manual basic training with further training in the area of facility and project management (e.g. specialist in operation maintenance or building technician EFZ, maintenance specialist with a federal certificate and further training in facility management or similar)
  • Several years of experience in the management and implementation of facility management, conversion or infrastructure projects as well as in technical building management
  • Interest and know-how in the areas of sustainability, energy efficiency and ESG management
  • Entrepreneurial mindset, negotiation skills and assertiveness
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication personality with a high service orientation

Look forward to a versatile key role with great creative scope, exciting projects and a committed team. We look forward to your application.
