08.10.2026 Steinhausen

Become part of our team!

We, Raiffeisenbank Cham-Steinhausen, are a successful and innovative bank, which is one of the largest Raiffeisen banks in Switzerland. At our locations in Cham, Steinhausen and Mettmenstetten, we employ around 75 people, including 4 apprentices. For our modern advisory bank in Steinhausen, we are looking for a winning, service-oriented and committed personality.

What awaits you?

  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous customer portfolio support
  • Ensuring simple, standardized consultations on numbers, savings, creation and development of needs-based solutions taking into account the customer’s living situation as well as within the segment-specific / customer-specific product range
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in market development measures and customer events, representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking education or equivalent with banking experience
  • Enjoying customer contact with a high level of service and good communication skills
  • Enthusiastic and sales-oriented personality with a winning appearance
  • Flexibility and teamwork
  • Self-employed, responsible and goal-oriented working practices

You value direct customer contact and are looking for a varied and responsible activity? Do you like working in a team? We look forward to your application. A motivated and established team in modern premises awaits you. Silvia Emmenegger-Widmer Team Leader Asset Advice Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We live diversity and equal opportunities

We promote the different perspectives, experiences and competencies in our teams. Because this diversity makes us special and contributes decisively to our success.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.
