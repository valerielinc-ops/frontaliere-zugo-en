Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services for older people and employs around 4'000 staff. Outpatient and inpatient services blend seamlessly into one another. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is Switzerland's leading provider of private home care services.

For Senevita Casa Zug, we are looking for a qualified nurse with immediate effect, on a monthly or hourly salary, 50-100% - Ägerital and Menzingen region, with or without case management

Your important contribution

  • You play a key role in ensuring that our customers enjoy holistic, first-class and individual primary nursing care at home
  • You support relatives and the people receiving care, as well as the team, with all questions relating to care
  • You work in and coordinate interdisciplinary collaboration with doctors, health insurance companies and the team

This is how you enrich our team

  • You have tertiary-level training in nursing (HF, FH, AKP, DN II, also DNI and FA and -care)
  • Your flexibility and willingness to work make you a reliable support in the team
  • Your good spoken and written German skills help you communicate with the people around you
  • Your sense of responsibility and ability to recognise emergency situations and respond appropriately to them make you a valuable member of the team
  • Your cheerful nature is infectious and actively contributes to a positive workplace culture that delights our customers and enriches the working environment
  • You have completed the InterRAI HC course or are willing to complete it

What we are proud of

  • Our priority lies in personal customer care and continuity through assigned caregivers. In doing so, we focus on building long-term relationships.
  • We offer flexibility in scheduling assignments
  • You work independently and are still not alone – you have freedom in how you organise your work, as well as the exchange of experience and mutual support within the team
  • Senevita Akademie's comprehensive continuing education offering is free of charge for our employees
  • We also offer attractive employee benefits & partner discounts

Are you empathetic, and is caring for people your passion? Then you are exactly right for us!
