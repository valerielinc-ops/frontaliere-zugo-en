Original description

For our client Roche in Rotkreuz, we are looking for an MSAT Product and Process Science Engineer to support product care, process improvements and data-driven manufacturing activities in a regulated production environment.

In this role, you will work closely with production, engineering, quality and other internal stakeholders to ensure robust manufacturing processes for highly integrated sensor and cartridge products. The position offers the opportunity to combine scientific expertise, hands-on process understanding and data-driven problem solving in an agile MSAT environment.

Your Tasks

  • Take responsibility for regular product care activities within the squad
