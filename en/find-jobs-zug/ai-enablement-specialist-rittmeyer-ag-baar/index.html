Full description

Artificial intelligence opens up new opportunities for our employees, processes and business models. In order to turn potential into tangible added value, we are looking for a committed personality who empowers people, develops use cases and actively supports the responsible introduction of AI in the BRUGG Group. Do you want to translate AI opportunities into concrete solutions? Then you are right with us.

The BRUGG IT

rethink. automatic machines. innovate.

As a company of the Brugg Group, the BRUGG IT department of Rittmeyer offers infrastructure services for the entire international group of companies. This benefits three organizations with a total of 1500 end users. The central service desk is the first point of contact for our users and interface to the rest of the IT disciplines.

What you can do with us

  • You develop and establish a group-wide AI literature and enablement program for different target groups
  • You support departments in identifying suitable AI use cases, describing them in a structured manner and assessing them in terms of benefits, effort and feasibility.
  • You will moderate workshops, training sessions, use-case clinics and community formats and translate complex AI topics into understandable practice
  • You work closely with Lead AI Governance, AI Technology Lead, Business Units and other functions
  • Make successes, boundaries and learning experiences visible and promote the reuse of good solutions within the group
  • You measure reach, skills growth, adoption and business value and develop the program further based on data

What you bring

  • You have a completed degree or comparable qualification in business, business informatics, computer science, data/AI, organizational development, adult education or a related field
  • Several years of experience in at least two of the areas AI Enablement, Digital Transformation, Business Analysis, Process Management, Change, Training/Moderation or Product/Project Management.
  • You will moderate workshops, training sessions, use-case clinics and community formats and translate complex AI topics into understandable practice
  • Strong understanding of business processes and economic value, as well as an awareness of the importance of governance, data protection and information security in the responsible use of AI
  • Communication-strong, pragmatic personality with the ability to connect business, technology and governance and align AI with tangible business value
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

What we offer you

  • Jobs with a future: meaningful work in a future-oriented and environmentally conscious company
  • Trusting work culture: DU culture, communication at eye level and open doors (up to CEO)
  • Flexibility and work-life balance: annual working time model, 5-6 weeks holidays, home office, part-time work
  • Mobility: Top location of our locations with motorway connection and public transport connection. Three locations in Switzerland: Baar, Avry and Camorino. Worldwide locations: Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Croatia, Italy, France, USA and Singapore
  • Workplace: Great office space with ergonomic workstations and modern IT infrastructure
  • Perks: From free fruits and discounted lunch menus to provided work clothes to the individual use of velos and pool vehicles
