Full description

We are looking for a team-capable, service-oriented and friendly personality as a company medic immediately or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Maintenance services
  • Provision of first aid in the event of accidents and acute illnesses of employees and visitors
  • Independent decision on the need for access to external aid organisations or further treatment by a doctor
  • Management of the range of occupational safety products and medicines
  • Advising staff on health issues and personal problem situations
  • Vacation and sickness coverage of job-sharing work colleagues
  • Miscellaneous administrative tasks

Requirements

  • Completed training in a medical profession (MPA, nurse or similar) and min. 3 years of professional experience
  • First Aid Training (BLS)
  • Correct reputation (criminal register + enforcement register)
  • Good communication skills and stylish knowledge of German (verbal and written)
  • High social skills and flexibility
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
