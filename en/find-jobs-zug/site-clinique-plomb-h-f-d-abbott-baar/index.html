Our Global Clinical Operations team supports the successful execution of clinical trials across various Abbott business units. We work closely with trial sites, study directors and interdisciplinary teams to ensure the highest quality and compliance standards and advance innovative therapies for patients worldwide.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a Clinical Site Lead (m/w/d) Switzerland | Home office based

The Clinical Site Lead (CSL) assumes operational responsibility for conducting clinical trials at assigned trial sites. In this role, you will be the central point of contact for study sites and ensure that clinical trials are conducted in accordance with regulatory requirements, Good Clinical Practice (GCP) and internal Abbott guidelines. In addition, you will make a significant contribution to the achievement of recruitment goals, data quality and trial success.

YOUR TASKS

  • Independent supervision and management of trial sites within clinical trials
  • Central contact person for internal and external stakeholders at the assigned study sites
  • Identification, qualification and further development of suitable study sites and investigators
  • Implementation and coordination of site start-up activities in cooperation with the site operations teams
  • Ensuring timely activation of study sites and achievement of defined recruitment goals
  • Development and implementation of site-specific strategies to promote patient recruitment
  • Continuous evaluation of study performance and identification of potential for improvement
  • Implementation of site initiation visits, monitoring visits and close-out visits
  • Review study data and source documentation for completeness, accuracy, and quality
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, GCP guidelines, and study protocols
  • Support with audits, inspections and compliance activities
  • Ensure timely reporting of adverse events, product complaints, and protocol deviations
  • Analysis and solution of study- or site-related challenges as well as initiation of appropriate corrective measures
  • Training and regular refresher of study staff on study protocols and regulatory requirements
  • Ensuring complete and proper study and site documentation
  • Collaborate with Clinical Operations, Study Management, and other internal stakeholders to ensure study progress
  • Support in budget and contract processes in close coordination with the relevant departments
  • Regular communication of the study status and relevant developments to internal and external stakeholders

YOUR PROFILE

  • Degree in natural sciences, health sciences, engineering, nursing or a comparable field
  • Alternatively, comparable professional experience in the field of clinical research, cardiology or medical technology
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in Clinical Research, Clinical Operations or Site Management
  • In-depth knowledge of Good Clinical Practice (ICH-GCP) as well as relevant regulatory requirements and standards
  • Experience in conducting, supervising and monitoring clinical trials
  • Good knowledge of clinical trial design and research processes
  • Confident handling of MS Office and, ideally, experience with clinical database systems (e.g. Oracle)
  • Strong communication, organizational, and time management skills
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • High level of initiative and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Team-oriented personality with the ability to work successfully in an international matrix environment
  • Very good knowledge of medical terminology
  • Fluent in German and English, both written and spoken
  • Other European language skills are an advantage
  • High willingness to travel of approx. 70%, including international travel
