The Position The Global Social Media and Digital Communications team drives the overarching strategy, governance, and engagement across all brand touchpoints and online communities. Operating as the central connective tissue across direct markets, country clusters, and cross-functional teams, the department is dedicated to scaling customer reach, establishing global compliance standards, and ensuring unified, high-impact brand storytelling across the entire digital ecosystem. The Social Media Channel Manager is responsible for the overarching global strategy, governance, and performance of all social media channels and online communities. In this role, you will ensure that all social media initiatives align with broader business objectives while overseeing the operational team to scale customer reach effectively across platforms.

The Opportunity

  • Define and own the global social media and community building strategy, ensuring seamless alignment with global brand and compliance frameworks.
  • Establish governance for channel usage, setting organizational standards for customer engagement across platforms such as Facebook, YouTube, and X.
  • Drive social identity by synthesizing high-level social insights into actionable, long-term business strategies.
  • Manage global and affiliate social media budgets and high-level relationships with external agencies and vendors to support content development and platform management.
  • Oversee the global "engine room," coordinating efforts between direct markets and country clusters to ensure consistent brand storytelling.
  • Collaborate with senior leadership in customer service and product management to integrate social media into the broader customer journey.
  • Audit and optimize the global content calendar and publishing processes to ensure maximum quality and operational efficiency.

Who You Are

  • You demonstrate proven strategic mastery with the ability to design and implement end-to-end communication strategies for complex, global organizations.
  • You bring advanced analytics expertise, translating complex social insights into business-wide identity and growth strategies.
  • You possess demonstrated leadership and people management experience, successfully managing specialists and leading cross-functional squads or project teams.
  • You hold in-depth knowledge and compliance expertise regarding global and local compliance frameworks within regulated social environments.

Global Grade: SE7 - SE8
