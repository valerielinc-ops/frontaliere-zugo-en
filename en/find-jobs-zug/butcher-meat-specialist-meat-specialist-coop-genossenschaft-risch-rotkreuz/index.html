Metzger:in (meat specialist / meat specialist)

Coop Genossenschaft — Risch-Rotkreuz, ZG, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Specialty: Sales Food
  • Location: Coop, Lucerne Road 8, 6343 Risch-Rotkreuz
  • Pensum: 80 - 100%

Tasks

  • The open counter is our flagship and an important point of contact for our customers. You advise and serve them passionately at the buffet.
  • Freshly cut and dressed: You prepare the meat products professionally and attractively and also produce homemade products such as sausages.
  • You manage the goods in self-service and help them sell better.
  • Customer orders via the holiday business, but also outside, are also recorded and provided.

Requirements

  • You bring joy / flair in customer advice or service, ideally in the food sector, and are interested in meat and fish.
  • You also score with professional experience in a serviced butcher shop. Experiences as a cook (m/f/d) also help you in your daily consulting work.
  • You appreciate the close customer exchange and are happy to advise for your life.
  • You consistently implement quality specifications and guidelines.
