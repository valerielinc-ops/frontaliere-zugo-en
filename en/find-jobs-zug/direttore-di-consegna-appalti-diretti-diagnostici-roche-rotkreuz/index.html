The Position The Diagnostics Direct Procurement team is a vital function dedicated to driving strategic procurement initiatives and optimizing value for our operations and designated manufacturing network. We act as essential business partners to our sites, ensuring that all procurement activities adhere to category guidance, policies, standards, and procedures while continuously identifying and delivering overall savings and continuous improvement opportunities. As the Delivery Manager, you will oversee the end-to-end procurement process, which encompasses requirements analysis, supplier management - including negotiation, contracting, and performance monitoring - as well as handling escalations from business stakeholders. You will translate business needs into clear procurement specifications, utilize business enterprise tools like SAP, and collaborate closely with Procurement, Business stakeholders, and Category Management teams to execute optimal sourcing strategies, meet end-user needs, and leverage demand aggregation.

The Opportunity To provide a clear understanding of the role, here are the core responsibilities you will be managing:

  • Supplier Management: Responsible for managing supplier relationships, ensuring aligned partnerships and protecting Roche supply
  • Cost Optimization: Coordinate the sourcing process (RfIs, RfQs, RfPs) and leverage eSourcing tools, category guidance, and procurement knowledge to meet end-user requirements while driving maximum efficiency and value.
  • Drive Compliance & Continuous Improvement: Execute support requests using continuous improvement methodologies, assist in creating and reviewing purchasing documentation, and ensure strict compliance with all policies, standards, and operating procedures to maintain the organization’s right to operate.
  • Maintain Critical Information Flows: Proactively communicate the status and outcomes of procurement activities, and consistently submit operations data to Category Management teams to aid in developing new strategies, capitalizing on demand aggregation opportunities, and addressing high-value or non-standard requests.
  • Collaborate Across Functions: Partner effectively on an as-needed basis with relevant business support functions to drive operational efficiencies and deliver maximum value-add to the broader business.
  • Monitor Targets & KPIs: Track, monitor, and deliver on Direct Procurement Operations targets and customer service metrics, ensuring prompt escalation of any issues as they arise.

Who You Are

We are looking for a dedicated professional who brings the following qualifications to our team:

  • Education & Certifications: You hold a university degree in an engineering or business-related field.
  • Procurement & Industry Experience: You bring 3+ years of procurement experience—including contracting, negotiation, spot buying, and supporting cost reduction and value improvement programs—alongside specific experience in mechanical manufacturing processes (sheet metal fabrication, fabricated metal components, casting and plastic molding/injection). Experience in Life Sciences is a plus.
  • Systems & Technical Proficiency: You are highly proficient in business enterprise systems (e.g., SAP), with proven knowledge of systems and processes for the efficient management of contract documentation and templates.
  • Communication & Stakeholder Management: You possess excellent communication skills with a demonstrated ability to translate business requirements into clear procurement specifications, always keeping internal customer service top of mind.
  • Language Skills: You have business-level fluency in English. German is a strong plus.
