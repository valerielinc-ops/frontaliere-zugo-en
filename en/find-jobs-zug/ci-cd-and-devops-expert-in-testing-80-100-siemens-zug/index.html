  • Designing, implementing, and executing CI/CD strategies covering development, testing, release, update, and support processes for DevOps operations, across multiple projects including legacy software, cloud deployment and AI solutions
  • Designing, implementing, and orchestrating CI pipelines for automated tests, design, setup and maintain cloud deployment mechanisms using GitLab pipelines and AWS allowing for:
  • parallel deployments for developers
  • staged deployments for development, integration, and production environment
  • production grade availability and performance of asset
  • Design, setup and maintain observability tools to support in asset operations
  • Observe tools and services that provide inputs on asset health and security status, interpret and derive work-items
  • Contribute to test architecture and test strategy
  • Work and collaborate in global development and test teams
  • Be part of an agile development team, share and develop your knowledge and mentor other team members Your talents and experience
  • A master’s, or bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering or in a related field
  • Proficient in GitLab with regards to CI/CD
  • Experience in Cloud, Edge and IoT technology stack, including AWS, Python, JavaScript
  • Docker, Podman, Microservices, Linux
  • Experience in REST API testing
  • Have the technical skills to review, verify, and validate the software code developed in the project
  • Experience in test automation (GUI, API, E2E) in product, integration or system test
  • Familiar with test and development processes of agile software development projects: SAFe, Scrum
  • Proactive, motivated and committed person with a growth mindset, that likes to cooperate with other teams in a global setup and is willing to bring our solutions to the latest level of technology
