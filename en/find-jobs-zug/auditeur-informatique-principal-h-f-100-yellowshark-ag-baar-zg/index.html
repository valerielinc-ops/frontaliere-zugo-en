Our client is looking for a committed and experienced professional who specializes in auditing, analyzing and optimizing IT and data processes. In this position, you will be given the exciting task of developing innovative, data-driven audit approaches and actively contributing to the further development of the company's internal control systems. A particular focus here is on identifying optimization potential and sustainably improving internal processes. The company is characterized by a collegial and appreciative working atmosphere that provides room for creative ideas and independent work. In addition, you will be offered diverse development prospects, targeted continuing education opportunities and the chance to make a long-term contribution to a dynamic team and play a significant role in shaping the company's future.
