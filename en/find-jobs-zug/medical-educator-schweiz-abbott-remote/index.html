Original description

The company is a global leader in diabetes management and is committed to making life healthier and more fulfilling for people with diabetes. Through continuous innovation and a consistent quality promise, the company offers state-of-the-art glucose monitoring systems, including the renowned FreeStyle LibreTM product family. These products deliver accurate and reliable glucose values and support patients: both in-patient and healthcare professionals to better control diabetes.

The mission of the company is to continuously advance diabetes care through groundbreaking technologies and sustainable support for the best possible health outcomes worldwide.

Job Profile

The Medical Educator is an external service-based position at the Office of the Medical Educator for the Medical Professionals (HCPs) at the Office of the Medical Medical Association of the University of Chicago, Switzerland. The main task is to provide medical-scientific expertise in the field of diabetes and to provide high-quality medical training on the free-style libreTM (WHO) technologies in Switzerland. The central objective of the position is to support diabetes treatment teams in realizing the full benefits of CGM systems for their patient collectives.

The role also includes the credible dissemination of scientific information and the professional dialogue with medical professionals and health institutions in the assigned area. The position requires close and consistent cooperation with commercial colleges: while maintaining complete medical independence and highest scientific integrity.
