Full description

Clinic Adelheid is the rehabilitation center of Central Switzerland. We look after patients for further treatment after operations, diseases of the musculoskeletal system, nervous system and internal organs. The clinic with 140 beds and spacious therapy rooms is located in a dreamlike location above Lake Gerise and is a company of the non-profit company Zug. We are looking for our team at 1. April 2027 or by agreement one or more Psychologist 60 - 80 % Their main tasks

  • Diagnosis and psychotherapeutic treatment of inpatients and outpatients
  • Psychological discussions in disease processing of various clinical pictures mainly in the psycho-oncological and internistic field and in psycho-social stresses
  • Performing psychoeducational and therapeutic group therapies
  • active cooperation with experts from other disciplines and contacts with external bodies
  • Providing internal interdisciplinary training

Our desired profile

  • License or Master in Clinical Psychology
  • Completed or advanced psychotherapist training
  • Clinical experience
  • self-employed and responsible working practices

Your new job We offer you an interesting, versatile area of responsibility in a committed and collegial team. With our flat hierarchical levels, you are on the pulse of events and can actively participate. Attractive employment conditions (e.g. 27 days holidays, outstanding social benefits, inexpensive food) as well as personal development opportunities round off our offer. We look forward to meeting you personally. Mrs. Esther Kramer, Head of Therapies, is happy to provide you with further information by telephone 041 754 31 50. We look forward to your application.
