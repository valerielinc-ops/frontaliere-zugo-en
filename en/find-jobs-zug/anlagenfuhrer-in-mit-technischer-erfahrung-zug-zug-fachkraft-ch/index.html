Full description

The activity includes the operation and monitoring of production facilities. The emphasis is on smooth production and careful quality controls. You benefit from a modern environment and work in a dedicated team. For our client we are looking for one or more activities

  • operation and monitoring of production machines and plants
  • ensuring a smooth production process
  • performance of quality controls and documentation of the results
