27.10.2026 Baar

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Zug, with its locations in Baar, Zug and Walchwil, is a successful, dynamic bank with a high level of customer confidence and a strong presence in the population. In order to strengthen our customer consulting team, we are looking for an independent and committed personality as a customer consultant at our headquarters in Baar. You take responsibility for the welcome desk and are jointly responsible for the consulting and development of our team-supported customers.

What awaits you?

  • You are the host and first contact person for our customers
  • You advise our team-supported customers in the basic products "Savings, figures, investing & pensions"
  • You work in a top modern consulting bank and have a well-functioning and well-established team at your side

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training
  • Experience in customer advice of a bank
  • Communicative and open personality, pleasure in customer contact and ability to keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • Anchorage in the Canton of Zug is advantageous

Do you like it lively? Our team is young, dynamic and wants to make a difference – we are looking forward to you. Karin Rubisch Head of Customer Consulting Baar Why Raiffeisen?
