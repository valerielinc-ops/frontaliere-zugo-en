Full description

For an established, innovative and nationally active trust company that has successfully specialized in advising customers from the medical sector, we are looking for motivated reinforcement. In a highly specialized environment, you take on varied mandates and benefit from exceptional working time models. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Finance: management of financial accounts, accounts payable and accounts payable to sole proprietorship and legal entities.
  • Taxation: preparation of tax returns for natural and legal persons, including intercantonal and international tax exemptions.
  • Support & Contact: Completion of general trust work as well as the professional, direct exchange with customers and authorities.

This is what you bring with you

  • Foundation: Completed basic commercial training (ideally in the escrow sector) as well as in-depth professional experience in the escrow industry.
  • Competence: You are absolutely certain of the financial statements and balance sheet
  • Professional training is a clear advantage.
  • Working method: An independent, structured and reliable working method characterized by a friendly appearance and pleasure in customer contact.
  • Skills: Safe handling of MS Office applications as well as very good knowledge of German in words and writing.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • A modern 40-hour week divided into a 4.5-day week (free on Friday afternoons) as well as flexible working hours and home office options.
  • Holidays & provision: 5 weeks of holidays plus an additional day off for your birthday as well as an above-average BVG preventive care solution.
  • Culture & Benefit: A motivated, collegial team, full support in your further education, a good public transport connection and free employee parking on site.
