Full description

Do you want to take on responsibility, lead a strong team and actively develop the electric section in Zug? At Equans, you can expect a wide range of tasks, exciting projects and the opportunity to contribute your professional experience and leadership skills in a future-oriented environment. As Head of the Electric Division, you are the central contact person for your team, our customers and internal interfaces. You ensure that projects are implemented qualitatively, economically and safely – with a clear focus on service quality, team development and sustainable success.

You can move that with us.

  • You take responsibility for the successful implementation of all electrical projects and services in the electric sub-area in Zug.
  • You lead, promote and motivate your team and develop the employees professionally and personally.
  • You ensure compliance with labor, safety and quality standards.
  • You control and monitor project implementation including budget, cost control and relevant key figures.
  • You maintain existing customer relationships and support the expansion of new customer contacts.
  • You implement standards, processes and improvements in the electrical sector.
  • You promote occupational safety and ensure that training and awareness-raising are carried out in the team.
  • You support in contract audits and ensure the professional implementation of service contracts.

That's what makes you.

  • Completed vocational training as an electrical installer EFZ.
  • Professional certificate as electrical project manager* in installation and security is absolutely necessary.
  • Training in the field of safety is mandatory.
  • Several years of experience in the planning and realization of electrical plants.
  • Leadership experience or strong leadership competence with high social competence.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and a good understanding of business connections.
  • Secure handling of current standards, MS Office and industry-standard tools; SAP knowledge is an advantage.
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • English is beneficial.
  • Confident appearance, analytical thinking and high results orientation.

That's what we offer you.

  • A responsible leadership role at Equans Switzerland.
  • room for manoeuvre in the further development of the sub-area Electric in Zug.
  • Exciting projects in an innovative and future-oriented environment.
  • A dedicated team with strong cohesion.
  • Attractive employment conditions and development opportunities.
  • Are you ready to advance the electric section with your experience, expertise and personality? We look forward to meeting you.
