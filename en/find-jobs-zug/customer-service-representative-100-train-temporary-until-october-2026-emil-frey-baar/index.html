What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not just a Swiss family business with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation, and customer orientation are the pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessory parts, as well as the provision of financial services. Herold Fahrzeugvermietung AG is the licensee of Hertz for the Swiss market. Hertz is one of the most well-known brands in the world and stands globally for an excellent car rental offer. Your tasks

  • You receive the customers and support them in choosing the right vehicle.
  • You complete the car rental formalities with them and advise them on our additional services.
  • You also plan the reservations, organize the delivery of the vehicles, and complete the return of the vehicles.
  • Do you want to convey to the customer a positive impression of their stay in Switzerland and an excellent service at the Hertz counter? Then you're right with us. Learn more also on our website www.hertzcareer.ch. Your profile
  • You love dealing with customers and a lively atmosphere.
  • Service performance and customer satisfaction are at the center of your activities.
  • In addition to your work experience in customer service or in a sales function, you can also communicate in English.
  • You have been in possession of a driver's license for motor vehicles for more than a year.
  • You manage hectic situations with constant friendliness and calmness.
  • You are a dynamic person and are used to working independently.
  • As a flexible employee, you like irregular working hours (also on weekends). What we offer There is only the possibility of an extension or a permanent employment after the temporary duration until the end of October 2026. You will find with us a new challenge with exciting tasks and much more:
  • Numerous benefits (new and used cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels, and much more)
  • Your own, attractive pension fund as an independent provident institution
  • A long-term corporate perspective
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • At least 25 days of vacation Hertz Switzerland practices a team-oriented culture and unites in the company various nations and cultures.
