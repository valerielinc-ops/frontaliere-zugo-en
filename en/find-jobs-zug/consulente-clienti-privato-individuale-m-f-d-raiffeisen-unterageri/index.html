Become part of our team!

For the Raiffeisenbank region Ägerital-Sattel, a successful, modern and regionally oriented bank, we are looking for an independent, solution- and service-oriented personality as a private customer consultant for the market in Ägerital, Sattel and Rothenthurm. Do you like to share your creative ideas, advise customers with passion and take on responsibility in a small, successful team? Then you are golden with us. We are looking for you to strengthen our PKI team immediately or by agreement.

What awaits you?

  • Recognizing and exploiting the customer potential through active and continuous support of the personal customer portfolio
  • Ensuring competent and holistic advice across all phases of life
  • Building up its own customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Development of suitable solutions in the areas of basic products, digital offers, living, financing, provisioning and investing, taking into account the individual wishes, goals and living situations of the customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures, in particular campaigns and performance of representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking education incl. Further training in banking or finance (SAQ certification individual client advice is advantageous)
  • Independent, committed and goal-oriented working method as well as enjoying intensive customer contact
  • Customer-oriented personality with consulting, negotiation and sales skills
  • Motivated, team capable and flexible personality
  • Salesmanlike flair and likeable, convincing, safe and winning appearance

You inspire people, recognize opportunities and want to accompany customers in all phases of life? I look forward to meeting you personally. Andrea Anna Oeuvray Head of Private Client Consulting Why Raiffeisen?
