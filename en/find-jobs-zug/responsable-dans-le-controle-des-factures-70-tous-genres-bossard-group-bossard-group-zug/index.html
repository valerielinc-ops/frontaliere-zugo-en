Invoice Control 70% (all genders) | Bossard Group

In this central role, you ensure that our flows of goods are right. You check invoices, keep data up to date and are the central interface between purchase, receipt of goods, sales and freight forwarders. Your commitment makes the difference!

Your tasks

  • Recording, preparation and control of physical invoices
  • Maintenance and updating of data
  • Payment clearances
  • Contact person for purchases, receipts, sales and forwarders
  • General administrative activities
  • Holiday representation: During around 10 weeks per year, you take on an increased workload of 80 - 100% in order to optimally support the team during this time

You bring that with you

  • 5 years of experience in the office environment, commercial education beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, basic knowledge of English
  • Safe handling of PC applications and a pronounced number flair
  • Transport and independent work
  • Flexibility, team spirit and a positive attitude
  • Responsibility and good communication behaviour
