Full description

Control systems, control systems and measurement technology from Rittmeyer are the basis for innovative solutions in energy and water supplies, hydropower plants and waste disposal plants worldwide. As an engineering and service company with the highest quality standards, Rittmeyer specializes in software and system integration for turnkey systems in the infrastructure sector. For our small team Purchasing and Logistics we are looking for a committed and communicative personality who likes to take responsibility and keep an overview. In this versatile function, you are in daily contact with suppliers, customers and internal contacts and make an important contribution to smooth processes in the company. You like to take part and make sure that planning becomes smooth processes? We look forward to your application! Purchasing (a) Baar, Switzerland 100%

  • Your responsibilities You are responsible for the entire operational purchasing process, from the receipt of an offer to the invoice release You collect offers, carry out order planning and trigger orders You monitor order confirmations and delivery dates and ensure that agreed dates are met You coordinate differences adjustments and clarify open questions with internal and external contacts You are in close contact with the project managers and the suppliers to ensure a smooth process You check and release supplier invoices and ensure correct maintenance of the purchase master data
  • What you bring with you You have a successfully completed commercial training, ideally with further training to become a purchasing specialist You bring several years of experience in operational purchasing and export with you are well versed in dealing with Navision or Business Central and you move safely in digital processes You are characterized by flexibility and a positive, service-oriented attitude You keep an overview even in high workloads, set priorities and act solution-oriented You communicate in German and English, both in writing and verbally
  • What we offer you A very interesting job in a modern and environmentally conscious company with a motivating and collegial work culture. Our flat hierarchies and short decision-making processes bring us quickly and efficiently to our jointly developed goals. You can expect attractive employment conditions from a well-positioned company in a future-oriented market environment. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application!
