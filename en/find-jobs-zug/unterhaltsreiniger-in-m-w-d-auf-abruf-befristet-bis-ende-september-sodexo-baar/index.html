Full description

Sodexo was founded in Marseille in 1966 by Pierre Bellon and is the world's leading provider of sustainable company catering and special experiences in all areas of life. With its two business areas, Food and Facilities Management Services, Sodexo meets all the challenges of daily life and pursues a dual goal: to improve the quality of life of our employees and the people we serve. Growth and social commitment go hand in hand. Sodexo’s goal is also its motto: “to create a better everyday life for everyone to build a better life for all.” We are the only provider in Switzerland to specialize in facility management and catering services. We pursue a holistic approach for our customers, in which we focus on the infrastructure, people and their well-being, as well as the environment. Our approximately 650 employees work in the areas of cleaning, catering, corporate catering, logistics, security and reception services, conference services and technical building management. We always strive to make the everyday lives of people in front of and behind the scenes better with our actions and thoughts. Would you like to work for one of the largest family businesses in the world? Then become part of our team and apply for this exciting task.
