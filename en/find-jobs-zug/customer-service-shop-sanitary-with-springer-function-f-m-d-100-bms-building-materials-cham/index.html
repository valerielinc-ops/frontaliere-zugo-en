Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell construction and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Want a change in everyday work? For our richner shops in the Mitte region, we are looking for a committed personality for flexible use at different locations.

With us, you play a key role by being flexible in different areas of the shop. Whether customer advice, sales, presentation of goods or administrative tasks – it never gets boring with us.

If you enjoy taking on new challenges, are team-oriented and want to advance your career, apply now. Immerse yourself in an exciting working environment and shape your future with us!

What are you waiting for? Become part of our team and bring your energy and creativity to our shop.

Task area

  • Responsibility for part of the sales area Mitte as an employee with cross-location deployment
  • Technical and sales advice to customers
  • Preparation and post-processing of offers, collection, delivery and returns notes
  • Entry and exit handling and control of delivery notes
  • Picking and provision of our products
  • Direct contact person for our pick-up customers

Profile

If your heart beats faster in the task area described above and you bring the following competencies, you are our desired candidate:

  • You have several years of professional experience in the sanitary and service sector
  • Training as a logistician EFZ would be advantageous
  • You have a valid ticket and your own car
  • A forklift card is desired, but not mandatory
  • You bring great passion for sales and advice
  • You distinguish high flexibility as well as motivation and drive to move the business forward
  • Your plus is the independent and structured working method as well as a resilient and resilient nature
  • Reliable gum
  • We require a good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as computer skills

We offer

  • a challenging, versatile and interesting job in a good working environment in which active cooperation and self-employment are emphasised
  • Intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company
  • Support our employees in their professional and personal development
  • No shift and weekend use
  • Basic wage Gross x 13 plus variable wage share and fixed fees
  • 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits

We look forward to your complete application documents in electronic form.
