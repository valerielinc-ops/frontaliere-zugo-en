Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With our services, we set a benchmark in financial consulting. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for support in customer consulting at our central location in Zug.

  • You work closely with an experienced financial consultant. Every day you learn something new and put it into practice.
  • They accompany our consultants in meetings and support them in the care of asset management customers.
  • They prepare the discussions and process the results.
  • In the team, you develop individual concepts that take all financial aspects into account. They are looking for the best solution and help with the implementation.
  • You have completed your commercial apprenticeship and gained your first professional experience at a bank.
  • They are interested in financial planning, stock markets and capital markets.
  • They enjoy contact with customers, communicate well and gladly, are eager and motivated.
  • You want to prove yourself in a performance-oriented environment and gradually assume responsibility.
  • They work in an uncomplicated and open environment.
  • You can help shape and develop a unique company.
  • We support you individually and support you in the context of the job profile also in an external further education, for example at a university of applied sciences or in a specialist training with a federal degree.
  • Our surveys on satisfaction regularly show that our employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop.
