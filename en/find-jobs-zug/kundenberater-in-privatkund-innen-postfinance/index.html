Full description

At PostFinance, our customers are the focus. As a customer advisor: you know what people really need around financial issues. You accompany them with competent advice at eye level - whether digital, on the phone or on site. You will become part of a dynamic and ambitious team with extensive financial and insurance experience. Together we shape the future of consulting: personally, relevantly and with effect.

You can do that.

  • You take responsibility for your own customer portfolio by expanding it and maintaining customer relationships.
  • You will conduct holistic consultations around the submarkets of numbers and savings, investing, pensions and financing.
  • You reconcile customer appointments and shape your everyday life with great autonomy.
  • You contribute your ideas and achieve ambitious goals together with the team.
  • You develop your knowledge in a targeted way, especially in the topics of investing, provisioning and financing, and actively share your know-how in the team.

You bring that with you

  • Experience in pension and investment advice as well as enthusiasm for financial topics
  • An open, empathetic appearance that creates trust and focuses on customers
  • Motivated, reliable and independent personality with Macher-Mindset
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are an advantage
