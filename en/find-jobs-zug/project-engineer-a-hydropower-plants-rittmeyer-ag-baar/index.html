Full description

Control systems, control systems and measurement technology from Rittmeyer are the basis for innovative solutions in energy and water supplies, hydropower plants and waste disposal plants worldwide. As an engineering and service company with the highest quality standards, Rittmeyer specializes in software and system integration for turnkey systems in the infrastructure sector. Are you ready for an exciting challenge in the world of hydropower? We are looking for a motivated talent who is enthusiastic about innovative automation projects in hydropower plants. If you like to take responsibility, work solution-oriented and be a real team player, then we want to get to know you. Project Engineer (a) Hydropower Plants Baar, Switzerland 100% You create and develop plant-specific application software for automation and process control systems You take over the testing of power plant plants in the test field, including customer acceptance on site You make commissioning of machine control systems, turbine controllers, water household automations as well as auxiliary and auxiliary systems, IT networks and operating systems in hydropower plants You work closely with project management, service technicians and customers and suppliers You provide a timely, economical and high-quality project implementation What you bring with you You have a technical education or study with an electrotechnical focus or have acquired appropriate knowledge through several years of practical experience. You are ready to actively participate in the commissioning of hydropower plants Ideally, you will have several years of professional experience in automation technology, preferably in the areas of hydropower plants, switchgear and/or control technology.

  • Your reliability, self-initiative, self-employment, teamwork and ability to speak and write in German, other languages such as Italian or French are a plus A willingness to travel of about 30% in the context of commissioning on site rounds off your profile. We are characterized by our DU culture, already from the application process. Our flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths bring us quickly and efficiently to our jointly developed goals. You can expect attractive employment conditions from a well-positioned company in a future-oriented market environment. Interested? We look forward to your application!
