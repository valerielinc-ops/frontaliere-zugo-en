Full description

Cham Swiss Properties is a real estate company based in Cham (ZG). We focus on the value-creating development of attractive living and working spaces at central locations with good public transport connections in Switzerland. Our team is building and expanding a high-quality, sustainable and diversified real estate portfolio. The skills of the approximately 70 employees of Cham Swiss Properties comprise the entire value chain of the real estate cycle. Cham Swiss Properties is listed on the Swiss Stock Exchange SIX Swiss Exchange.

Their tasks

You develop the strategic purchasing of construction and planning services into a central value-added function and ensure professional contract management. Together with our client representatives, you accompany the project teams through the entire procurement process, create market transparency and ensure systematic, market-driven and legally compliant procurement at the best possible conditions. You ensure compliance with procurement standards and conduct price and final negotiations for major awards.

  • Establishment and further development of company-wide procurement for construction and planning services and definition and assurance of uniform standards, templates and processes
  • Support of the client representatives and project teams in submissions, offer evaluations, award recommendations and economic procurement decisions
  • Preparation, examination, coordination and structured filing of planner, entrepreneur, framework and contracting contracts as well as development and negotiation of company-wide framework and purchasing agreements
  • Procurement market research, market analysis, benchmarks and plausibility checks to ensure consistent competition among entrepreneurs and planners
  • Leading or accompanying price, contract and final negotiations in strategically important awards
  • Advising building owner representatives on contractual issues as well as identifying and evaluating contractual opportunities and risks
  • Establishment and maintenance of an efficient entrepreneur, planner and supplier network
  • Introduction of key figures and instruments for measuring success as well as continuous identification and realization of savings and optimization potential

Your profile

  • HF/CAS level training in construction management or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in strategic purchasing, procurement and/or contract management, preferably in the area of construction and planning services
  • In-depth knowledge of SIA standards
  • Sound understanding of construction costs, work contracts and procurement processes
  • Proven success in price and contract negotiations as well as pronounced cost awareness
  • High communication skills, social competence and enjoyment of working with internal project teams
  • High commitment and commitment to demanding tasks
  • assertiveness, tenacity and negotiating skills while at the same time high team orientation

We offer

  • Design room for manoeuvre in setting up and further developing strategic purchasing and contract management
  • A dynamic and professional environment with short decision paths
  • Individual promotion and training support
  • Modern workplaces with ergonomic equipment and small office units
  • Home office opportunities and flexible working conditions
  • Regular team events and a pleasant working atmosphere
  • Free parking for all employees
