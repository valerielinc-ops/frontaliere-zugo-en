Your tasks

  • Support and coaching of teams and key people in an agile organizational environment
  • Support for specialist and product managers in the further development of their roles and working methods

  • Knowledge of Lean and Change Management
  • Strong communication and advisory skills
  • High level of independence and personal responsibility
  • Structured, solution-oriented and targeted working method
  • Empathy and sensitivity in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Conflict ability and ability to perform convincingly even in challenging situations
  • Flowing in German and English
