Full description

Do you want to take on responsibility, lead a strong team and actively develop the electric section in Zug? At Equans, you can expect a variety of tasks, exciting projects and the opportunity to contribute your professional experience and your leadership skills in a future-oriented environment.
As Head of the Electrical Division, you are the central contact for your team, our customers and internal interfaces. You ensure that projects are implemented qualitatively, economically and safely – with a clear focus on service quality, team development and sustainable success. Tasks: • You take responsibility for the successful implementation of all electrical projects and services in the electric sub-area in Zug. • You lead, promote and motivate your team and develop the employees professionally and personally. • You ensure compliance with labor, safety and quality standards. • You control and monitor project implementation including budget, cost control and relevant key figures. • You maintain existing customer relationships and support the expansion of new customer contacts. • You implement standards, processes and improvements in the electrical sector. • You promote work safety and ensure that training and awareness-raising are carried out in the team. • You support in contract audits and ensure the professional implementation of service contracts. Requirements • Completed vocational training as an electrical installer EFZ. • Professional certificate as electrical project manager* in installation and security is absolutely necessary. • Training in the field of safety is mandatory. • Several years of experience in the planning and realization of electrical plants. • Leadership experience or strong leadership competence with high social competence. • Entrepreneurial thinking and a good understanding of business connections. • Secure handling of common standards, MS Office and industry-standard tools; SAP knowledge is an advantage. • Very good knowledge of German; English is beneficial. • Confident appearance, analytical thinking and high results orientation. We specialize in holistic facilities management services for every phase of the business real estate and area lifecycle. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve comfort in buildings. In this way, we minimize costs and create optimal conditions so that our customers can concentrate on their core business. As a company of Equans Switzerland, the leading full-service provider for holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers and working together on the energy transition.
