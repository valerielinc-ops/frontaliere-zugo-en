Full description

VZ Banque de Dépôt is owned by VZ Group, Switzerland's leading independent financial services provider. Securities are our passion: we ensure that our customers are able to carry out their transactions efficiently and offer them attractive conditions. VZ Banque de Dépôt is growing strongly, and we are constantly looking for committed people with strong communication skills.

  • You advise our private customers by telephone on the services of VZ and VZ Financial Portal.
  • You support our clients in the activation and use of our digital services and the VZ Financial Portal.
  • You identify their needs and arrange appointments with our branch advisors.
  • You support our advisors in French-speaking Switzerland for all questions about our banking services and related to the VZ Financial Portal.
  • You work closely with the teams at our headquarters in Zug.
  • You continually develop your knowledge, especially in the field of digital banking.
  • You contribute to the development of VZ Banque de Dépôt by participating in continuous improvement projects, aimed at simplifying our processes and working methods.
  • Business learning completed, ideally in banking
  • Customer outreach and clear and professional communication in French
  • Good knowledge of German for internal communication (indispensable)
  • Interest in digital technologies and digital banking services
  • Availability for occasional trips to Zurich or Zug
  • Customer orientation, accountability, initiative and teamwork
  • Interest in automation and artificial intelligence to optimize our working methods.
  • With us, you can bring your ideas and develop in a dynamic environment.
  • Thanks to the VZ Bank Academy, you will deepen your banking skills and benefit from individual support.
  • We support your professional development through external continuing training.
  • Our strong growth offers you many opportunities to shape your career with us.
  • Our satisfaction surveys regularly confirm that our employees regard VZ as an extremely positive working environment. Experience it, too.
