The Position

The Employee Counseling Team of Roche Diagnostics International AG is part of Roche’s newly created Local People Care Solutions organization. It is committed with passion and dedication to the well-being and health of our employees throughout Switzerland. In this responsible role as Employee Counselor / Case Manager (80%) (m/f/d) you actively support the performance of our team in a trusted environment. Become part of our self-organized team and actively shape a healthy, inclusive working environment.

Their tasks

  • They advise and accompany employees confidentially and sensitively in complex professional and private life situations.
  • They support line supervisors and people & culture specialists (HR) in a solution-oriented manner in challenging issues.
  • You actively control the entire case management process for successful reintegration after extended absences.
  • They independently take over the entire administration and network all internal and external stakeholders efficiently.
  • They maintain an active, professional exchange with the relevant Swiss social insurance companies.
  • They work proactively and at eye level in a modern, self-organized team environment.
  • They are actively involved in site-specific care team assignments and internal projects.

Who you are

  • Education & further education: You have completed university studies in psychology or social work as well as further education in solution-oriented consulting or case management.
  • Professional experience: You have several years of profound experience in the consulting or case management area, ideally in the Swiss context.
  • Language competence: You communicate stylishly in German (level native language) and have excellent English skills in word and writing.
  • Personality: They are characterized by high flexibility, a reflected feedback culture and negotiating skills in dealing with different stakeholders.
  • Working methods: They value administrative accuracy (Google Suite/MS Office) and bring a healthy dose of humor and enjoyment of teamwork.

This position is for the time being limited to one year, with the option of permanent employment.
