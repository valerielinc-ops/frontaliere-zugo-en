Technology is your passion and you like to take it on yourself? Do you appreciate direct contact with customers and ensure smooth operation of the building? Then you are exactly right with us. You can move that with us.

You describe yourself as a service provider: in the innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances.

  • Completed training as a specialist for maintenance, housekeeper or basic training in the field of HLKSE
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function
  • Sound technical understanding and craftsmanship
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method

That's what makes you.

You manage several properties on site and are the contact for our customers in the field of building technology. Your responsibilities include the following activities:

  • You look after objects on site and are the first point of contact for our customers and users.
  • You ensure the technical and infrastructural operation of the properties.
  • You carry out repair and maintenance work on house technical equipment and facilities.
  • You coordinate and monitor service and maintenance work on heating, ventilation, air conditioning and sanitary facilities.
  • You create offers and support in the implementation of customer orders.
  • You participate in the planning and realization of projects.
  • You take care of piquetting services and contribute to trouble-free operation.

That's what we offer you.
