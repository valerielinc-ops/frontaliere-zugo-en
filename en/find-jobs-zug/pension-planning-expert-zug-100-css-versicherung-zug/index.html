Full description

You have a good sense of people and their needs. And you bring valuable experience in preventive counseling and have a healthy ambition. Take advantage of this unique opportunity and participate in our new business area "Life" right from the start. You support our agencies on site and strengthen the customer advisors through targeted coaching in lead generation. You focus on proactively advising our customers for our pension products. The customer advisors of the general agency support you with leads from our broad customer base. From us comes a first-class employment package, a top internal education and training and the flexibility so that you can put your job and private life on the line. Let’s talk about a common future.

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • You work on the leads of your general agency and as a coach you support the consultants in their professional and sales development.
  • You maintain existing relationships and actively win new customers for private provision.
  • You conduct preventive consultations personally or remotely and develop individual solutions that you sell successfully.
  • You’ll strengthen your commitment to existing customers, identify sales opportunities and work closely with colleagues to leverage these cross- and upselling potentials.
  • For valuable customer interactions, you use modern tools and present us with suitable marketing and sales measures.
  • You also answer the concerns and questions of our customers in partnership and with a lot of empathy.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You have completed vocational training and have successfully received the VBV certification. If you also have a further education such as dipl. Financial advisor/in IAF? All the better!
  • You bring at least three years of experience in preventive counseling and pursue your sales goals with a lot of passion.
  • You realize that you like to be among people – you can put yourself well in your opposite, recognize his needs and respond to them individually in your advice.
  • You express yourself very well in German (verbal and written). Maybe you even know other languages?
  • You have a large relationship network, maintain it sustainably and preferably live in the region.
  • For consultations outside the agency and even outside the official contact times, you are flexible.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
