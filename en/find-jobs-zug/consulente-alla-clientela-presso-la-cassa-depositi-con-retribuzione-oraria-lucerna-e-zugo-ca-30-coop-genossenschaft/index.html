Customer consultant: in deposit fund in the hourly wage, Lucerne and Zug, about 30%

Coop Cooperative — Lucerne, LU, Svizzera

  • Job opening: as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement
  • Specialization: Finance / Controlling
  • Place of work: Coop Deposit Fund, Rössligasse 18, 6004 Lucerne
  • Pensum: about 30%

Tasks

  • You are the contact person at the counter and advise our customers
  • You make the deposits and withdrawals
  • You conduct the foreign currency purchase and sale and process cash bonds
  • Various administrative activities round off this versatile position
  • You fulfill the tasks on an annual average of about 30%

Requirements

  • You bring experience in the commercial sector or in sales – or are a motivated lateral entrant
  • You have a flair for numbers and dealing with cash power You have fun
  • In addition, you are an enthusiastic personality and convince our customers with your winning way
  • Flexible assignments are no obstacle for you and it gives you pleasure to work independently and actively in different shifts
  • You work in Zug and Lucerne. Your assignments vary between 1 – up to 3 days per week – as needed. Planning is known 3 weeks in advance.

What we offer

  • Perks: 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount on various insurance services, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group, perks for leisure activities
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive Conditions of Employment and Certified Equal Pay, Total Employment Contract (GAV)
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: More than 600 internal training courses, support for external education and training, talent management with a wide range of development opportunities
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription, in-house sports offers
