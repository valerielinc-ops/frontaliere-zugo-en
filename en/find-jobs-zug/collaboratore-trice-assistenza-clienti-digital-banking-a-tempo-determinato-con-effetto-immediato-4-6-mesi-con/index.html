Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With VZ Depotbank, we offer our customers modern, digital and affordable banking services. For the upcoming peak phase, we are looking for a temporary assignment of 4 to 6 months (with the possibility of permanent employment) immediately or by agreement for reinforcement for our customer service at the headquarters in Zug.

  • You are the first point of contact for our customers with questions about logging into eBanking and mobile banking.
  • You support our customers by telephone when using their digital banking services. You answer customer concerns in a friendly, professional and solution-oriented manner.
  • You will be carefully introduced to our systems and processes and receive support from an experienced team at all times.
  • You gain valuable practical experience in the customer service of a modern and digital bank.

  • Experience in customer service or telephone support is advantageous, open to motivated lateral entrants

  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Reliable, patient and customer-oriented workflow
  • High load capacity and overview even in a dynamic environment
  • Rapid understanding for digital applications and processes
  • Communication strength and joy in dealing with people
  • A temporary assignment of 4 to 6 months with a quick start.
  • A comprehensive introduction to our eBanking solutions and support processes.
  • The opportunity to get to know the banking world up close.
