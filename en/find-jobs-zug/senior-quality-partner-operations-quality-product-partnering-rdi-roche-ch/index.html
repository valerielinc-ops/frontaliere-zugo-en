The Position For the Operations Quality Product Partnering RDI department, we are looking for a highly motivated and committed personality. In this innovative environment, together with the responsible product and production managers, you ensure that our medical devices and in vitro diagnostics (IVDs) always meet the highest internal and external quality standards.

As a Senior Quality Partner, you are responsible for quality assurance and product care (product care / improvements). You ensure that our internal requirements as well as international laws and regulations (such as ISO 13485, FDA and IVDR) are complied with and documented seamlessly during design transfer to production, production and product changes.

The possibility In this responsible role, you will take on the following main tasks:

  • You check and approve changes (change control) as part of the manufacturing process and product maintenance.
  • You will evaluate and authorize project, product and process documents as well as other relevant production specifications and proofs.
  • You process and approve deviations (nonconformities) and ensure their timely completion by actively tracking.
  • You are responsible for quality-related product release activities (such as DHR verification) and independently carry out product releases if necessary.
  • You handle the quality-relevant aspects of design transfer and technical product transfer projects into production.
  • You identify potential for optimization and continuously improve our processes in order to sustainably increase quality and efficiency.
  • You lead or support cross-divisional projects in Quality Management and actively participate in our QMS as a process consultant.

Who you are

You’ll be open to new ideas, question conventional thinking patterns, and appreciate a performance-driven, mutually respectful work environment. For this position, you bring the following:

  • Completed technical or scientific studies or comparable, many years of professional experience in a technical field.
  • At least 3–5 years of in-depth experience in the quality management of medical devices / IVDs as well as very good knowledge of regulatory requirements (ISO 13485, FDA, etc.).
  • Experience with process risk analyses (pFMEAs) as well as in-depth practice in the application of problem-solving and improvement methods.
  • Routine in the definition, collection and analysis of statistical data to optimize product quality and processes.
  • Strong and convincing communication skills in German and English to develop partnership solutions in a complex environment across all hierarchical levels.
