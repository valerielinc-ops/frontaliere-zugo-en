Original description

Abbott Diabetes Care – Switzerland

Position overview Abbott Diabetes Care is a global leader in diabetes management and is committed to helping people with diabetes live healthier and more fulfilling lives. Through continuous innovation and a consistent quality promise, Abbott offers state-of-the-art glucose monitoring systems, including the renowned FreeStyle LibreTM product family. These products provide precise and reliable glucose levels and help patients and healthcare professionals better manage diabetes. Abbott’s mission is to continuously advance diabetes care through cutting-edge technologies and sustained support for the best possible health outcomes worldwide.

Job profile The Medical Educator is a field service-based position for medical professionals (HCPs) at Abbott Diabetes Care in Switzerland. The main task is to provide medical-scientific expertise in diabetology and to provide high-quality medical training on sensor-assisted glucose monitoring technologies (FreeStyle LibreTM) in Switzerland. The central goal of the position is to help diabetes treatment teams realize the full benefits of CGM systems for their patient communities. In addition, the role includes the credible communication of scientific information and the professional dialogue with medical professionals and health institutions in the assigned area. The position requires close and compliant cooperation with commercial colleagues while maintaining complete medical independence and the highest scientific integrity.

Their tasks

  • Providing medical and scientific expertise in diabetology to support FreeStyle LibreTM products in Switzerland; cross-functional cooperation for the development, coordination and implementation of strategies and programmes at customer level in the respective area
  • Preparation and implementation of scientific and medical presentations for medical professionals – individually or in groups, in presence, online or recorded
  • Execution of field visits to HCPs and evidence-based exchange on relevant medical-scientific topics in the responsible area
  • Planning, coordination and implementation of internal training and further education programmes for employees on medical and scientific topics
  • Completion of internal medical and scientific tasks (e.g. clearances, fair market value assessments, B2B CRM activities) and administrative support
  • Establishment and maintenance of a network of professional societies, opinion leaders (KOLs), doctors and other relevant specialists in the field
  • Cooperation with ADC Medical Affairs DACH, EMEAP and Global Medical Affairs
  • Organisation of medical and scientific programmes for local congresses including presentations
  • Support in the selection and implementation of special elements and events of the FreeStyle Academy TM

Your qualifications

  • Approved:r Health professional:in (e.g. diabetes consultant:in, nutrition consultant:in or doctor:in) with extensive clinical experience in the care and treatment of people with diabetes
  • Experience in the pharmaceutical or medical technology industry
  • Established network in the field of diabetology in Switzerland
  • Proven proactive, solution-oriented (“can‐do”) working method
  • Strong organisational and project management skills
  • High digital competence and technical understanding
  • Team-oriented working methods combined with creativity and initiative
  • Ability to collaborate across functions and work effectively in matrix organizations
  • Fluent German,E
