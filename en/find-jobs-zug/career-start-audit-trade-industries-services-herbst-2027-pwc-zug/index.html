Full description

Numbers tell stories and we help make them credible and trustworthy. Together we examine the consolidated and annual accounts of national and international companies and gain unique insights into their business models, strategies and future plans. This is about more than balance sheets and financial information: Through our work, we create trust, promote transparency and make an important contribution to a strong economy. From the beginning, you are part of the team, work on exciting customer mandates and grow step by step into your role as a future:r dipl with the support of experienced colleagues. Auditors: into it.

  • Audit financial statements: audit consolidated and annual financial statements, balance sheets, income statements and internal controls, thereby creating confidence in financial reporting.
  • Analyze companies: Analyze financial data and business processes, identify audit risks and develop an understanding of the economic context behind the numbers.
  • Discover sectors: Get to know different companies, markets and business models and build a broad economic understanding early on.
  • Use data in a targeted manner: Use data analyses, digital testing methods and AI to identify abnormalities and prepare audit processes in a well-founded manner.
  • Assess information: Depending on your mandate, participate in the review of sustainability information and learn how it relates to business processes and corporate reporting.
  • Build relationships: Collaborate with experienced colleagues and contacts from finance to CFOs and gain insight into key business decisions.

Your perspective: Your path to a diploma

Your career start combines practical mandate work with a clearly structured training:

  • Learn the profession: After your onboarding, work directly in exam teams, learn about our exam methodology and audit software and gradually take on your own exam areas.
  • Grow in the Busy Season: From January to April, you work intensively on annual audits and binding customer appointments. You apply your knowledge directly, learn in a team and develop quickly.
  • Reach your diploma: We accompany you on the way to / to the dipl. Auditors: in and support your education in time and financially. Paid lessons, self-study and exams are associated with your practical work.
  • Your professional basis: You are about to graduate or have a Bachelor or Master in Economics, (Business) Informatics, STEM or a comparable field of study.
  • Your interest in the profession: You want to understand how companies map their financial situation and continuously deepen your knowledge in accounting, financial reporting and auditing.
  • Your analytical strength: You work carefully, question information and want to learn to assess complex issues in a structured way.
  • Your cooperation: You communicate clearly, actively participate in the team and enjoy working with customers and international colleagues.
  • Your development potential: You are willing to learn, open to digital ways of working and ready to take on responsibility. Very good knowledge of German and English round off your profile.

Why PwC? At PwC, you don’t just start into a first job. You will learn a recognized profession, work with customers early on and gain insights into the financial sector and different management levels. At the same time, you will be targeted on your way to / to dipl. Auditor: accompanied.
