Full description

An inspiring meeting point to experience meaningful leisure activities! Thanks to the support of the Migros Culture Percentage, the Club School offers an attractive range of language, movement and creativity courses. Join our course participants in developing their talents and achieving their learning goals.

Luogo di lavoro: Club School Migros, Club School Migros, IBAW – Institute for Vocational Education and Training Zug, Industriestrasse 15b, 6300 Zug

Mansioni
