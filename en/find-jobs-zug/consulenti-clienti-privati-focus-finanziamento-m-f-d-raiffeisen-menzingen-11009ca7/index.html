07.09.2026 Menzingen

Become part of our team!

We attach great importance to a holistic and sustainable consultation and support of our customers and are looking for a committed and experienced personality as a private customer consultant to complement our team. With us, people count – in the team and in consulting. We support each other and take responsibility for each other and for our customers. If team spirit and customer proximity are just as important to you, we look forward to your application.

What awaits you?

  • Consulting with depth: You develop individual and holistic solutions, tailored to the customer’s living situation, wishes and goals
  • With commitment and competence, you provide a high-quality consulting experience
  • Solution-oriented, individual and holistic support and advice about the entire consulting and service offer (basic solutions, provision and investment, housing and financing, entrepreneurship) with focus on financing
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous support of the personal customer portfolio
  • Cooperation in market development measures and performance of representative tasks

What do we offer you?

  • A varied task with development opportunities
  • A warm, open-minded team that enjoys working and celebrates joint success
  • Modern offices
  • Preferential conditions on our banking products – just for you!
  • 1⁄2 day free on your birthday

What do you bring?

  • Banking and financial training completed
  • In-depth knowledge and several years of experience in credit counselling (mortgage)
  • Sales and customer-oriented personality with a high willingness to provide services
  • Sympathetic and winning appearance as well as good communication skills and pleasure in customer contact
  • Teamwork is more than just a word for you: you like to work together to achieve goals

Do you want to inspire with consulting competence and commitment? Then you are exactly right with us! I look forward to your application. Brigitte Blöchlingen Chairman of the Bank Management Why Raiffeisen?
