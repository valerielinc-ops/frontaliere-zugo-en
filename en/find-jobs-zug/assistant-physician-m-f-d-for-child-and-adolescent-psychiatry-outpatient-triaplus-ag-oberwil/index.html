Full description

Their tasks shall include

  • Psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of our patients
  • Implementation of child and adolescent psychiatric investigations
  • Psychoeducation, psychotherapy and counselling of patients and their relatives in a broad spectrum of diagnosis
  • Possibility of carrying out group therapies
  • Cooperation in the multidisciplinary team in close networking with external specialists and taking over the case management
  • Preparation of treatment documentation
  • Ensuring the ambulatory emergency service in the form of regular background services (weekdays, no nights)
  • Supervision of employees
  • Public relations and participation in internal and external training and information events

They bring it with them.

  • Completed specialist training desired, application for advanced specialist training possible
  • High affinity and interest in social psychiatric and psychotherapeutic issues
  • High social competence and empathetic treatment of patients
  • Evidence-based work, networked thinking
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • Self-responsible and inclusive working and leadership style
  • Leadership experience desired, but not a requirement

We offer them

  • We are small enough to be “family”, for short distances and quick decisions – and so big that you can find all the settings here and you can specialize / develop professionally
  • A lot of quality of life with at least 5 weeks holidays, from 55. Age 6 weeks
  • We contribute to your public transport costs
  • In the Triaplus you have the possibility of internal rotation
